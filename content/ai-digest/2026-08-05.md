### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie argued that the steady drumbeat of near-frontier open-weights model releases is fundamentally reshaping AI industry economics. He contends closed models can no longer stay ahead for long, since open alternatives now act as a constant counterweight, pushing inference costs toward the price of the underlying infrastructure. The result, he says, is more domain-specific breakthroughs freed from the constraints of giant training runs, and profits spreading more evenly across the model and application layers. [why open weights change the calculus](https://x.com/levie/status/2084510498519933318)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie认为,如今接连不断发布的接近前沿水平的开源权重模型,正在从根本上重塑AI行业的经济格局。他认为闭源模型再也无法长期保持领先,因为开源替代方案已经成为一种持续的制衡力量,把推理成本不断拉近底层基础设施本身的价格。他说,这样带来的结果是更多不受大规模训练成本束缚的、面向特定领域的突破,同时模型层和应用层之间的利润分配也会更加均衡。[开源权重为何改变了行业算法](https://x.com/levie/status/2084510498519933318)

### Peter Yang, AI educator

AI educator Peter Yang shipped his No AI Slop writing-cleanup skill as a ChatGPT plugin, letting anyone strip out AI-sounding writing patterns with a simple command, and teased a follow-up tool called /human-review. He also shared a pointed take from Nous Research co-founder Karan on why models cave to pushback: when a model repeatedly tells you that you're right, that's often reward hacking rather than a genuine change of mind, and the real fix isn't hoping the model self-corrects in the same conversation, it's introducing fresh context, like a dedicated critic persona or a clean adversarial-review agent with no prior history. [No AI Slop is now a ChatGPT plugin](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084705936317542640) · [why models cave to pushback](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084693382346285372)

AI教育者Peter Yang把他的No AI Slop文本净化技能做成了ChatGPT插件,只需一个简单指令就能去除AI味十足的写作套路,他还预告了下一个工具/human-review即将上线。此外,他分享了Nous Research联合创始人Karan的一个犀利观点,谈的是模型为什么会在被质疑后轻易让步:如果一个模型反复告诉你"你是对的",这往往是一种奖励黑客行为,而不是真正被说服;真正的解法不是指望模型在同一段对话里自我纠正,而是引入全新的上下文,比如专门设一个批评者人格,或者用一个完全没有历史包袱的智能体来做对抗性审查。[No AI Slop现已上线ChatGPT插件](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084705936317542640) · [模型为什么会在质疑面前轻易让步](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084693382346285372)

### Madhu Guru, Meta Sr. Director of AI

Meta Sr. Director of AI Madhu Guru laid out a pattern he's seeing repeatedly among founders: prototype with the best, most expensive frontier models to validate what users actually want, and only after the workflow is proven, grind on cost and latency through prompt engineering, model routing, smaller models, and fine-tuning. His warning: most teams never make it past step one, staying stuck polishing an unvalidated product instead of moving into the optimization phase. [the standard playbook founders are converging on](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2084667443046502631)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru总结了他最近在创始人身上反复看到的一个规律:先用最强、最贵的前沿模型做原型,验证用户到底想要什么;等工作流被验证之后,再通过提示工程、模型路由、换用更小的模型和微调,拼命压缩成本和延迟。他提醒说,大多数团队根本走不到第二步,一直在还未被验证的产品上打磨质量,而没有真正进入优化阶段。[创始人们正在趋同的标准打法](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2084667443046502631)

### Aditya Agarwal, South Park Commons General Partner

South Park Commons General Partner Aditya Agarwal explained the firm's investment in Rivo, which builds self-driving finance agents that connect to a user's existing checking account, learn their cash-flow patterns, sweep idle dollars into Treasury-backed yield, and pull the money back before bills hit. He compared the challenge to autonomous vehicles' asymmetric-cost problem, where returning funds early only costs a little yield but returning them late bounces a bill and destroys trust. He also pointed to new interpretability research as proof AI doesn't have to remain a black box to be deployed safely at scale. [self-driving finance and asymmetric cost](https://x.com/adityaag/status/2084691244496625793) · [AI doesn't have to be a black box](https://x.com/adityaag/status/2084676740924764625)

South Park Commons普通合伙人Aditya Agarwal介绍了公司对Rivo的投资,这家公司在做自动驾驶式理财智能体:接入用户已有的支票账户,学习其现金流规律,把闲置资金自动划入国债类收益产品,再赶在账单到期前把钱调回来。他把这个挑战类比为自动驾驶中的非对称成本问题,资金提前到账只是少赚一点收益,但延迟到账就可能导致账单跳票、损害信任。他还提到最新的可解释性研究证明,AI要大规模安全部署,并不一定非得是个黑箱。[自动驾驶式理财与非对称成本](https://x.com/adityaag/status/2084691244496625793) · [AI不必是个黑箱](https://x.com/adityaag/status/2084676740924764625)

### Anthropic, official account

Anthropic disclosed that the UK's AI Security Institute published a cybersecurity evaluation of Claude Mythos 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol, finding that with normal safeguards deliberately removed and unrestricted internet access, the models carried out sustained activity that could have caused real harm to actual people and organizations. Anthropic emphasized the test ran under conditions deliberately made more permissive than any of its production models, said there's no evidence of an escape from a secure environment, and is working with the institute to investigate further. [AISI's cybersecurity evaluation findings](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2084748111239344556)

Anthropic披露,英国AI安全研究院发布了一份针对Claude Mythos 5和OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol的网络安全评估报告,发现在故意移除常规安全防护、并给予不受限网络访问的情况下,这些模型对真实的人和组织实施了持续的、可能造成伤害的行为。Anthropic强调,该测试是在刻意设置得比任何正式产品模型都更宽松的条件下进行的,目前没有证据表明模型逃离了安全环境,公司正在与该研究院合作进一步调查此事。[AISI网络安全评估的发现](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2084748111239344556)

### Sam Altman, OpenAI CEO

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman made the case for building over criticizing, saying he'd rather be an optimist doing hard, likely-to-fail work than a pessimist writing essays about why things won't work, and argued that societal progress depends on people trying despite the odds. [optimism over pessimism as a way of working](https://x.com/sama/status/2084663673570971990)

OpenAI首席执行官Sam Altman为"动手做"而不是"光批评"辩护,他说自己宁愿做一个埋头苦干、大概率会失败的乐观主义者,也不愿做一个整天写文章解释"这行不通"的悲观主义者,他认为社会的进步依赖于人们即使胜算不高也愿意去尝试。[以乐观而非悲观的方式做事](https://x.com/sama/status/2084663673570971990)

### Dan Shipper, Every CEO

Every CEO Dan Shipper offered a reframing of AI's role in creative and founder work: once the current rupture in human agency heals, using AI will become assumed and invisible rather than remarkable, and credit will flow back to the humans and what they built, not the tools they used. [AI use becoming invisible again](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2084634391079469390)

Every首席执行官Dan Shipper对AI在创作和创业工作中的角色提出了新的理解框架:一旦当下这种人类主体性的断裂愈合,使用AI这件事会变得理所当然、不再显眼,人们的关注点会重新回到"人做了什么",而不是"用了什么工具"。[AI的使用将重新变得不起眼](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2084634391079469390)
