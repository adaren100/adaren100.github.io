### Madhu Guru, Meta AI Director

Meta senior AI director Madhu Guru continued his evals series with part 7, the Goldilocks principle for eval construction: your evals should measure at the level of the various jobs to be done, not just the final answer. His example is a financial analysis agent whose ultimate output is a stock recommendation, and the most common mistake he sees is teams building a golden set of right answers and checking whether the agent recommended the right stock. The problem is that a bunch of meaningful jobs happen before that recommendation: understanding the client's portfolio, risk tolerance, investment horizon, goals and constraints; gathering evidence on the stocks, the sectors, the macro environment, Fed policy and news events; analyzing revenue growth, valuation guidance and projections down to a narrower set of candidates; and only then making the call on ticker, bid and sell price, and timeframe. Each stage produces an intermediate output and each can have its own eval, so when the final recommendation is wrong, a well designed eval set tells you client understanding 92%, evidence extraction 92%, data analysis 70%, recommendation 75%, and now you know where to go dig. Not too granular, not too coarse: make your eval set as granular as you need to diagnose and act. [the Goldilocks principle for evals](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2091684812012875981)

Meta的AI高级总监Madhu Guru更新了他的evals系列第七篇,讲eval构建的"金发姑娘原则":你的evals应该按照一个个待完成的任务来衡量,而不是只盯着最终答案。他举的例子是一个金融分析agent,它的最终产出是一条选股建议,而他见过最普遍的错误,就是团队做一套标准答案,然后检查agent有没有推荐"对"的那只股票。问题在于,在这条建议之前,其实还发生了一堆有意义的工作:理解客户的持仓、风险承受度、投资期限、目标和约束;收集证据,包括各只股票、所在板块、宏观环境、美联储政策以及近期和即将发生的新闻;分析数据,看营收增长、估值指引和增长预测,收敛出一个更小的候选池;最后才是给出建议,包括标的代码、买卖价位和时间窗口。每个阶段都会产出一个中间结果,每个阶段都可以有自己的eval,所以当最终建议出错时,一套设计良好的eval集会告诉你:客户理解92%,证据提取92%,数据分析70%,建议生成75%,你立刻就知道该往哪儿挖。不要太细,也不要太粗:你的eval集要细到刚好够你诊断问题并采取行动。[eval构建的金发姑娘原则](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2091684812012875981)

### Guillermo Rauch, Vercel CEO

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch went down a rabbit hole that turned into the day's best piece of systems archaeology. When your terminal gets into a bad state with garbled output you run `reset`, and he noticed it was oddly slow. The reason turned out to be that in 1979, 3BSD's `tset` carried a `sleep(1)` to let mechanical printer-and-ink terminals settle down, and that full second is still sitting there decades later. He asked fx to write him a faster alternative in Zig, and it now takes 1ms instead of 1s. What he took away from digging through ncurses, tset, terminfo and the myriad ways a terminal can get cursed is how far back our technology stack goes, and how enduring good system design is even with the occasional spider webs you find along the way. His postscript: once you start using fx, you notice slowness like this everywhere. [why reset is slow, and a 1ms replacement](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2091957823945216474)

Vercel首席执行官Guillermo Rauch钻进了一个兔子洞,结果挖出了今天最好看的一段系统考古。终端进入"坏状态"、输出乱码的时候,你会敲`reset`,而他注意到这个命令慢得有点奇怪。原因是:1979年,3BSD的`tset`里塞了一个`sleep(1)`,为的是让那种机械打印式的墨水终端"缓一缓",几十年过去了,这整整一秒还原封不动地待在那儿。他让fx用Zig给他写了个更快的替代品,现在耗时1毫秒,而不是1秒。翻遍ncurses、tset、terminfo,以及终端能被"诅咒"的各种花式姿势之后,他的感受是:我们的技术栈能一直追溯到多久以前,以及好的系统设计有多么经久耐用,哪怕路上时不时能扫到几张蜘蛛网。他还补了一句:一旦你开始用fx,这种慢你会到处都看得见。[reset为什么慢,以及一个1毫秒的替代品](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2091957823945216474)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie made the case that zero data retention is underappreciated as a driver of AI growth, because it dramatically simplified the compliance process most companies deal with when handing data to subprocessors. Without it, he argues, the alternative path would have been a far slower adoption rate. His reasoning runs through both sides of the market. On the vendor side, the applied AI tools primarily responsible for driving AI growth generally have agreements with customers to only offer ZDR models, so customers do not have to inspect each model they turn on, and anything else becomes an exception process that takes far longer. Inside enterprises, most companies have internal governance requirements limiting them to ZDR models, because they have no way to explicitly separate PII and other sensitive or confidential data from what goes into the context window. His conclusion: all of this could change if the entire industry moved that direction, but with one-off model holdouts it would take years for these policies to shift, and without ZDR, AI diffusion grinds to a halt. [ZDR is load bearing for AI adoption](https://x.com/levie/status/2091909170308296950)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie认为,ZDR(零数据留存)对AI增长的贡献被严重低估了,因为它极大简化了绝大多数公司在把数据交给子处理方时要走的合规流程。他的判断是,如果没有ZDR,采用速度会慢得多。他的论证覆盖了市场的两端。供给侧这边,真正拉动AI增长的那批应用层AI工具,通常会和客户约定只提供支持ZDR的模型,这样客户就不必逐个审查自己启用的每个模型,否则就得走例外流程,时间成本高出一大截。而在企业内部,大多数公司的内控治理要求就是只能用ZDR模型,因为它们根本没办法把PII和其他敏感、机密数据从进入context window的内容里干净地摘出来。他的结论是:如果整个行业集体转向,这一切当然可能改变,但只要还有个别模型不跟进,这些政策就得好几年才动得了,而没有ZDR,AI的扩散会直接卡死。[ZDR是AI落地的承重墙](https://x.com/levie/status/2091909170308296950)

### Garry Tan, Y Combinator CEO

Y Combinator president and CEO Garry Tan posted the day's sharpest one-liner on the state of the labs: the current meta is that OpenAI is actually the much more open lab. He also pushed back on the idea that AI dissolves the underlying structure of software, noting that there will still be APIs, ACLs, SQL and deterministic underlying data structures. What changes, in his telling, is the obligation on vendors: software companies have to build the AI harness and the full solution for their customers, or be subsumed by it. Separately, he backed housing supply with a three word position, legalize building housing. [OpenAI is the more open lab](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2091873781325418723) · [build the harness or be subsumed](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2091872254959489275) · [legalize building housing](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2091899569412202914)

Y Combinator总裁兼首席执行官Garry Tan发了今天关于各家实验室现状最犀利的一句话:当下的格局是,OpenAI反而是那个开放得多的实验室。他还反驳了"AI会把软件的底层结构溶解掉"这种说法,指出API、ACL、SQL以及底下那些确定性的数据结构都还会在。在他看来,真正变的是厂商身上的义务:软件公司必须为客户搭出AI harness和完整方案,否则就会被这套东西吞掉。另外,他用三个词表明了在住房供给上的立场:让盖房子合法。[OpenAI才是更开放的那个实验室](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2091873781325418723) · [要么搭出harness,要么被吞掉](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2091872254959489275) · [让盖房子合法](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2091899569412202914)

### Nan Yu, Product Enthusiast

Product thinker Nan Yu pushed back on a point of view that keeps making the rounds and always gets a lot of likes, that SaaS apps should simply expose themselves to outside agents. His counter is that existing SaaS businesses need both: a built-in agent that can operate the app, and good MCP and hooks for Codex, Grokbot and the rest. The reason he gives is that users are hyper polarized right now, so you need both. He also took a swing at shipping-for-promo culture, quoting a mock performance review line about shipping emoji reactions to Gmail that show up as ugly random one-character emails with metadata to anyone using another mail app, including the iPhone mail app, the most common mail app on Earth. [SaaS needs both the agent and the hooks](https://x.com/thenanyu/status/2091926179704135684) · [shipping for the promo packet](https://x.com/thenanyu/status/2091993558018572434)

产品人Nan Yu反驳了一个最近反复刷屏、每次都能收获一堆点赞的观点:SaaS应用只要把自己敞开给外部agent就行了。他的看法是,现有的SaaS生意两样都得要:既要有一个能操作自家应用的内置agent,也要给Codex、Grokbot这些外部agent提供好用的MCP和hooks。他给的理由是,用户现在极度两极分化,所以你必须两头都占。他还顺手讽刺了一把"为了晋升而发版"的风气,引用了一段调侃绩效评语的话:把emoji回应功能塞进Gmail,结果在任何用其他邮件客户端的人那里,都显示成一封丑陋的、只有一个字符外加一堆元数据的邮件,而这里面就包括iPhone自带邮件,地球上最常用的邮件应用。[SaaS两样都得要](https://x.com/thenanyu/status/2091926179704135684) · [为了晋升材料而发版](https://x.com/thenanyu/status/2091993558018572434)

### Nikunj Kothari, FPV Ventures Partner

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari offered a small, concrete measure of how much friction has quietly disappeared. His mother-in-law was visiting from India and wanted a spontaneous date night with her granddaughter, but had not signed up for roaming and they needed a way to communicate. He got her a data eSIM in under three minutes and she was connected. His note for anyone who remembers the old version: this would historically have been a trip to the store, or a call to the carrier to add an extra line he would then need to cancel a month later. [what a wonderful world we live in](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2091696412744700268)

FPV Ventures的合伙人Nikunj Kothari给出了一个很小但很具体的样本,说明有多少摩擦已经悄悄消失了。他岳母从印度过来,想临时起意跟外孙女出去过个"约会之夜",但没开漫游,两边没法联系。他花了不到三分钟给她弄了张数据eSIM,人就联网了。他给还记得"从前版本"的人补了一句:放在过去,这事要么得跑一趟营业厅,要么得打电话给运营商加一条副卡,然后一个月后再打电话去销号。[我们活在一个多好的世界里](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2091696412744700268)
