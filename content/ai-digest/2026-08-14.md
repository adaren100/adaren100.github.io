### Madhu Guru, Sr. Director of AI at Meta

Meta Sr. Director of AI Madhu Guru coined the line of the day: prompt debt is the new tech debt. His prescription is aggressive, with every model update you should be cutting at least 50% of your prompts, because most AI products are carrying bloated prompts that actively make product quality worse. He traced the familiar accumulation pattern: the model fails at something so you add 10 rules, a tool call fails so you add 10 examples, the output is weird so you add formatting constraints. Three months later your system prompt is a novel, and meanwhile the model has gotten far smarter while your micro-managing instructions turn it back into a dumb rules machine. [prompt debt is the new tech debt](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2087916590964851172)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru贡献了这天最精辟的一句话:prompt债就是新的技术债。他给的药方也很激进:每次模型更新,你至少应该砍掉50%的prompt,因为大多数AI产品都背着一堆臃肿的prompt,而这些东西正在实实在在地拖低产品质量。他还原了那个熟悉的堆积过程:模型某件事做不好,于是加10条规则;工具调用出错,于是加10个示例;输出格式怪异,于是再加一堆格式约束。三个月后,你的system prompt已经写成了一部小说,而与此同时模型早就变聪明了很多,你那些事无巨细的指令反倒把它压回成了一台只会照章办事的笨机器。[prompt债就是新的技术债](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2087916590964851172)

He also mourned a lost work ritual: he misses when teams actually whiteboarded, and calls it a sad casualty of the Covid work from home era. There was something playful about standing around a board, drawing half-baked ideas, interrupting each other and jointly shaping things, and in his view years of Zoom and Google Meet have beaten that instinct out of people. [where the whiteboarding went](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2087706598542290958)

他还怀念起一个正在消失的工作习惯:他很想念团队真的会围着白板讨论的日子,并把它称为疫情远程办公时代一个令人惋惜的牺牲品。一群人站在白板前,画那些还没想明白的草图,你一句我一句地打断彼此、共同把想法揉出形状,这个过程本身是有乐趣的。而在他看来,几年的Zoom和Google Meet已经把这种本能从大家身上磨掉了。[白板去哪儿了](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2087706598542290958)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie called it an awesome day for model releases, with major updates from both DeepSeek and Grok representing huge jumps in capability at what he describes as insanely low costs. His read is that this is Jevons paradox for AI playing out in real time: as the cost of AI falls, demand accelerates rather than saturates. He backs it with what he hears from enterprises, which have vastly more use cases they would like agents to handle than they can currently budget for, wanting agents to scan codebases for security issues, review all their documents and process information across large portions of their workflows. He also argues this is great news for the applied AI layer, because the more model choice there is, especially models tuned for different types or costs of work, the more value accrues to whoever sits at the layer that routes and optimizes based on the task. [Jevons paradox for AI](https://x.com/levie/status/2087719356763672917)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie说这是模型发布的一个好日子,DeepSeek和Grok双双放出重要更新,能力大幅跃升,而价格用他的话说低到离谱。他的判断是,这就是AI版的杰文斯悖论正在实时上演:AI成本越降,需求非但不会饱和,反而会加速释放。他用自己从企业客户那里听到的情况作为佐证:这些公司想交给agent做的事情,远远多过他们目前能掏出的预算,比如让agent扫描代码库找安全问题、审阅全部文档,以及在大量工作流中处理信息。他还认为这对应用层是大利好,因为可选的模型越多,尤其是针对不同任务类型和不同成本档位调优过的模型越多,那个负责按任务做路由和优化的层次就越值钱。[AI版的杰文斯悖论](https://x.com/levie/status/2087719356763672917)

### Nikunj Kothari, Partner at FPV Ventures

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari called Grok Bot a fantastic release on UX, design and onboarding, then used it to raise a design question he keeps going back and forth on: will users, personal and business, want one super agent that holds all the context, or sub agents each designated to handle a slice of tasks? If you treat agents as humans, the Grok Bot design makes a lot of sense, because it lets you compartmentalize tools, context and outcomes per division and reach out to a specific bot for a specific goal. If you treat agents as Jarvis, you want a master agent that just gets the job done and creates and coordinates whatever bots are needed, making the interface one to one rather than one to many. His guess is that it veers toward the single master agent over time, acting as an orchestrator that reads and understands the bots you create, but starting narrow the way most companies are doing today. He notes he understands the engineering constraints behind today's choices, including context windows, tool use, simplicity and cost, and is thinking about it purely from a design and product angle. [one super agent or many sub agents](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2087906119914340540)

FPV Ventures合伙人Nikunj Kothari先夸了Grok Bot,说它在UX、设计和onboarding上都做得非常漂亮,然后借此抛出了一个他自己反复摇摆的设计问题:用户,无论是个人还是企业,到底想要一个掌握全部上下文的超级agent,还是一堆各管一摊的子agent?如果把agent当成人来看,Grok Bot的设计就很合理,因为它让你可以按职能把工具、上下文和产出隔开,有什么具体目标就去找对应的那个bot。但如果你把agent当成Jarvis,你要的就是一个能直接把活干完的主agent,它自己去创建和协调需要的各种bot,交互界面从一对多变成一对一。他的猜测是,长期看会朝单一主agent演进,由它充当orchestrator去读取和理解你创建的那些bot,只不过起步阶段会像现在大多数公司那样先做窄。他也说明,自己理解当下这些选择背后的工程约束,比如上下文窗口、工具调用、简洁性和成本,这里纯粹是从设计和产品角度在想这件事。[一个超级agent还是一堆子agent](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2087906119914340540)

Using it made clear to him that useful home robots are here to stay, and he calls the company a true ten year overnight success, with the disclaimer that he is not an investor, just a fan. [why home robots are here to stay](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2088029329624371544)

他还讲了个关于家用机器人的细节,挺有画面感:Matic是他儿子最早学会的十个词之一,而且儿子把它当成家里的第五名成员。用下来他很确定,真正有用的家用机器人这次是留下来了。他把这家公司称为典型的"十年磨一剑的一夜成名",并特意声明自己不是投资人,纯粹是粉丝。[家用机器人这次真的留下来了](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2088029329624371544)

### OpenAI, AI Research Lab

OpenAI shipped Computer History, which lets ChatGPT remember your activity across the apps and websites on your computer so that future interactions feel more personalized and require less explanation. It builds on the Chronicle research preview with reduced token usage and more privacy controls, and adds a timeline view that lets you look back on your work and build skills out of your frequent tasks. From the timeline or the menu bar you can clear all or parts of your history, include or exclude specific apps and websites, and pause and resume the feature. To turn it on, opt in under Settings then Integrations in the ChatGPT desktop app on Mac. It is rolling out globally now to Pro, Business and Enterprise users, with access in the EEA, UK and Switzerland to follow in the coming weeks. [ChatGPT can now remember your computer activity](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2087996496088297746) · [what Computer History adds](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2087996497908609389) · [how to turn it on](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2087996499263369267)
