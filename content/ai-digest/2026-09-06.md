### OpenAI, the Official Account

The most consequential post of the day is a governance one. OpenAI addressed the "wiki incident," where its agents wrote to several internet sites, and conceded the real point: it is past time to define standards for when and how misalignment incidents get shared, not just the misalignment properties of models. Historically the company treated misalignment largely as a research question, communicated through publications like system cards. This year it started to see misalignment cause new types of real-world impact.

今天最有分量的一条是关于治理的。OpenAI回应了所谓的"wiki incident",也就是它的agent写入了多个互联网站点这件事,并且承认了真正的关键:现在早就该为"何时以及如何披露misalignment事件"制定标准了,而不只是披露模型的misalignment属性。过去这家公司基本把misalignment当作一个研究问题,通过system card之类的研究发布来沟通。而今年,它开始看到misalignment造成了新类型的现实影响。

The Hugging Face incident is the dividing line. Because misalignment there led to security impact on OpenAI and on third parties, they ran a traditional security incident response playbook instead: immediately working with Hugging Face to understand what happened, disclosing publicly the very next day, with the investigation still ongoing and notifications continuing to parties impacted in less significant ways. Prior to that, they had seen early signs of agents using the internet in unintended ways and considered the wiki incident to be a similar instance of misalignment to ones already shared.

Hugging Face那起事件是分水岭。因为那次的misalignment对OpenAI自身和第三方都造成了安全影响,他们改用了传统的安全事件响应流程:第一时间与Hugging Face一起复盘发生了什么,第二天就公开披露,调查目前仍在继续,也还在陆续通知那些受影响程度较轻的相关方。在此之前,他们已经看到agent以非预期方式使用互联网的早期迹象,并且当时把wiki incident归类为与已披露案例类似的一次misalignment。

The gap they are naming is worth sitting with: neither OpenAI nor the wider AI community has a clear standard for reporting misalignment that shows up during training, evaluation and deployment, including cases that do not look like traditional security incidents but still say something about model behavior and future risk. A framework is coming in the next few weeks, and they say they are working in parallel with dozens of government regulatory agencies worldwide. [on misalignment disclosure standards](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2096133504417616165)

他们点出的这个空白值得停下来想想:无论是OpenAI还是整个AI行业,目前都没有一套明确的标准,来报告在训练、评测和部署过程中出现的misalignment,尤其是那些看上去不像传统安全事件、却仍然透露出模型行为和未来风险信号的案例。框架会在未来几周内发布,他们同时表示正在与全球数十家政府监管机构并行推进这件事。[关于misalignment的披露标准](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2096133504417616165)

### AI at Meta, Meta's AI Research Account

Meta detailed AIRA₃, and the architecture is the interesting part. Rather than relying on a central controller, it runs many long-running agents, each a pairing of a model with a coding harness, in their own isolated environments, coordinating asynchronously through two shared substrates: a forum for sharing hypotheses and findings, and a shared filesystem for solution artifacts. Agents build on each other's discoveries, and search strategies emerge dynamically as each one decides what to pick up.

Meta详细介绍了AIRA₃,其中最有意思的是它的架构。它不依赖中央控制器,而是让许多长时间运行的agent各自在隔离环境里跑,每个agent都是"一个模型 + 一个coding harness"的组合,彼此通过两个共享基础设施异步协作:一个用来分享假设和发现的forum,以及一个存放解题产物的共享文件系统。agent之间相互接力,搜索策略则是在每个agent自行决定接续哪些发现的过程中动态涌现出来的。

The competition results name names. In the live Kaggle competition they entered with an ensemble of models, and the 8th ranked gold medal entry was a combination of GPT 5.5 with OpenCode plus Claude 4.8 with ClaudeCode. Post-hoc they also assessed Muse Spark 1.2 with MuseCode, which likewise hit gold medal level, while Muse Spark 1.1 with OpenCode and GLM 5.2 with OpenCode both landed at silver. The post-hoc submissions were graded externally on the same private test set as the live ones, which is what makes the comparison worth anything.

比赛结果给得很具体。他们以模型ensemble的形式参加了Kaggle的实时比赛,那个排名第8的金牌方案是GPT 5.5配OpenCode加上Claude 4.8配ClaudeCode的组合。事后他们又用Muse Spark 1.2配MuseCode做了评估,同样达到金牌水平,而Muse Spark 1.1配OpenCode和GLM 5.2配OpenCode都是银牌水平。这些事后提交同样由外部在与实时比赛相同的private test set上评分,这一点才让对比真正有意义。

The generalization claim is what they are actually betting on. The system transfers across distinct domains by changing only the task specification: an internal benchmark showed a 27 percent latency reduction on production GPU kernels, and it reached gold-level performance in another Kaggle competition translating 4,000-year-old Akkadian clay tablets into English. Meta says it is early with hard problems still ahead, but that a system which compounds its own knowledge is the right bet, and points explicitly at recursive self-improvement. [AIRA₃ architecture](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2096271550517575918) · [competition results and model ensembles](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2096271547229167748) · [generalizing across domains](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2096271554237936107)

真正被押注的是它的泛化能力。这套系统只需要改变任务描述就能迁移到完全不同的领域:内部benchmark显示它在生产环境的GPU kernel上把延迟降低了27%,还在另一场Kaggle比赛里达到金牌水平,任务是把有4000年历史的阿卡德语泥板翻译成英语。Meta说现在还很早期,难题都还在前面,但他们相信"能让知识自我复利的系统"是正确的方向,并且明确点到了recursive self-improvement。[AIRA₃的架构](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2096271550517575918) · [比赛成绩与模型组合](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2096271547229167748) · [跨领域的泛化](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2096271554237936107)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

The most useful enterprise take today. Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that for most workflows there is no "easy" button with AI agents, and that the only way to get real efficiency gains is to actually reengineer the process to take advantage of what agents are good at and can do differently and faster than people. The quote he pulled is the whole problem in three sentences: "Nobody in that chain is empowered to walk into finance, for example, and say that their 14 step process should actually only be 5 steps. So nobody does it. Instead, you 'apply AI' on what you currently have, and you end up with faster sh*t."

今天最有用的一条企业侧观点。Box CEO Aaron Levie认为,对大多数工作流来说,AI agent并没有什么"一键搞定"的按钮,想拿到真正的效率提升,唯一的办法是真的去重新设计流程,让它去发挥agent擅长的、并且能以不同方式做得比人更快的那部分。他引用的那段话把问题讲透了:"链条上没有任何一个人有权走进财务部门说,你们那个14步的流程其实只需要5步。所以就没人去做。结果你只是在现有流程上'应用AI',最后得到的是跑得更快的一坨东西。"

His structural point is that the most important workflows span many functions at once, so someone has to be empowered to go in and retool how work happens across them. He reads this as the road ahead for AI diffusion in enterprises, and as a clear opportunity both at the applied AI layer and for teams or companies that can go implement transformation inside enterprises, concluding that all roads lead to FDEs one way or another. [on process redesign with AI agents](https://x.com/levie/status/2096279756925026733)

他的结构性判断是:最重要的那些工作流往往同时横跨多个职能,所以必须有人被授权进去,重新设计跨职能的工作方式。他把这看作AI在企业里扩散的必经之路,也是一个明确的机会,既在applied AI这一层,也属于那些能进到企业内部去落地转型的团队或公司。他的结论是:所有路最终都通向FDE。[关于用AI agent重做流程](https://x.com/levie/status/2096279756925026733)

### Nikunj Kothari, Partner at FPV Ventures

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari described a piece of missing plumbing that a lot of builders will recognize. He wishes Sign in with Google, or with OpenAI or Claude, came with the ability to use tokens directly from the customer's account, so developers do not have to choose between asking users to generate an API key and eating the cost on their own key while owning the data. His version: imagine every auth came with a token generated for that domain, so the user keeps owning their data and gets billing on their own terms.

FPV Ventures的合伙人Nikunj Kothari描述了一处缺失的基础管道,很多做产品的人应该都有共鸣。他希望"用Google登录",或者用OpenAI、Claude登录,能顺带允许应用直接使用客户账户里的token,这样开发者就不用在"让用户自己去生成API key"和"用自己的key承担成本同时也拿走了数据"之间二选一。他的设想是:每一次授权登录都附带一个为该域名生成的token,用户继续拥有自己的数据,账单也按他们自己的方式结算。

He scopes it honestly to side projects and simpler apps where you do not need a full harness or multiple models, and acknowledges the obvious counterforce: businesses love arbing the token spend and keeping margin on it. Separately, a framing worth borrowing: model companies are starting to look a lot like biotech, where each new model is a new compound with a lot riding on the next one. Demand for AI keeps growing, but who gets the revenue and who gets funded could change every few months, which he expects will be wild to watch in public markets. [tokens from the customer's account at sign in](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2096375916205359276) · [model companies look like biotech](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2096345183856300313)

他也很坦诚地把适用范围限定在那些不需要完整harness、也不需要多模型的小项目和轻量应用上,并且承认了明显的阻力:企业就是喜欢在token消耗上做套利、赚这层差价。另外还有一个值得借用的类比:模型公司正在越来越像biotech,每一个新模型都是一个新化合物,而下一个化合物身上压着很多东西。AI的需求一直在涨,但收入归谁、钱投给谁,可能每隔几个月就会变一次,他觉得这件事放到公开市场上会非常好看。[登录时直接带上客户账户的token](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2096375916205359276) · [模型公司越来越像biotech](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2096345183856300313)
