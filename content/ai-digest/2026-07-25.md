### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie shared detailed results from testing Claude Opus 5 with the Box AI Agent on Box's Complex Work Eval, an agentic benchmark for real enterprise document work. He reported meaningful gains over Opus 4.8 across industries: +17% on due-diligence review, +30% on life-sciences target identification, +12% on legal clause-by-clause review, and +19% and +13% in technology and healthcare respectively, attributing the improvement to more complete, precise, multi-step analysis over messy source material. He also signed an industry letter backing open-weight AI models, arguing they accelerate diffusion of AI across the economy, lower costs for certain workloads, and let more players experiment with training and safety approaches that the whole industry can learn from. [Box's Opus 5 benchmark results](https://x.com/levie/status/2080704871934931221) · [signing the open-weights letter](https://x.com/levie/status/2080675210991443982)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie分享了用Box AI Agent在Box's Complex Work Eval(一项面向真实企业文档工作的agentic基准测试)上测试Claude Opus 5的详细结果。相比Opus 4.8,各行业都有明显提升:尽职调查审查+17%,生命科学领域的靶点识别+30%,逐条法律条款审查+12%,科技和医疗行业分别提升19%和13%,他将这些进步归因于模型在处理混乱原始材料时,能做出更完整、更精准的多步分析。他还签署了一封支持开放权重AI模型的行业联署信,认为这类模型能加速AI在经济各领域的普及,降低某些工作负载的成本,并让更多团队去尝试不同的训练和安全方案,从而让整个行业受益。[Box的Opus 5基准测试结果](https://x.com/levie/status/2080704871934931221) · [签署开放权重联署信](https://x.com/levie/status/2080675210991443982)

### Dan Shipper, Every CEO

Every CEO Dan Shipper published a detailed Day 0 vibe check of Claude Opus 5 after a week of testing it across coding, writing, and Every's internal agent. His verdict: Opus 5 broke backward compatibility with their existing skills and plugins, often stopping early or missing instructions, until the team deleted their old workflows and started from scratch, at which point it performed dramatically better; he also found medium or low thinking effort worked better than high. He described Opus 5 as having Fable's personality without Fable's top-end capability, an odd middle ground he'll mostly reach for once he runs out of Fable tokens. [the Opus 5 vibe check](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2080700057892815114)

Every首席执行官Dan Shipper在花一周时间从编程、写作和Every内部agent等多个角度测试Claude Opus 5之后,发布了一份详细的"Day 0初体验"报告。他的结论是:Opus 5和他们原有的skills、插件不太兼容,经常提前停手或漏掉指令,直到团队删掉旧的workflow、从零开始重建,表现才有了明显提升;他还发现中等或较低的思考强度,效果反而好于高强度思考。他形容Opus 5拥有Fable的性格,却没有Fable的顶级能力,处于一种略显尴尬的中间地带,自己大概会在用完Fable的token额度后才会更多用它。[Opus 5初体验报告](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2080700057892815114)

### Garry Tan, Y Combinator President and CEO

Y Combinator President and CEO Garry Tan shared a striking historical stat: how fast a country adopted new technology over the last 200 years accounts for at least 25% of why some nations are rich and others aren't today, pointing to the Ottomans' delayed adoption of the printing press as a costly example. He also predicted that unlocking macro productivity gains from AI will require managers and CEOs to greenlight radically different staffing and workflow plans, something he doesn't think has really happened yet, and expects it could take a decade rather than two years. [on tech adoption and national wealth](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2080849953413541982) · [on productivity gains taking a decade](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2080699367883980924)

Y Combinator总裁兼首席执行官Garry Tan分享了一个很有冲击力的历史数据:过去200年里,一个国家采纳新技术的速度,至少能解释25%的国家贫富差距,他举了奥斯曼帝国迟迟不采用印刷术为例,说明这种滞后代价高昂。他还预测,要真正释放AI带来的宏观生产力提升,需要管理者和CEO们批准彻底不同的人员配置和工作流程方案,而他认为这一点目前还没有真正发生,预计这个过程可能需要十年,而不是两年。[关于技术采纳与国家财富](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2080849953413541982) · [关于生产力提升需要十年](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2080699367883980924)

### Claude, Anthropic's AI assistant

The official Claude account announced that Opus 5 is available today on all paid plans and the Claude API at the same price as Opus 4.8, is now the default model on Claude Max and the strongest option on Claude Pro, and also ships in a Fast mode running about 2.5x the default speed.
