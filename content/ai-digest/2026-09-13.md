### Sam Altman, OpenAI CEO

The biggest post of the day by far: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman publicly agreed with Dario that the industry needs to pace the frontier, saying it has been a primary topic of discussion inside OpenAI in recent weeks. He called the commitment to independent evaluators with employee-like access a great idea and said OpenAI will do the same, with more to share soon. The post drew over 45,000 likes. [agreeing to pace the frontier](https://x.com/sama/status/2098811563415150910)

今天毫无疑问最重磅的一条:OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 公开表态认同 Dario 的观点,认为行业需要给前沿模型的推进"控制节奏",并说这几周这一直是 OpenAI 内部讨论的核心话题。他认为让独立评估人员拥有"类似员工级别的访问权限"是个很好的主意,OpenAI 也会这么做,更多细节很快公布。这条帖子拿到了超过 45000 个赞。[同意控制前沿节奏](https://x.com/sama/status/2098811563415150910)

### Thariq, Claude Code at Anthropic

Claude Code's Thariq wrote that if you had shown him Claude Code in 2018, he would have thought it was AGI. Software engineering and society have already absorbed a dramatic amount of change, but he says cracks are starting to show: things are accelerating faster than he can keep up with, and most people he knows in AI are tired but powering through. He argues we need time to harden our systems and for society to deliberate on how the technology is deployed. He describes his p(doom) as fairly low and is confident humanity can adapt, but says that depends on making hard decisions like this one together. [on slowing down](https://x.com/trq212/status/2098860941391872132)

Anthropic Claude Code 团队的 Thariq 说,如果 2018 年有人给他看今天的 Claude Code,他会以为那就是 AGI。软件工程这个行业乃至整个社会已经消化了巨大的变化,但他觉得裂缝开始出现了:技术加速的速度已经快到他自己都跟不上,他认识的大多数 AI 从业者都很疲惫,只是在硬撑。他认为我们需要时间去加固系统,也需要让社会好好讨论这项技术该怎么使用和部署。他自己的 p(doom) 挺低,相信人类足够有韧性、能适应,但前提是大家能一起做出像这样的艰难决定。[关于放慢节奏](https://x.com/trq212/status/2098860941391872132)

### Guillermo Rauch, Vercel CEO

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch took the contrarian side on the slowdown debate: the concerns over AI safety and cybersecurity are legitimate, he says, but America risks talking itself into self-inflicted obsolescence and bureaucracy. He called the OpenAI-hacking-Hugging-Face argument spurious (an agent in "ExploitGym"... exploited), and argued adversaries have the techniques, data and will to attack and won't be slowed by "embedded evaluators." On the building side, he reported that teams at Vercel iterate just as fast on Zig, Go and Rust as on TypeScript and Python, declaring that "agents are the new compilers" that compile intent into fast software. He also shared that their agent can now orchestrate subagents across different models and reasoning efforts (e.g. Fable planning, Grok executing), configured via AGENTS.md or the prompt, working with any model and gateway. [against the slowdown](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2098787667030712757) · [agents are the new compilers](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2098833404707922239) · [multi-model subagents](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2098803573861621778)

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 在"要不要放慢"这场讨论里站在了反方:AI 安全和网络安全的担忧是合理的,但他觉得美国这个全球 AI 领头羊,可能正在把自己"说"进一种自我造成的落后和官僚泥潭。他认为拿"OpenAI 黑了 Hugging Face"来当论据站不住脚,一个在"ExploitGym"里的 agent,当然会去 exploit。对手有训练方法、有数据、也有攻击意愿,不会因为什么"embedded evaluators"就慢下来,人家内部嵌的更可能是"加速器"。在做产品这一侧,他说 Vercel 的团队现在做 Zig、Go、Rust 项目的迭代速度,已经和 TypeScript、Python 一样快了:"agent 就是新的编译器",把意图编译成高性能软件。他还分享了他们的 agent 现在能调度使用不同模型和不同推理强度的 subagent,比如 Fable 负责规划、Grok 负责执行,在 AGENTS.md 或 prompt 里写明偏好就行,适用于任何模型和任何 gateway。[反对放慢](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2098787667030712757) · [agent 是新的编译器](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2098833404707922239) · [多模型 subagent](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2098803573861621778)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie called the essay a good post that captures the practical realities of the path forward for frontier AI, even if he doesn't agree with all of it. At current capability levels, he expects some form of coordinated industry self-regulation, which he sees as generally good; the question is whether everyone can agree on what it looks like, and given the politics, labs may not even get a say. The bigger question is whether all countries participate: any slowdown hinges on broad participation, which game theory suggests is unlikely until risks become more severe and obvious. His forecast: pretty messy for a while. [on coordinated self-regulation](https://x.com/levie/status/2098785357307539882)

Box CEO Aaron Levie 觉得这篇文章写得不错,虽然不完全认同,但确实反映了前沿 AI 往前走的很多现实情况。以现在模型的能力水平,他认为行业迟早会出现某种协调一致的自我监管,整体来说是好事;关键是大家能不能就具体怎么做达成共识,而且照现在的政治走向,实验室们可能连发言权都没有。更大的问题是各个国家会不会都参与进来:任何"放慢"都取决于广泛参与,但从博弈论角度看,在风险变得更严重、更明显之前,这不太可能发生。他的判断是:接下来一段时间会相当混乱。[关于行业自我监管](https://x.com/levie/status/2098785357307539882)

### Alex Albert, Research at Anthropic

Anthropic's Alex Albert urged people to spend a few minutes this weekend reading the essay in full. He pointed out that embedded evaluators may sound unusual in tech but are normal elsewhere: big banks have federal examiners with desks in the building, and every US nuclear plant has full-time on-site inspectors. He thinks frontier AI labs should work the same way and sees this as a very practical first step. [on embedded evaluators](https://x.com/alexalbert__/status/2098814342443761909)

Anthropic 的 Alex Albert 建议大家这个周末花几分钟把这篇文章完整读一遍。他指出,"embedded evaluators"在科技圈听起来可能有点奇怪,但在其他行业很常见:大银行里就有联邦监管人员在楼里办公,美国每座核电站都有全职驻场的检查员。他认为前沿 AI 实验室也应该这样运作,而这是非常务实的第一步。[关于驻场评估人员](https://x.com/alexalbert__/status/2098814342443761909)

### Madhu Guru, Sr Director of AI at Meta

Meta's Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru (previously led Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana at Google) predicted that over the next 12 months many of the brightest minds in frontier model evals will move to groups like METR. That skill is currently concentrated in a few labs, data providers and independent research groups, and he expects more funding, financial independence from lab equity and the pull of addressing AI's existential risk to drive the migration. In a separate post, he said that before we solve AI alignment we have a serious human alignment problem, calling the bad-faith reactions to Dario's piece jarring. He doesn't agree with every point, but called it a thoughtful piece everyone should read that builds on ideas Demis Hassabis raised in July, and said humans need to align on the opportunities and risks, how to measure them, and how companies, governments and countries coordinate. [evals talent will move to METR](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098859477219037691) · [human alignment problem](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098803717432860987)

Meta AI 高级总监 Madhu Guru(之前在 Google 负责 Gemini、Veo 和 Nano Banana)预测:未来 12 个月里,会有很多顶尖的前沿模型 evals 人才流向 METR 这类机构。这项能力目前集中在少数几家实验室、数据供应商和独立研究团队手里,而更多的资金投入、不再依赖实验室股权带来的财务独立,再加上应对 AI 存在性风险的使命感,会推动这波人才迁移。在另一条帖子里,他说在解决 AI alignment 之前,我们先有一个严重的"人类 alignment"问题:看到各方对 Dario 那篇文章的恶意解读,让人很不舒服。他并不认同每一个观点,但认为这是一篇值得所有人读的深思熟虑之作,也延续了 Demis Hassabis 7 月提出的一些想法。他觉得人类需要先在几件事上对齐:机会、风险和二阶效应是什么,怎么衡量,以及公司、政府和国家之间如何协调。[evals 人才会流向 METR](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098859477219037691) · [人类的 alignment 问题](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098803717432860987)

### Peter Yang, AI tutorials creator

AI tutorial creator Peter Yang shared a practical agent use case: Grok bot helped him recover about $500 in unclaimed money with a prompt asking it to research every state's official unclaimed property database using his full name and every city he has lived in, then report what it found, who owes it and how much. He notes the prompt works with other agents too. [unclaimed money prompt](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2098818985164935288)

AI 教程博主 Peter Yang 分享了一个很实用的 agent 用法:Grok bot 帮他找回了大约 500 美元的无人认领资金。他的 prompt 是让 agent 用他的全名和他住过的每一个城市,去查每个州官方的无人认领财产数据库,然后告诉他查到了什么、谁欠的、欠多少。他也提到这个 prompt 用其他 agent 也能跑。[找回无人认领资金的 prompt](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2098818985164935288)
