### Claude, Anthropic's Official Account

Claude Fable 5.1 is available everywhere as of today, and alongside it Anthropic introduced Claude Mythos 5.1, a model built for cyberdefenders and life scientists that ships only through trusted access programs. The split is the interesting structural detail: one model goes out to everyone, and the one aimed at the two domains with the sharpest dual-use edges goes out through a gated channel.

Claude Fable 5.1从今天起全面开放,同时Anthropic还推出了Claude Mythos 5.1,这是一个面向网络安全防御人员和生命科学研究者的模型,只通过受信任访问计划发放。这种拆分本身是个值得注意的结构性细节:一个模型面向所有人,而那个瞄准了双用途边界最锋利的两个领域的模型,走的是受控通道。

The safeguards numbers deserve a closer read than the capability numbers, because they are about false positives rather than raw ability. Cybersecurity safeguards now flag benign requests about 60 percent less often, and on basic biology and medical questions the fallback rate has dropped by roughly 85 percent. Those are refusal-tax reductions, and they matter most to exactly the users who kept hitting a wall while doing legitimate work.

比起能力数字,这次的安全护栏数字更值得细看,因为它们衡量的是误报而不是硬实力。网络安全方面的护栏现在对无害请求的误标率下降了约60%,而在基础生物学和医学问题上,回退率降低了大约85%。这些都是在削减"拒答税",而受益最大的,恰恰是那些一边做正经工作一边不断撞墙的用户。

### Alex Albert, Anthropic Research

Alex Albert, who works on research at Anthropic, singled out Enterprise Frontier Safeguards as the non-model announcement he is most excited about, and his framing is the clearest explanation of why it exists. Companies are giving agents more and more access to internal systems, and under traditional zero data retention there has been no way to spot patterns in what those agents might be doing across sessions. His shorthand is that EFS is ZDR++, meaning zero data retention rebuilt for a world with agents: a company's data stays in its own cloud, and an automated monitoring layer flags risky patterns back to that company's own team.

在Anthropic做研究的Alex Albert专门点出Enterprise Frontier Safeguards是这次他最兴奋的非模型发布,而他的表述也把这东西存在的理由讲得最清楚。企业正在给agent开放越来越多的内部系统权限,而在传统的zero data retention之下,你没有任何办法看出这些agent跨会话到底在做什么模式化的行为。他的说法是,EFS就是ZDR++,也就是为agent时代重建的zero data retention:企业的数据留在自己的云里,一个自动化监控层把有风险的模式标记出来,反馈给企业自己的团队。

He reads this as an observability and risk mitigation layer for agents, and predicts it becomes a standard enterprise requirement for running increasingly powerful AI on internal systems. He also notes the blog post is worth reading even if you are not a CEO or CISO, as a window into how large companies are actually reasoning about AI adoption.

他把这理解为给agent用的可观测性与风险缓解层,并预测它会成为企业在内部系统上运行越来越强大的AI时的标配要求。他还提到,就算你不是CEO或CISO,那篇博客也值得一读,因为它是一扇窗,让你看到大公司在AI落地这件事上真实的思考方式。

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie brought the most useful third-party evidence of the day, because Box has been testing Fable 5.1 in early release against its own complex enterprise work eval. The headline is a 7 percentage point jump over Fable 5 on unstructured data tasks, measured by running the Box Agent through real world scenarios across financial services, life sciences, the public sector and other industries, all of which demand analytical, math, logic and domain knowledge to complete.

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie带来了当天最有用的第三方证据,因为Box已经在早期版本阶段用自家那套复杂企业工作评测跑过Fable 5.1了。核心结论是:在非结构化数据任务上,相比Fable 5有7个百分点的提升。测法是让Box Agent去处理金融服务、生命科学、公共部门等多个行业的真实场景,而这些任务无一例外都需要分析能力、数学、逻辑和领域知识才能完成。

The individual failure modes are what make the number legible. In financial services, up 17 percent, Fable 5.1 correctly applies capital allowances before computing tax liability on a tax-adjusted profit projection, a subtle ordering that Fable 5 misses in a way that corrupts every figure downstream through retained earnings. In technology, up 37 percent, on a cost-optimization analysis where a key metric's normalization was ambiguous, Fable 5.1 recognized the ambiguity, computed both forms and presented the correct one, where Fable 5 simply committed to the wrong normalization. In the public sector, up 16 percent, Fable 5.1 works through a full weighted-mean methodology and produces correct rankings while Fable 5 miscategorizes one item and cascades the error through the whole sheet. Fable 5.1 will be available shortly in Box AI Studio.

真正让这个数字变得可读的,是那些具体的失败模式。金融服务提升17%:在一个税后利润预测任务里,Fable 5.1会先正确地应用资本减免再计算税负,而Fable 5漏掉了这个微妙的顺序,导致从那里开始一路到留存收益的每一个数字全错。科技行业提升37%:在一个成本优化分析里,某个关键指标的归一化方式存在歧义,Fable 5.1识别出了这个歧义,把两种算法都算了一遍,然后给出正确的那个;而Fable 5直接选了错的那种归一化。公共部门提升16%:Fable 5.1完整走完了加权平均的方法论并给出正确排名,而Fable 5把其中一项归错了类,错误又顺着整张表扩散开来。Fable 5.1很快会在Box AI Studio上线。

Separately he made a structural argument about who gets to have models. Now that open weights base models are getting far better and post-training infrastructure is maturing and commercializing, companies sitting on large amounts of data have a new option. Licensing that data out for someone else's model training used to be the only play; now you can reasonably train your own without taking on the cost and complexity of competing with the labs on research. He still expects general purpose frontier models to keep an edge on breadth, but sees a future with far more models than today, spread across every vertical and domain. ### Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind is bringing agentic video understanding to its latest Gemini models, and the efficiency claim is the striking part: better accuracy on video analysis while using up to 88 percent fewer tokens. The mechanism is what earns the word agentic. Instead of scanning an entire file, Gemini reasons across the video's transcript, audio and frames together, dynamically adjusting the frame rate to pull only the exact moments it needs.

Google DeepMind正在把agentic video understanding带进最新的Gemini模型,而最抓人的是效率上的说法:视频分析准确率更高的同时,token用量最多减少88%。真正让"agentic"这个词站得住的是它的机制。Gemini不再扫描整个文件,而是把视频的转录文本、音频和画面帧放在一起推理,并动态调整帧率,只抽取它真正需要的那些片段。

The gains are most significant on long-form content, from 10-minute guides to multi-hour recordings, which is exactly where naive uniform sampling wastes the most. It is rolling out to 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash and 3.5 Flash-Lite via API in Google AI Studio, and is coming soon to the Gemini app. [agentic video understanding with 88% fewer tokens](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2094840179676660097) · [how the dynamic frame rate works](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2094840182457422260)

### AI at Meta

AI at Meta shipped Muse Voice Transcribe, and the architecture description is unusually specific for a launch post. It is an autoregressive multimodal LLM from the Muse Spark family. Audio is processed in 80 millisecond chunks at 12.5 Hz, one token each, and at every chunk the model decides whether to keep listening or emit text.

AI at Meta发布了Muse Voice Transcribe,而这次发布文里的架构描述细致得不太寻常。它是Muse Spark系列中的一个自回归多模态LLM。音频以80毫秒为一块、按12.5 Hz的节奏处理,每块对应一个token,而在每一块上,模型都会决定是继续听下去还是直接吐出文字。

The training detail is where it gets interesting. RL with a combined word error rate and delay reward gives the model adaptive delay: it waits longer on hard words and commits sooner on easy ones, trading accuracy against latency word by word rather than at a single global setting. Meta claims that adaptive delay puts it on the pareto frontier of the speed-accuracy trade-off as measured by time to final transcription. It is available today via the Meta Model API, Meta AI for Mac, and Muse Code. [how Muse Voice Transcribe works](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2094839238495801457) · [pareto frontier on speed vs accuracy](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2094839240748138978) · [available today](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2094839243126243712)

有意思的是训练上的细节。用词错误率(WER)和延迟共同构成奖励的RL,让模型获得了自适应延迟能力:遇到难词就多等一会儿,遇到简单的词就早点定下来,把准确率与延迟的权衡下放到逐词粒度,而不是设一个全局参数。Meta称,自适应延迟让它在"到最终转录结果的时间"这个衡量口径下,处在速度与准确率权衡的pareto frontier上。今天起可通过Meta Model API、Meta AI for Mac以及Muse Code使用。[Muse Voice Transcribe的工作原理](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2094839238495801457) · [速度与准确率的pareto frontier](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2094839240748138978) · [今天起可用](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2094839243126243712)

### Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta

Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta and previously the lead on Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana at Google, argued that self-improving products are a meta idea every company should be implementing, and then did the useful thing of listing what you actually need to build one. A crisp definition of the metrics you care about, split into primary, secondary and guardrail. An articulation of your strategy, roadmap and which metrics you most want to move. A knowledge base of past product decisions and principles, so the agent knows how decisions get made at your company. Connections into your internal product systems: dashboards, APIs, MCPs and whatever tools the agent needs to observe and act. And a harness built with knowledge of your end to end product development flow. His encouraging note is that depending on product complexity, you can prototype with fairly simple versions of each and reuse existing software engineering agents.

Meta的AI高级总监Madhu Guru(此前在Google主导过Gemini、Veo和Nano Banana)提出,自我改进型产品是每家公司都该落地的元级思路,然后做了件很实在的事:把真正需要的组件列了出来。第一,对你在意的指标有清晰定义,分成主要指标、次要指标和护栏指标。第二,把你的战略、路线图以及最想推动的指标讲明白。第三,一个记录过往产品决策与原则的知识库,让agent知道你们公司是怎么做决策的。第四,打通内部产品系统的连接:仪表盘、API、MCP,以及agent需要用来观察和行动的各种工具。第五,一个基于你端到端产品开发流程构建的harness。他给的鼓励是:视产品复杂度而定,上面每一项你都可以先用相当简单的版本做原型,并复用现有的软件工程agent。

His second post is a sharper claim about the PM job itself: there is immense alpha in understanding the model frontier for your specific product and use cases, and you should be able to answer four questions better than most frontier lab researchers and PMs can for your use cases. What can models in each size category do well today, where does each model fail, what workarounds overcome those failures, and given the trajectory what will these models likely do two to three months from now, and what should your roadmap look like as a result. He is explicit that this is now a core part of the job, not an optional edge. It was his most resonant post at 274 likes.
