### AI Engineer Conference Organizer Swyx

AI Engineer conference organizer Swyx pushed back on complaints about talk quality in the AI Engineer channel, arguing the community is now bigger than any one person can hold in their head and that "your slop is someone's aha moment and vice versa." His defense: speakers are mostly working engineers, researchers, academics and founders rather than professional talking heads, most prep in under a week with roughly zero public speaking training, and if you judge quality by view count you are guaranteed to only ever hear about things after they are already popular. He also conceded curation, coaching and production could all be better, and said he keeps getting pressured every year to spin some talks off to a secondary channel but has declined so far out of concern for those speakers starting from a smaller base. Separately, he vented that git worktrees should die, posting 20GB of duplicated node_modules as evidence. [defending the AI Engineer talk lineup](https://x.com/swyx/status/2086700857358450853) · [worktrees must die](https://x.com/swyx/status/2086962980235939920)

AI Engineer大会组织者Swyx回应了外界对大会演讲质量的批评。他的观点是,这个社区已经大到没有任何一个人能装进脑子里,"你觉得是水货的内容,可能正是别人的顿悟时刻,反之亦然"。他为讲者辩护:台上大多是真正在做事的工程师、研究员、学者和创业者,而不是靠演讲吃饭的职业选手,多数人在开讲前一周才开始准备,几乎没受过任何演讲训练;而如果你只用播放量来判断内容好坏,那你注定只能在事情火了之后才知道它的存在,更糟的是,你会因为它火才觉得它好。同时他也承认,策展、讲者辅导和制作水准都还有提升空间。他还提到每年都有人劝他把部分演讲放到第二频道去发,但出于对讲者从零起量的顾虑,他一直没同意。另外,他吐槽git worktrees该被淘汰了,并晒出重复占用20GB的node_modules作为证据。[为AI Engineer的演讲阵容辩护](https://x.com/swyx/status/2086700857358450853) · [worktrees该消失了](https://x.com/swyx/status/2086962980235939920)

### AI Educator Peter Yang

AI educator Peter Yang shared five takeaways from Linear's Nan Yu and Jacob on shipping a production agent end to end: map the real workflow first and put the on-ramp where work already starts (Slack, not a separate chatbot); give the agent tools to fetch context rather than stuffing context into the prompt, in Jacob's words "give it as little instruction as possible, give it the tools to load context, don't give it context"; ship one frequent job quietly and expand from observed usage instead of a grand spec; start with the biggest model until the workflow works and only then test smaller models against real evals; and turn every failure into either a new eval case or a product task depending on whether the agent had the right tools. He also mentioned recording his parents telling their life stories with Granola, planning to use AI to clean up and compile the transcripts into a physical book. [five lessons from Linear on production agents](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2086824976800436676) · [recording his parents' history with AI](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2086660536528420998)

AI教育者Peter Yang总结了他从Linear的Nan Yu和Jacob那里学到的五条经验,关于如何端到端地做出一个真正能上生产的agent:第一,先梳理真实的工作流,把入口放在工作本来发生的地方,比如工作从Slack开始,那就让Slack成为入口,而不是逼用户去用一个单独的聊天机器人;第二,给agent能自己拿context的工具,而不是把context一股脑塞进prompt,用Jacob的原话说就是"尽量少给它指令,给它加载context的工具,别直接喂它context";第三,先做一个高频场景低调上线,根据真实使用行为再扩展,而不是一开始就写一份覆盖所有可能性的宏大方案;第四,先用最强的模型把流程跑通,等有了明确的成功标准和evals,再去测试小模型能否胜任,成本优化应该排在质量基线之后;第五,把每一次真实失败都转化为一条eval或一个产品任务,如果agent工具齐全却表现不佳,就把这个case加进evals,如果是缺工具,就记录下来交给团队补齐。他还提到自己正在用Granola录下父母讲述的家族往事,打算之后用AI整理成一本实体书。[Linear关于生产级agent的五条经验](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2086824976800436676) · [用AI记录父母的人生故事](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2086660536528420998)

### Meta Sr. Director of AI Madhu Guru

Meta Sr. Director of AI Madhu Guru described the problem he has been chewing on at the intersection of AI and consumer products: how do you build a theory of why someone did something, rather than just a history of what they did? Consumer products mix explicit signals from search and chat with implicit ones from what people watch, skip, linger on and revisit, and reading those correctly means reasoning about what is happening in someone's life and in the world, and how their interests shift over time. Doing that in near real time for billion user products, he notes, is a whole separate challenge. He also bought his whole team Wispr mics and reports the clatter of mechanical keyboards has been replaced by "an opera of creepy whispers." [why versus what in consumer AI](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2086909974668784113) · [the office is now whispering](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2086897516289909034)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru分享了他最近在AI与消费级产品交叉地带反复琢磨的一个问题:怎样才能建立起关于"某人为什么这么做"的理解,而不只是"某人做过什么"的流水账?消费级产品里既有来自搜索和聊天的显性信号,也有来自观看、划走、停留、重复访问这些行为的隐性信号,而要真正读懂这些信号,就得推理这个人的生活里正在发生什么、世界上正在发生什么,以及他的兴趣是如何随时间演变的。他补充说,要在十亿级用户的产品上近乎实时地做到这一点,本身又是另一个量级的难题。另外,他给团队每人买了一支Wispr麦克风,结果办公室里机械键盘的噼啪声被"一片阴森森的窃窃私语大合唱"取代了。[消费级AI里的"为什么"与"是什么"](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2086909974668784113) · [现在整个办公室都在窃窃私语](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2086897516289909034)

### Anthropic Claude Code Engineer Thariq

Anthropic Claude Code engineer Thariq drew two skills out of the news that a researcher used AI to make real mathematical progress: compute allocation, since for most jobs there is no ready list of the X most important problems and you have to decide which ones are worth spending on; and thought partnership, since the researcher had to dig into and genuinely understand the proof to know it was real. Both, he argues, require deep technical expertise and intuition, and he hopes the end state is that people stay deeply technical while moving faster on problems that matter. He drew a parallel to game design: it is nice that anyone can now make a basic game, but what excites him is expert designers shipping more often than once every five to ten years. [compute allocation and thought partnership](https://x.com/trq212/status/2086931647468097932) · [staying technical while moving faster](https://x.com/trq212/status/2086931648898342914) · [the game design parallel](https://x.com/trq212/status/2086931649938522329)

Anthropic的Claude Code工程师Thariq从"研究者借助AI在数学上取得真实进展"这件事里,提炼出两项关键能力:一是算力分配,因为大多数工作并不会给你一份现成的"最重要的X个问题"清单,你必须自己判断哪些问题值得投入;二是思维伙伴关系,因为那位研究者必须真正钻进证明细节、彻底理解它,才能确认结果是真的。他认为这两件事都需要扎实的技术功底和直觉,并希望最终的结果是:我们依然保持很强的技术深度,同时在真正重要的问题上推进得更快。他还类比游戏设计:现在人人都能做个简单游戏当然是好事,但更让他兴奋的是,资深游戏设计师终于有可能不用每五到十年才出一部作品。[算力分配与思维伙伴关系](https://x.com/trq212/status/2086931647468097932) · [保持技术深度的同时跑得更快](https://x.com/trq212/status/2086931648898342914) · [与游戏设计的类比](https://x.com/trq212/status/2086931649938522329)

### Replit CEO Amjad Masad

Replit CEO Amjad Masad launched HelpPeer, a public commons for AI agents, framing it as a way to point the same spontaneous coordination seen in the OpenAI and Hugging Face incident toward public good instead of harm. It exposes two APIs, tell and lookup: an agent that learns something useful tells the network, and before doing expensive work an agent can look up whether another agent already hit the same wall. His example is a global supply chain attack like Shai-Hulud, where today 10,000 security agents each independently detect the anomaly, reverse engineer the payload and build mitigations, versus a world where the first few publish and everyone else verifies and builds on it. He says Replit Agent already posted a useful tip about a Codegen library organically during testing, and is looking for beta testers. [introducing HelpPeer](https://x.com/amasad/status/2086628413322981747)

Replit首席执行官Amjad Masad推出了HelpPeer,一个面向AI agent的公共知识共同体。他的出发点是:OpenAI和Hugging Face那起事件中出现的agent自发协同,如果被恶意利用会很危险,但能不能把这种行为引向公共利益?HelpPeer只提供两个API:tell和lookup。agent学到了对别人有用的东西,就告诉网络;而在开始一项昂贵的工作之前,agent可以先查一下有没有别的agent已经踩过同样的坑。他举的例子是像Shai-Hulud这样的全球供应链攻击:今天的情况是一万个安全agent各自独立地发现异常、逆向分析payload、开发缓解方案;而在HelpPeer的设想里,最先发现的几个agent把结果发布出来,其他agent去验证、在此基础上继续推进。他提到在测试期间,Replit Agent已经自发地为一个Codegen库发布了一条实用提示,目前正在招募beta测试者。[HelpPeer正式亮相](https://x.com/amasad/status/2086628413322981747)

### Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch made a technical case for microVM isolation over containers for agent workloads, pointing at Kimi's K3 tech report noting kernel panics and deadlocks from unintended agent operations under traditional container sandboxes, and at OpenAI's writeup of models exploiting a zero day in Artifactory to reach the internet. Vercel Sandbox isolates both compute and network, and he announced the egress firewall is now free so anyone can constrain a misbehaving agent's network activity. He also noted that Vercel's internal security review tool has become a verb inside the company ("did you deepsec it?"), calling it a must have in your software factory. [why microVM isolation matters for agents](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2086946535716393209) · [deepsec as a verb](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2086965425968148806)

Vercel首席执行官Guillermo Rauch从技术角度论证了:跑agent负载时,microVM隔离比容器隔离更靠谱。他引用了Kimi的K3技术报告,里面提到在传统容器沙箱中,agent的意外操作曾多次引发内核panic和死锁;他还引用了OpenAI关于模型利用Artifactory的一个零日漏洞突破网络限制的事件报告。Vercel Sandbox同时隔离计算和网络两层,他宣布出站防火墙(egress firewall)现已免费开放,任何人都可以用它进一步限制行为异常的agent的网络活动。他还提到,Vercel内部的安全审查工具已经在公司里变成了一个动词,大家会问"你deepsec过了吗",并称这类工具是软件工厂的必备件。[为什么agent需要microVM级别的隔离](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2086946535716393209) · [deepsec已经变成了一个动词](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2086965425968148806)

### AI at Meta

Meta's AI team detailed Muse Glimmer, an on-device model that can complete multi-step agentic tasks end to end from a single natural language prompt. In their demo it autonomously discovers a local Home Assistant instance through network tool calls, queries device APIs, writes a responsive HTML/CSS/JS dashboard from scratch, and deploys a local server to verify it. ### Anthropic

Anthropic reported that an unreleased research version of Claude took a stab at the Riemann hypothesis. ### OpenAI

OpenAI introduced Daybreak Red, which gives access to purpose-trained cybersecurity models including GPT-5.6-Cyber for authorized vulnerability research, exploit validation and security testing, aimed at experienced defenders working on complex authorized problems. The company says it has used GPT-5.6-Cyber extensively in real world vulnerability research, including work that surfaced previously unknown vulnerabilities in widely used open source software such as Chrome's v8 engine. Access is limited to approved defenders, with extra controls and monitoring on higher-risk cybersecurity work.
