### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

The most substantive independent evidence on Astra today came from Box CEO Aaron Levie, who had early preview access and ran it against Box's enterprise complex work eval. Headline: 77 percent overall versus 74 percent for GPT-5.6 Sol, the best model they have ever tested on their expanded and hardest test set. The aggregate gain is modest; the per-task-type gains are not.

今天关于Astra最有分量的第三方证据来自Box CEO Aaron Levie,他拿到了早期预览权限,在Box的"企业复杂工作"评测集上跑了一遍。总分:77%,对比GPT-5.6 Sol的74%,是他们在这套扩充过的、最难的测试集上跑过的最好成绩。总分提升不大,但分项提升不小。

The individual jumps are where the story is. Media and entertainment went from 48 to 100 percent, on a task ranking film genres and countries by profitability where the trick is applying the following year's tax-incentive corrections without double-counting them; Astra was perfect on every attempt while Sol got the rankings right but the underlying ratios wrong. Technology went from 69 to 97 percent on a region-funding decision where the documents never state the metric the brief asks for: Astra flagged the gap, labelled its own figure a proxy, and caught a growth claim that did not match the numbers beneath it, while Sol reported the proxy as the real thing.

真正的看点在分项跳变上。Media and entertainment从48%涨到100%,任务是根据一年的制作数据给电影类型和国家按盈利能力排序,难点在于要套用下一年度的税收激励修正而且不能重复计算;Astra每次尝试都是满分,Sol排序对了但底层的比率算错了。Technology从69%涨到97%,任务是从一堆性能和基础设施文档里选出该优先投入哪个地区,而文档里根本就没有直接给出题目要求的那个指标:Astra指出了这个缺口,主动把自己算出的数字标注为代理指标,还抓出了一处与下面数据对不上的增长表述;Sol则直接把代理指标当成真实指标报了出来。

Legal is the one enterprise buyers should read twice. On reviewing an NDA against a company's own contracting policy, reaching the right verdict is not enough, the answer has to cite the provision behind it. Both models declined to approve the draft, but only Astra separated whether the liability cap's structure was permissible from whether its amount was defensible and pointed to the policy language that settles it. Sol argued the same conclusion without citing the provision, which Levie calls a critical error in that industry. Healthcare went 53 to 77 percent, catching a terminology error in a knee-imaging report Sol missed twice, and Energy went 82 to 97 percent, correctly identifying that only one of two facilities was actually missing data while Sol reported both as incomplete. Astra is coming to Box AI Studio as an option shortly.

Legal这一项是企业采购方应该反复读的。任务是拿一份NDA对照公司自己的合同政策做审查,光是结论正确还不够,答案必须引用支撑它的具体条款。两个模型都拒绝批准这份草稿,但只有Astra把"责任上限的结构是否合规"和"它的金额是否站得住"分开来看,并指出了决定这一点的政策原文。Sol给出了同样的结论却没有引用条款,Levie说这在法律行业属于致命错误。Healthcare从53%到77%,抓到了一份膝关节影像报告里的术语错误,而Sol两次都漏了。Energy从82%到97%,正确判断出两个厂区里只有一个真的缺数据,Sol则报告说两个都不完整。Astra很快会作为选项进入Box AI Studio。

He also marked another moment for open weights AI, arguing the infra platforms are working, the models are getting better, the ecosystems are being deeply invested in, and the business models are working. ### Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta

Meta senior AI director Madhu Guru, who previously led Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana at Google, argues the hard part of becoming more ambitious is not raising the goal, it is changing the how. His premise: AI and current market conditions make asymmetric outcomes far more possible, across scale, velocity, product breadth, and personal career and wealth goals.

Meta的AI高级总监Madhu Guru,此前在Google主导过Gemini、Veo和Nano Banana,他的观点是:变得更有野心,难的不是把目标定高,而是改变实现方式。他的前提是:AI加上当下的市场环境,让不对称的回报变得远比以前可能,无论是在规模、速度、产品广度上,还是在个人职业和财富目标上。

The blocker, he says, is inertia, both institutional and personal, and it shows up as three sentences: we have always done it this way, that feels too risky, that would be cringe. His prescription is concrete. Write down your current goals for your product or yourself, then ask what 100X would look like, then challenge yourself on which habits and assumptions you would have to drop to get there. [on ambition and changing the how](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2095526844653302269)

他说真正的阻力是惯性,既有组织层面的也有个人层面的,而它通常表现为三句话:我们一直都是这么做的;这听起来风险太大;这也太尴尬了吧。他给的处方很具体:把你现在为产品或为自己定的目标写下来,然后问一句"如果是100倍会长什么样",再逼自己想清楚,为了到那儿你必须扔掉哪些习惯和假设。[关于野心与改变实现方式](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2095526844653302269)
