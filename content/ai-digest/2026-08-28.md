### OpenAI, AI Lab

OpenAI put its name to an open letter calling for a global effort on cyber defense, co-signed with Anthropic, AWS, Google, Microsoft, and Oracle. The framing is what makes it worth reading: the company argues there is a limited window in which to strengthen defenses, and that the industry should spend that window giving defenders the tools, resources, and support to protect shared infrastructure. Frontier labs that compete on everything else agreeing on one thing is rare, and the thing they agreed on is not a capability benchmark or a licensing regime. It is the claim that offense is currently moving faster than defense. [the call for a global cyber defense effort](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2093074192636018977)

OpenAI在一封呼吁全球共同加强网络防御的公开信上署了名,联署方还包括Anthropic、AWS、Google、Microsoft和Oracle。真正值得读的是它的措辞方式:OpenAI认为,留给加固防御的窗口期是有限的,行业应该趁这段时间,把工具、资源和支持交到防御方手里,去保护大家共同依赖的基础设施。在其他所有事情上都在互相厮杀的前沿实验室,难得在一件事上达成一致,而这件事既不是某个能力基准,也不是某套牌照制度,而是一个判断:攻击的速度,眼下正在跑赢防御。[全球网络防御倡议](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2093074192636018977)

### Sam Altman, OpenAI CEO

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman's post backing the letter was the single loudest thing on the timeline today at over 9,000 likes, and it reads less like a launch and more like an alarm. He called this a critically important moment for cyber defense with AI and said there is not much time to act. The line worth noting is the one that gives away the competitive dynamics: he said OpenAI is happy if you want to work with them, or with any of their competitors or partners, as long as you take the moment seriously. That is a CEO explicitly declining to make a security problem into a customer acquisition channel, and arguing that only an urgent and intense collective response will work. [not much time to act](https://x.com/sama/status/2093060670472241368)

OpenAI首席执行官Sam Altman为这封信站台的那条帖子,是今天时间线上声量最大的一条,点赞超过9000,读起来不像发布会,更像警报。他说,这是AI时代网络防御的一个极其关键的时刻,留给行动的时间不多了。真正值得留意的是那句暴露了竞争格局的话:他说,你想和OpenAI合作也好,和他们的任何竞争对手或合作伙伴合作也罢,他们都乐见其成,只要你认真对待这个时刻。一位CEO明确表示不把安全问题做成获客渠道,并且强调只有紧急而高强度的集体响应才可能奏效。[时间不多了](https://x.com/sama/status/2093060670472241368)

### Anthropic, AI Safety and Research Company

Anthropic is opening up the Model Hardware Standard, an effort that started inside its collaboration with HHMI and is now going to research preview. The honest part of the announcement is the scoping: MHS currently covers lab and manufacturing equipment best, and the preview exists precisely because it does not yet cover everything else well. The extension target is the thing people are already doing anyway, since many developers are using Claude Code to operate hardware like boards and cameras, and the goal is to get all of it under one interface. Anthropic is inviting stakeholders across science, robotics, electronics, and manufacturing to join, with the open source community named as the next step after that. [how the Model Hardware Standard began](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093038433782624261) · [an invitation to the research preview](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093038432302080019) · [what MHS covers today](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093038431190548803)

Anthropic开始对外开放Model Hardware Standard,这套标准最初诞生于它和HHMI的合作,现在进入研究预览阶段。公告里最实诚的部分是它对适用范围的交代:MHS目前覆盖得最好的是实验室和制造设备,而之所以要做预览,恰恰是因为除此之外的场景还没覆盖好。它想扩展的方向其实是大家早就在做的事:很多开发者已经在用Claude Code去操作开发板、摄像头这类硬件,而目标是把这些设备全部收进同一套接口之下。Anthropic正在邀请科学、机器人、电子和制造领域的相关方加入,并把开源社区列为下一步。[MHS是怎么来的](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093038433782624261) · [研究预览邀请](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093038432302080019) · [MHS目前覆盖什么](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093038431190548803)

### Claude, Anthropic AI Assistant

Anthropic launched a Claude Team plan for scientists, opening 10,000 seats across fields from math to chemistry to physics. Standard seats are free and premium seats with 5x usage limits are $15 per month, an 80% discount, held for one year. The distribution mechanism is the detail worth copying: principal investigators at academic and nonprofit research institutions sign up and then add the researchers in their own group, which pushes the vetting problem onto people who already know who belongs in the lab. It builds on Claude Science from June and the AI for Science credits program, and Anthropic says it plans to go well beyond the initial 10,000 seats over the coming months. [a Claude Team plan for scientists](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2093059087298601113)

Anthropic推出了面向科研人员的Claude Team方案,首批开放10000个席位,覆盖从数学、化学到物理等各个领域。标准席位免费,高级席位每月15美元、用量上限是标准版的5倍,相当于打了两折,并且这个价格锁定一年。真正值得抄的细节是它的分发机制:由学术机构和非营利研究机构的课题负责人来注册,再由他们把自己组里的研究者加进来,等于把"谁有资格"这个审核难题,交给了本来就最清楚实验室里有哪些人的人。这个项目建立在6月上线的Claude Science和提供免费额度的AI for Science之上,Anthropic表示未来几个月会把规模远远推过最初这10000个席位。[面向科研人员的Claude Team方案](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2093059087298601113)

### Google DeepMind, AI Lab

Google DeepMind is piloting double-blind evaluations for frontier AI, which it calls an industry first. The setup is a secure environment where neither the test prompts nor the model weights are revealed to the other side, so external evaluators can assess safety and performance without the lab seeing the tests and without the evaluator seeing the model. This attacks the structural weakness in every third party audit arrangement to date, which is that a lab that can see the eval can train against it. Separately, the team published a podcast episode with VP of Research Zoubin Ghahramani on why uncertainty matters, arguing that teaching systems self doubt and probability is what makes real world decisions safer, with the conversation running from Bayesian thinking in AI through to AGI. [double-blind evaluations for frontier AI](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2092961763553677387) · [why uncertainty makes systems safer](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2093019627437662680) · [where to listen](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2093019632475001225)

Google DeepMind开始试点前沿AI的双盲评估,并称这是行业首例。做法是搭建一个安全环境,测试提示词和模型权重对彼此都不可见,这样外部评估方就能在实验室看不到题目、评估方也看不到模型的前提下,完成安全性和性能评估。这一招直击此前所有第三方审计安排的结构性弱点:只要实验室能看到评测集,它就可以针对评测集训练。另外,团队还发布了一期播客,由研究副总裁Zoubin Ghahramani讨论不确定性为什么重要,核心观点是让系统学会自我怀疑、学会用概率说话,才能让它在真实世界里做出更安全的决策,话题从AI中的贝叶斯思维一路聊到AGI。[前沿AI的双盲评估](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2092961763553677387) · [不确定性如何让系统更安全](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2093019627437662680) · [收听方式](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2093019632475001225)

### Garry Tan, Y Combinator CEO

Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan floated the most quietly unsettling take of the day: on a long enough time frame, AI will generate cash flows faster than the economy can find productive uses for new capital. That is not a bubble argument, it is close to the opposite, and it drew 86 replies because it implies the bottleneck stops being capital and becomes imagination. On the shipping side, he cut token load for GStack by 50% with no reduction in capability, which is the kind of unglamorous optimization that decides whether an agent stack is affordable to run at all. ### Peter Yang, AI Educator

AI educator Peter Yang made the sharpest product argument of the day, and it is bad news for a lot of startups. He gets three to five requests daily to test a new AI product, and almost all of them require creating an account and logging into a separate site or app, while he does essentially everything through ChatGPT, Grok, and similar harnesses that already hold all his context. His conclusion is that new products increasingly need to work inside today's top AI harnesses rather than asking users to start from scratch somewhere else, and he bets this segment of users expands drastically soon. He also raised a design gap in ChatGPT Health after uploading 160 pages of medical records, arguing it currently feels like a strictly single player experience when it should be built for the caregiver and family too, with sharing allowed among close family. And his no-ai-slop skill has hit 6,000 GitHub stars, which he demonstrated in reverse by having it generate deliberately awful wedding vows for a couple united by their shared passion for B2B SaaS. [why new AI products need to live in existing harnesses](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093126719888916616) · [ChatGPT Health needs a caregiver mode](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093099238381240447) · [running no-ai-slop in reverse](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093132262602920002)

AI内容创作者Peter Yang抛出了今天最锋利的一个产品判断,而这对很多创业公司来说不是好消息。他每天会收到三到五个新AI产品的试用请求,几乎每一个都要求他注册账号、再登录到一个独立的网站或应用里;而与此同时,他基本上所有事情都是在ChatGPT、Grok这类已经掌握了他全部上下文的harness里完成的。他的结论是:新产品越来越需要直接跑在当下最主流的AI harness里,而不是让用户换个地方从零开始;他还判断,这类用户很快会大幅扩张。他同时指出了ChatGPT Health的一个设计缺口。他上传了160页医疗记录,体验非常好,但目前这更像一个严格意义上的单人游戏,而它本应同时为照护者和家人设计,至少应该允许近亲之间共享。另外,他的no-ai-slop skill已经拿到6000个GitHub star,他还反过来用了一次:让它专门生成一段极其油腻的结婚誓词,送给一对因共同热爱B2B SaaS而走到一起的新人。[新AI产品必须活在现有harness里](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093126719888916616) · [ChatGPT Health需要照护者模式](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093099238381240447) · [反着用no-ai-slop](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093132262602920002)
