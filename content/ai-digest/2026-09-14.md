### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie weighed in on the pacing debate, acknowledging that "pacing" can be a trigger word because it sounds like an arbitrary slowdown or a way to hobble competitors through undue regulation. Still, he argued the specific improvement goals laid out in Dario's essay are absolute necessities: we expect equivalent standards in aerospace, life sciences and health care, and AI will underpin financial trading systems, medical devices, biotech, defense and government workflows. How to get there without meaningfully slowing innovation or reducing competition is, in his words, one of the most complex questions of the 21st century, but the need is clearly real. [on pacing and safety standards](https://x.com/levie/status/2099167992835924301)

Box CEO Aaron Levie 也加入了"控制节奏"的讨论。他承认"pacing"这个词挺敏感,听起来像是随意给能力踩刹车,或者借过度监管去拖竞争对手后腿。但他认为 Dario 文章里提出的那些具体改进目标是绝对必要的:航空航天、生命科学、医疗这些行业我们早就有同等要求,而 AI 将来会成为金融交易系统、医疗设备、生物技术突破、国防系统和政府流程的底层技术。怎么在不明显拖慢创新、不削弱竞争的前提下做到这一点,在他看来是 21 世纪最复杂的问题之一,但这个需求是实实在在的。[关于节奏与安全标准](https://x.com/levie/status/2099167992835924301)

### Peter Yang, AI Tutorials & Interviews Creator

Peter Yang reminded everyone that while we debate whether AI leads to a bad outcome for humanity, most of the world hasn't started using it yet. On his podcast, Brex CEO Pedro walked through a chart where each dot is 3.2 million people: 84% have never used AI, 16% are free chatbot users, about 0.3% pay $20 a month, and only about 0.04% use agents effectively. The chart is likely from February 2026, so adoption has probably picked up, but in his view we're still super early. He also flagged to OpenAI Devs that Record & Replay seems broken and won't trigger. ### Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw Creator at OpenAI

OpenClaw creator Peter Steinberger announced that the next release (or dev channel) creates worktrees about 80% faster by using APFS, Btrfs, XFS and ReFS folder clones, which also saves lots of disk space. The implementation is written in Rust ("because you can't escape Rust"), and after testing it for a few weeks, if it helps the majority of users, the plan is to try to get it into Codex. [80% faster worktrees](https://x.com/steipete/status/2099197266636783989) · [written in Rust](https://x.com/steipete/status/2099201162922938418) · [possibly coming to Codex](https://x.com/steipete/status/2099262092633411832)

### Sebastian Raschka, ML/AI Research Engineer

Sebastian Raschka, author of "Build a Large Language Model From Scratch", released round 3 of his Reasoning From Scratch video series. This installment covers building a verifier for two purposes: evaluating a base model against future improvements, and later powering reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). The walkthrough spans building a math verifier, extracting and normalizing boxed answers, checking mathematical equivalence, evaluating on MATH-500, prompt sensitivity and memorization, reproducibility across CPU/MPS/CUDA, and base vs. reasoning models. [Reasoning From Scratch round 3](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2099231450411290900) · [YouTube version](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2099231453531734297)

《Build a Large Language Model From Scratch》作者 Sebastian Raschka 发布了 Reasoning From Scratch 视频系列第三集。这一集讲的是怎么构建一个 verifier,用途有两个:一是做评估,对比 base model 和之后的各种改进;二是为后面的 RLVR(基于可验证奖励的强化学习)训练打基础。内容覆盖搭建数学 verifier、提取并规范化 boxed 答案、判断数学等价性、在 MATH-500 上做评估、prompt 敏感性与记忆问题、CPU/MPS/CUDA 之间的可复现性,以及 base model 和推理模型的对比。[Reasoning From Scratch 第三集](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2099231450411290900) · [YouTube 版本](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2099231453531734297)

### Nikunj Kothari, Partner at FPV Ventures

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari says a founder texts him at least once a day about the same dilemma, and warned job applicants that a higher entry valuation and higher total comp are often a bug, not a feature. A company raising at a higher valuation isn't necessarily more secure: look objectively at the market, traction and positioning, remember that a company at 100x ARR has to grow into it, and do your own analysis of the likely exit, 409A prices and tax implications. Speaking from experience, Kothari once worked at a company that raised $60M from one of the best investors on the planet and folded three years later with all equity wiped out. He also shared a list of questions to ask when joining a startup. [higher valuation is often a bug](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2099198567923765357) · [questions to ask when joining a startup](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2099272635926671532)

FPV Ventures 合伙人 Nikunj Kothari 说,他每天至少会收到一条创始人发来的消息,都是在纠结同一个问题。他提醒求职者:入职时估值更高、总包更高,很多时候是 bug 而不是 feature。一家公司融资估值高,并不代表它更稳。要客观看数字:市场有多大、traction 怎么样、竞争位置如何;如果估值是 ARR 的 100 倍,那它得靠增长把估值撑起来。还要自己判断公司大概能以什么价格退出,再算上 409A 价格和税务影响。他说自己就亲身经历过:曾经待过一家从全球顶级投资人那里融了 6000 万美元的公司,三年后倒闭,所有股权清零。他还分享了一份加入创业公司前该问的问题清单。[高估值往往是 bug](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2099198567923765357) · [加入创业公司前该问的问题](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2099272635926671532)
