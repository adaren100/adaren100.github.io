### Nan Yu, Linear head of product

Linear head of product Nan Yu argued that systems thinking in product development is part engineering, part behavioral economics: before shipping a feature, you need to ask how it will be used, misused, or abused, whether users will understand its purpose, and how it interacts with everything else in the product. [on systems thinking in product](https://x.com/thenanyu/status/2081139836342145412)

Linear产品负责人Nan Yu认为,产品开发中的系统性思维,一部分是工程学,一部分是行为经济学:在上线一个功能之前,你需要想清楚它会被怎样使用、误用或滥用,用户是否能理解它的目的,以及它和产品里其他部分之间会如何相互影响。[关于产品中的系统性思维](https://x.com/thenanyu/status/2081139836342145412)

### Guillermo Rauch, Vercel CEO

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch described how he now does all of his research with agent CLIs and the filesystem instead of dedicated apps: a `research/` folder plus an AGENTS.md describing his preferred format, then he just launches his agent and asks a question, has it correlate knowledge across past sessions, and if he wants to share something, has it render an HTML report and deploy it to Vercel. He tied this to a broader argument that "the software factory is the product" — a company's product is only as good as the agents autonomously maintaining and growing it, comparing it to what Elon Musk figured out about manufacturing at Tesla. [on researching with agent CLIs](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2081103993917649134) · [the software factory is the product](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2081123293340520642)

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch描述了自己现在如何用agent CLI和文件系统来做研究,而不是依赖专门的应用:一个`research/`文件夹,加上一份描述他偏好格式的AGENTS.md,然后直接启动agent、提出问题,让它去关联过往session里的知识;如果想分享某个结论,就让它渲染一份HTML报告并部署到Vercel上。他把这个做法和一个更大的论点联系起来:"软件工厂本身就是产品"——一家公司的产品好坏,取决于用来自主维护和成长它的那些agent够不够好,他把这比作Elon Musk在Tesla制造环节上悟到的道理。[用agent CLI做研究](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2081103993917649134) · [软件工厂就是产品](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2081123293340520642)

### Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw creator

OpenClaw creator Peter Steinberger ran a full end-to-end QA sweep of OpenClaw using live API keys, splitting the work across 12 subagents with separate dev gateways to stress test, orchestrating with git worktrees, and having agents open PRs autonomously — with a goal of finding 200 bugs, fixing root causes instead of band-aids, and writing a running markdown test report. He said he's been running Codex all day for similarly massive parallel QA ahead of the next release, and that the model has gotten much better at understanding intent and catching complex behavior issues, though these workflows used to fall apart at compaction boundaries or when the model started gaming the tests. Separately, he hit a new record with his team's autoreview skill: 66 rounds on a gnarly refactor. [full end-to-end QA sweep](https://x.com/steipete/status/2081169376317932017) · [massive parallel QA with Codex](https://x.com/steipete/status/2081169373784633552) · [66-round autoreview record](https://x.com/steipete/status/2080899298838098034)

OpenClaw作者Peter Steinberger用真实的API key对OpenClaw做了一次完整的端到端QA测试,把工作拆给12个subagent,各自搭配独立的dev gateway来做压力测试,用git worktree来编排,并让agent自主开PR,目标是找出200个bug,每次都修复根因而不是打补丁,还实时更新一份markdown测试报告。他表示自己一整天都在用Codex做类似的大规模并行QA,为下一个版本做准备,模型在理解意图、发现复杂行为问题方面进步明显,不过这类workflow过去常常在compaction边界处崩掉,或者模型开始"作弊"应付测试。另外,他们团队的autoreview skill这次刷新了纪录:在一次棘手的重构上跑了66轮。[完整端到端QA测试](https://x.com/steipete/status/2081169376317932017) · [用Codex做大规模并行QA](https://x.com/steipete/status/2081169373784633552) · [66轮autoreview纪录](https://x.com/steipete/status/2080899298838098034)

### Madhu Guru, Meta Sr Director of AI

Meta Sr Director of AI Madhu Guru reflected on how fast the AI community converged on supporting open-weight models, tracing the shift to a rapid sequence of public "experiments" — DeepSeek, the Microsoft-OpenAI breakup, GLM, Kimi, Fable, and the OpenAI-Hugging Face episode — each revealing something different about incentives, innovation, and geopolitics. He argued that this kind of repeated contact with reality is how the field will work through many of AI's biggest societal questions over the next decade. [on converging toward open weights](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2081141594892415028)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru反思了AI社区是如何这么快就在支持开放权重模型这件事上达成共识的,他把这种转变归因于一连串密集发生的公开"实验"——DeepSeek、微软与OpenAI的分手、GLM、Kimi、Fable,以及OpenAI与Hugging Face的风波——每一件事都从激励机制、创新和地缘政治的角度揭示了不同的东西。他认为,正是这种不断与现实碰撞的过程,会在未来十年里帮助整个领域回答许多关于AI的重大社会性问题。[关于走向开放权重的共识](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2081141594892415028)
