### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie argued that AI spend is nowhere close to hitting any walls. He acknowledged the data skews toward engineering-centric companies, with the top 1% spending 7,500 dollars per employee per month on AI and the top 10% spending 660 dollars, but said the exponential growth trend shows up across all firm types. His extrapolation is that what the top 10% are doing today may easily be what the top 50% are doing three years from now, at least in token volume if not in dollars, which is why he sees so much remaining opportunity in the diffusion of AI through the enterprise. As token costs fall, he expects us to throw larger portions of work at agents: scanning all our code for security issues, testing all of our software, writing code for much larger projects, processing nearly all data. [why AI spend has no ceiling yet](https://x.com/levie/status/2088995821056659901)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie认为,AI支出离撞上任何天花板都还早得很。他承认这份数据偏向工程导向的公司:花得最多的前1%,每位员工每月在AI上要花7500美元,前10%是660美元。但他说,这种指数级增长在各种类型的公司里都在发生。他的推论是,今天前10%在做的事,三年后很可能就是前50%在做的事,至少在token用量上是这样,金额未必。这也正是他觉得AI在企业内部的扩散仍然存在巨大机会的原因。随着token成本继续下降,他预计我们会把越来越大比例的工作直接丢给agent:扫描全部代码找安全问题、测试所有软件、写更大项目的代码、处理几乎所有数据。[为什么AI支出还远没到顶](https://x.com/levie/status/2088995821056659901)

### Dan Shipper, Every CEO

Every CEO Dan Shipper said he is also skeptical of the centralization of power hypothesis, and traced it back to Lewis Mumford in 1964, who argued that from late Neolithic times in the Near East right down to today, two technologies have recurrently existed side by side: one authoritarian, system-centered, immensely powerful but inherently unstable, the other democratic, man-centered, relatively weak but resourceful and durable. That framing still echoes around the valley, and he cited Peter Thiel's 2018 line that crypto is libertarian and AI is communist. He granted that AI looks centralized today and that hardcore AGI people expect that to continue, but pointed to the resurgence of fine tuning models for specific purposes, and to Amjad Masad's argument that the human brain is good evidence for the benefits of decentralization. His conclusion: we may just not see it yet because we are still in the hyperliterate bee or ant days of AI, and he would be surprised if the optimal design continues to be maximally centralized. [why maximal centralization may not be optimal](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2089127868903375257)

Every首席执行官Dan Shipper说他同样怀疑"AI必然导致权力集中"这个假说,并把源头追到了1964年的Lewis Mumford:从近东的新石器时代晚期一直到今天,两种技术始终并存,一种是威权的,以系统为中心,极其强大却本质上不稳定;另一种是民主的,以人为中心,相对弱小,但足智多谋、经得住时间。这套框架在硅谷一直有回响,他还引了Peter Thiel在2018年的那句话:crypto是自由意志主义的,AI是共产主义的。他承认今天的AI看上去确实更像在往中心化走,硬核的AGI信徒也认为这个趋势会延续下去,但他同时看到,针对特定用途做fine-tuning正在重新兴起;而正如Amjad Masad所说,人脑本身就是去中心化好处的有力证据。他的结论是:我们现在还没看到,可能只是因为AI仍然停留在"蜜蜂"或"蚂蚁"那种超字面的阶段。如果最优解一直是最大程度的中心化,他会挺意外的。[为什么最大化中心化未必是最优解](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2089127868903375257)

He also used Fable to vibe code an app that visualizes and groups everyone who applied to come to Thesis. His takeaway: we live in incredible times where a detailed understanding of each of your customers, and how they cluster together into groups, is possible with very little effort. [the app he vibe coded with Fable](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2089121597017759800)

他还用Fable直接vibe code了一个应用,把所有申请参加Thesis的人可视化出来并做了分组。他的感受是:我们生活在一个不可思议的时代,只要花很小的力气,就能对每一位客户有相当细致的理解,还能看清他们是怎么聚成一个个群体的。[他用Fable做出来的那个应用](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2089121597017759800)
