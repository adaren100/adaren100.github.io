### Sam Altman, OpenAI CEO

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said the company has paused some frontier RL training to make sure it can meet the appropriate alignment, security and monitoring standards for the new level of capabilities in front of it. His framing is that model progress is now extremely rapid, and OpenAI always said it would take action if it felt capabilities were outstripping the pace of safety and alignment. He expects confidence in safety to increasingly set the pace of AI progress, believes the entire field will have to coordinate on shared safety standards eventually, and says OpenAI will act unilaterally in the meantime while remaining committed to making frontier capabilities widely available. [pausing some frontier RL training](https://x.com/sama/status/2089787807611195475)

OpenAI首席执行官Sam Altman宣布,公司已经暂停了一部分前沿RL训练,以确保能在即将到来的新能力水平上,达到相应的alignment、安全和监控标准。他的说法是:模型能力的进展现在极快,而OpenAI一直说过,一旦觉得能力的推进速度超过了安全和alignment的跟进速度,就会采取行动。他认为,对安全的信心将越来越多地决定AI进步的节奏;他相信整个行业最终必须就共同的安全标准达成协调,但在那之前,OpenAI会先单方面行动,同时继续坚持让前沿能力被广泛使用。[暂停部分前沿RL训练](https://x.com/sama/status/2089787807611195475)

He followed up to head off the obvious misreading: OpenAI still expects to ship great new models soon, and what this affects is the further out releases. [what this does and does not affect](https://x.com/sama/status/2089805495783813196)

他随后补了一句,免得大家误读:OpenAI仍然预计很快会推出很不错的新模型,这次受影响的是更靠后的发布计划。[受影响的到底是哪部分](https://x.com/sama/status/2089805495783813196)

### OpenAI

OpenAI put specifics behind that decision. RL training on its latest models intended for deployment was temporarily paused for two weeks while the team hardened and red teamed its research environments and expanded monitoring coverage. The largest planned frontier RL run remains on hold while smaller scale training and evaluations validate those safeguards and establish more evidence of alignment. The underlying reasoning is that as models become more capable, the risks of developing and testing them internally grow as well. [the two week pause, and what is still on hold](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2089777845187031262)

OpenAI给出了这个决定的具体细节。针对准备部署的最新模型,RL训练被临时暂停了两周,这段时间团队在加固和red team自己的研究环境,并扩大监控覆盖范围。规模最大的那次前沿RL训练目前仍然处于暂停状态,要等更小规模的训练和评估验证完这些防护措施、拿出更多alignment方面的证据之后再说。背后的逻辑是:模型能力越强,内部开发和测试它们所带来的风险也越大。[两周的暂停,以及还压着没跑的那次训练](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2089777845187031262)

The concrete changes are stronger workload and network isolation, continuous security testing, and expanded multistage monitoring for higher risk training, evaluations and tool using inference. The safeguards are designed to detect concerning behavior quickly and limit what these systems can access or affect. [the specific safeguards](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2089777846583763370)

具体的改动包括:更强的工作负载隔离和网络隔离、持续性的安全测试,以及针对高风险训练、评估和会调用工具的推理场景,扩展了多阶段监控。这些措施的目标是尽快发现可疑行为,并限制这些系统能接触到什么、能影响到什么。[具体做了哪些防护](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2089777846583763370)

### Sebastian Raschka, evangelist

Sebastian Raschka, author of Build a Large Language Model From Scratch, posted a correction to something he had agreed with in his own comments: you do not actually need to rerun the LLM to check whether a text is watermarked. The method in question is based on SynthID-Text, which uses watermarking functions that give 1s for certain terms and 0s for others and then runs a tournament style selection, so the terms that survive score highly. To check a text later, you just reapply those cheap watermarking functions and see whether the score clears a threshold. It is closer to hashing than to inference. [correcting himself on watermark detection](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2089527404138033497)

《Build a Large Language Model From Scratch》的作者Sebastian Raschka纠正了自己之前在评论区认同过的一个说法:检查一段文本是否带watermark,其实并不需要重新跑一遍LLM。他说的这个方法基于SynthID-Text,做法是用一组watermarking函数,对某些词给1、对另一些给0,然后做一轮锦标赛式的筛选,活下来的词得分都很高。之后要验证某段文本,只需要把那些很便宜的watermarking函数重新套一遍,看分数有没有超过阈值就行。这更接近做哈希,而不是做推理。[他对watermark检测的自我纠正](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2089527404138033497)
