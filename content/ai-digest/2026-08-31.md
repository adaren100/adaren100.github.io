### Guillermo Rauch, Vercel CEO

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch put a CVE number on the story everyone has been arguing about in the abstract. Two days ago JFrog disclosed CVE-2026-82329, a critical authentication bypass in Artifactory scoring 9.8 on CVSS, which he compares to a 9.8 on the seismic scale. It hits default configurations, needs no authentication and no user interaction, and because Artifactory hosts binaries it is an RCE bomb where you can poison essentially everything downstream, with admin escalation causing damage beyond even that. His point is what the timing implies: when the OpenAI and Hugging Face news broke about agents discovering zero-days, he wondered whether it was marketing language or reality, because no CVE filing had appeared. Now one has. He is careful to say there is no official confirmation the two are connected, but he had previously written that agents helping humans find serious vulnerabilities was obvious while autonomous exploitation was a bridge not yet crossed, and he now reads that bridge as crossed. His guidance for this world is blunt: assume everything hackable will get hacked, assume it happens autonomously, and defend yourself autonomously too, because your attack surface is bigger and your code more vulnerable than you think. He laid the full argument out in a Vercel post titled "Everything hackable will get hacked."

Vercel首席执行官Guillermo Rauch给这场一直停留在抽象层面的争论,补上了一个具体的CVE编号。两天前JFrog披露了CVE-2026-82329,Artifactory里的一个严重认证绕过漏洞,CVSS评分9.8,他形容这个分数放到地震等级上就是9.8级。它影响默认配置,不需要认证,不需要任何用户交互;而因为Artifactory本身托管着二进制产物,这就是一颗RCE炸弹,你几乎可以往下游的所有东西里投毒,而一旦拿到管理员权限,破坏面还会更大。他真正想说的是时间点的含义:当OpenAI和Hugging Face那边传出"agent自主发现0day"的消息时,他一度怀疑这是营销话术还是真事,因为当时并没有看到任何CVE备案。现在备案出现了。他很谨慎地强调,没有任何官方确认说这两件事有关联,但他此前写过,agent能协助人类发现严重漏洞是显而易见的,而自主完成利用则是一道尚未跨过的坎;如今他认为这道坎已经跨过去了。他给出的应对原则很直接:假设一切可被攻破的东西终将被攻破,假设这个过程是自主发生的,那么你的防守也必须是自主的,因为你的攻击面比你以为的更大,你的代码也比你以为的更脆弱。完整论述写在Vercel那篇《Everything hackable will get hacked》里。

He also surfaced the operational cost of virality, and it is a side of the internet most people never see. When something goes viral, especially on X, it attracts every kind of attention, including some of the largest botnets on the internet launching targeted DDoS from hijacked devices and residential networks. He used it to credit the Vercel CDN and Traffic Security teams protecting more than 400,000 customers and tens of millions of users around the clock. ### Amjad Masad, Replit CEO

Replit CEO Amjad Masad drew the lesson from the Hugging Face incident one level below the headlines: reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards is an incredibly powerful optimization algorithm, and it will keep producing increasingly weird and surprising behavior from LLMs. That is a claim about the method rather than the model, which is why he treats strange emergent behavior as the expected output rather than the anomaly. His sharper criticism is aimed at process. The obvious miss by OpenAI, in his reading, is that they should have been monitoring chains of thought, something OpenAI itself named as a safety strategy more than a year ago. [RL with verifiable rewards and the CoT miss](https://x.com/amasad/status/2094215744842248418)

Replit首席执行官Amjad Masad把Hugging Face事件的教训往下挖了一层,落到了标题之外的地方:带可验证奖励的强化学习是一种极其强大的优化算法,它会持续让LLM产生越来越古怪、越来越出人意料的行为。这是在评价方法本身,而不是某个模型,所以在他眼里,离奇的涌现行为是这套方法的预期产物,而不是意外。他更尖锐的批评指向流程。在他看来,OpenAI明显漏掉的一环是本该监控思维链,而这恰恰是OpenAI自己在一年多以前就点名过的安全策略。[可验证奖励的RL,以及被漏掉的CoT监控](https://x.com/amasad/status/2094215744842248418)

### Dan Shipper, Every CEO

Every CEO Dan Shipper answered the Hugging Face question in four lines, and the structure is the argument. Should the attack be taken seriously? Yes. Is it the first warning shot of a machine takeover? No. Will problems of this type look tractable or mostly solved with proper precautions in six months? Yes. Will that happen automatically? No, and as always it will require work from smart people and their agents. It is a deliberately unpanicked reading that still refuses the comfortable version, and the last answer is the one carrying the weight.

Every首席执行官Dan Shipper用四句话回答了Hugging Face这件事,而这四句的结构本身就是他的论点。这次攻击值得认真对待吗?值得。它是机器接管的第一声警报吗?不是。六个月后,只要有恰当的防范,这类问题会不会看起来是可解决甚至基本已解决的?会。这件事会自动发生吗?不会,一如既往,它需要聪明人和他们的agent真正去做事。这是一个刻意不恐慌的判断,但它同样拒绝了那个让人舒服的版本,而分量最重的是最后一问。

He also took a shot at a narrative he thinks is finally cracking, saying he is glad the market is catching up to how dumb the SaaSpocalypse was. ### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie explained why cheaper tokens do not mean less spending on tokens, and he framed it as Jevons paradox seen up close in enterprise deployments. Enterprises have an unending stream of tasks they would love to automate, but each individual task is ROI positive or not depending on the cost of automating it, including setup time and change management. As tokens get cheaper past a certain capability threshold, more of that backlog crosses the line: processing every contract, reading every log, watching new data streams for insights, running background agents through workflows. His numbers are the memorable part, arguing that even a 50 percent drop in token prices could produce a 5x increase in token consumption for these workloads. The conclusion he draws is that driving down the cost of AI is critical and good for every participant in the market, not just buyers. [Jevons paradox for token consumption](https://x.com/levie/status/2094123406811922930)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie解释了为什么token变便宜并不意味着在token上的花费会变少,他把这称作在企业落地现场近距离看到的杰文斯悖论。企业手上有源源不断的任务想要自动化,但每一个具体任务是否ROI为正,取决于自动化它的成本有多高,这里面还要算上部署时间和变革管理的开销。当token价格越过某个能力门槛后继续下降,这份积压清单里就会有越来越多的任务跨过盈亏线:处理每一份合同、读取每一条日志、盯着新的数据流找洞察、让后台agent去跑完整的工作流。最让人记得住的是他给的数字:哪怕token价格只降50%,这类负载的token消耗量也可能涨5倍。由此他的结论是,持续压低AI的成本是关键的,而且对市场里的所有参与者都是好事,不只是买方。[token消耗上的杰文斯悖论](https://x.com/levie/status/2094123406811922930)
