### Swyx, AI Engineer Conference Organizer

AI Engineer conference organizer Swyx ran the same prompt against two frontier coding agents overnight, asking GPT Luna Max and Claude Fable Ultracode to "build a mostly faithful clone of grok imagine with open models via fal," and got the attribution backwards when he woke up to the results. His read after checking: Fable objectively produced the better visual clone, but Luna somehow understood intent better and shipped the more usable one given his preference for open models. Separately he flagged that pdb envs now have experimental AFS clone support that does what people keep asking for but stays runtime agnostic and language agnostic, adding that the goal is to "replace git by making every single command agent native." [Luna versus Fable on the same brief](https://x.com/swyx/status/2087045848022843451) · [AFS clone support in pdb envs](https://x.com/swyx/status/2087017780617126075)

### Madhu Guru, Sr. Director of AI at Meta

Meta Sr. Director of AI Madhu Guru made a direct call on where the money is: there is going to be a ton of it in making open-weight models exceptional at boring, specific business domains. His advice is to pick a size by domain combination and go deep, naming mid-market legal, SMB retail and enterprise logistics as examples. The reason he thinks this is an opening rather than a losing race: the hyperscalers have all the primitives, but they will struggle with domain depth, scrappiness, and the sheer will it takes to make a model exceptional for one particular kind of business. [the open-weight vertical opportunity](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2087198985685750013)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru直接点出了他认为钱在哪里:把open-weight模型打磨到在那些"无聊而具体"的商业领域里表现出色,这件事里会有非常多的钱。他的建议是选定一个"规模 x 领域"的组合然后往深里做,他举的例子是中型市场的法律、中小企业零售、企业物流。他之所以认为这是机会而不是一场必输的仗,理由是:hyperscaler们手里握着全部的基础能力,但他们很难具备那种领域纵深、草根式的灵活劲头,以及为某一类特定生意把模型死磕到极致的意志。[open-weight模型的垂直领域机会](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2087198985685750013)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that a US company shipping a frontier-class model as open weights would have been unbelievable three months ago, and that it matters because it opens up AI adoption in scenarios that were not viable before. Models can now be deployed on prem or on private cloud infrastructure, which unlocks highly regulated domains, and teams can post-train a frontier-class model for verticals like legal and healthcare that need extra performance on their specific tasks. He adds a sovereignty argument: if a model ever gets pulled from the marketplace, open weights give users the confidence to deploy in mission critical areas. His conclusion is that this is great news for the applied AI layer, where you route work to different model families based on the task, and a great time to be building at the harness layer, while closed frontier models stay heavily used because hard problems need a mix of capability levels. [why frontier open weights change the calculus](https://x.com/levie/status/2087009941806797206)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie认为,三个月前如果你告诉别人一家美国公司会把前沿级别的模型以开放权重发布,没人会信。而这件事之所以重要,是因为它打开了此前根本走不通的AI落地场景:模型现在可以部署在本地机房或私有云基础设施上,这就让强监管行业有了可能;团队也可以针对法律、医疗这类对特定任务性能要求更高的垂直场景,对一个前沿级模型做post-train。他还补充了一条主权层面的理由:万一哪天某个模型从市场上被下架,开放权重能让用户在关键业务场景里部署得更有底气。他的结论是,这对应用层AI是大好事,因为你可以根据任务把活派给不同的模型家族,现在也正是做harness层的好时机;与此同时闭源前沿模型依然会被大量使用,毕竟真正难的问题往往需要不同能力档位的模型混着上。[前沿开放权重为什么改变了算盘](https://x.com/levie/status/2087009941806797206)

### Sebastian Raschka, evangelist

ML research engineer and author Sebastian Raschka walked through the architecture of Meta Muse Glimmer, the first open-weight LLM from Meta since the Llama days: a 30B multimodal reasoning model with a Gemma-like design, most likely distilled from the more capable Muse Spark, which remains API only. The notable choices are a 131k context window, which is reasonable but on the short end next to Qwen3.6 and Gemma 4 at twice that natively; a dense rather than mixture-of-experts design, making Qwen3.6 27B the fairer comparison; hybrid attention combining grouped-query and sliding window attention at a 3:1 local to global ratio against Gemma 4's 5:1; gated attention on both paths; and an extreme 32 query heads to 2 KV heads ratio. That last choice is what stands out, producing a KV cache of 52 KiB per token in BF16 versus 64 KiB for Qwen3.6 27B and 840 KiB for Gemma 4 31B. Meta's own benchmarks put it mostly ahead of Qwen3.6 while the independent Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index puts it slightly behind, so real usage will settle it, but Raschka reads it as a solid model for agentic workflows given the low memory footprint and fast prefill and decode. [breaking down Meta Muse Glimmer](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2087180773497421926)

机器学习研究工程师、技术作者Sebastian Raschka拆解了Meta Muse Glimmer的架构,这是Meta自Llama时代以来第一个开放权重的LLM:一个30B的多模态推理模型,设计风格接近Gemma,很可能是从能力更强的Muse Spark蒸馏而来,而Muse Spark目前只能通过API使用。几个值得注意的选择:context window是131k,说得过去,但在Qwen3.6和Gemma 4原生支持两倍长度的当下算是偏短的一端;它是dense模型而非MoE,所以更公平的对比对象是Qwen3.6 27B;注意力机制是GQA和滑动窗口注意力(SWA)的混合,local和global的比例是3:1,而Gemma 4是5:1;两条路径上都用了gated attention;以及一个非常极端的配置,32个query head只配2个KV head。最后这一点是真正的亮点,它让BF16下每个token的KV cache只有52 KiB,相比之下Qwen3.6 27B是64 KiB,Gemma 4 31B则高达840 KiB。Meta自己的benchmark显示它基本领先Qwen3.6,而第三方的Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index则把它排在Qwen3.6稍后,真实排名还得靠实际使用几天才能见分晓。不过Raschka的判断是,凭借极低的显存占用和相当快的prefill与decode速度,这是一个很适合agentic工作流的扎实模型。[拆解Meta Muse Glimmer](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2087180773497421926)
