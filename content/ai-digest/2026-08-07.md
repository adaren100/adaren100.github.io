### Amjad Masad, Replit CEO

Replit CEO Amjad Masad argued Airtable's rise and fall bookends the no-code era: he says he used to argue endlessly with investors that a UI could never let people build arbitrary software, and that the only real path to accessible software was always solving code itself, a take he says used to sound delusional and no longer does. [no-code's rise and fall](https://x.com/amasad/status/2085451197323034902)

Replit首席执行官Amjad Masad认为Airtable的兴衰恰好为"无代码"时代画上了首尾呼应的句号。他说自己曾无数次和投资人争论:纯UI工具永远无法让人构建任意复杂的软件,让软件更普及的真正路径始终是解决"代码"本身这个问题,这个观点曾经听起来很不切实际,但现在已经不再是了。[无代码时代的兴衰](https://x.com/amasad/status/2085451197323034902)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie pushed back on the idea that AI agents are bad for systems-of-record software, arguing that as agents generate far more code, process much more data, and make more decisions across enterprise systems, the platforms managing that data and enforcing governance, security, and compliance become more important, not less. He separately predicted 99% of AI tokens will be consumed in enterprise contexts, while cautioning that full economic diffusion of agents will take years since workflows themselves need to be re-engineered around them. [why systems of record get more important, not less](https://x.com/levie/status/2085474309943030032) · [99% of tokens will be enterprise tokens](https://x.com/levie/status/2085200776159490111)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie反驳了"AI agent会削弱系统记录类软件价值"的说法,他认为当agent生成的代码量成百倍增长、处理海量数据、并在企业各系统间做出更多决策时,负责管理这些数据、执行治理、安全与合规的平台反而会变得更重要,而不是更不重要。他还预测全球99%的AI token最终会消耗在企业场景中,但同时提醒,agent在整个经济体系中的全面普及仍需数年时间,因为企业的工作流程本身也需要被重新设计才能真正融入AI。[为什么系统记录类软件会更重要而非更不重要](https://x.com/levie/status/2085474309943030032) · [99%的token将用于企业场景](https://x.com/levie/status/2085200776159490111)

### Madhu Guru, Meta Sr. Director of AI

Meta Sr. Director of AI Madhu Guru shared a process tip for his teams: people are much clearer speaking through a new idea than writing it into a doc, because the core idea often gets buried as writers try to polish it and make it sound smart, so he asks teams to record themselves explaining an idea like they would to a friend, use AI only for basic cleanup while keeping the original rough structure, and share that instead. [why speaking beats writing for new ideas](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2085390240899043406)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru分享了一个给团队的工作建议:人们在口头表达新想法时往往比写文档时思路更清晰,因为写文档过程中,为了让内容显得更周全、更聪明,核心想法反而容易被淹没。所以他要求团队先把想法像跟朋友聊天一样口述录下来,只用AI做基础整理,尽量保留原始的结构和思路,再把这份文档分享出去。[为什么口述比写文档更适合表达新想法](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2085390240899043406)

### Nikunj Kothari, FPV Ventures partner

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari published a brain dump of fundraising tips for founders now that August fundraising season is underway: get warm intros to actual decision-making partners rather than associates, don't ask for too much or too little dilution, never compare your valuation to a competitor's round, and in this consensus-driven market, non-hot-sector founders need a genuinely contrarian thesis to stand out. He separately predicted terms like "out of distribution," "control plane," and "intelligence per watt" will see much heavier use across AI circles in the next 6-9 months. FPV Ventures合伙人Nikunj Kothari在8月融资季到来之际,发布了一份详尽的创始人融资建议:尽量争取和真正有决策权的Partner(而不是associate)建立联系并获得引荐;融资额度和股权稀释比例都不宜过高或过低;绝对不要拿自己的估值去和竞争对手的融资轮次做比较;在当下这个高度趋同的市场里,如果你不在热门赛道,就需要一个足够有说服力的逆向押注叙事才能脱颖而出。他还预测未来6到9个月里,"out of distribution"(分布外)、"control plane"(控制平面)、"intelligence per watt"(每瓦算力智能)这类术语会在AI圈子里被更频繁地使用。[VC不会告诉你的融资建议](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2085382457457828153) · [接下来会流行起来的AI术语](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2085209022115029132)

### Google DeepMind, official account

Google DeepMind is open-sourcing the code and weights behind WeatherNext, its cyclone-forecasting model, for academic and operational use; trained on years of global atmospheric data plus nearly 5,000 historical cyclones, it generates a 15-day probabilistic forecast in under a minute on a TPU, and during Hurricane Melissa it gave forecasters an 80%-confidence Category 5 landfall prediction five days out, now available to forecasters as 1,000 predictions per storm via WeatherLab. [open-sourcing WeatherNext](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2085395452657049735) · [WeatherNext's Hurricane Melissa forecast](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2085395450656428306)

Google DeepMind正在开源其气旋预测模型WeatherNext的代码和权重,供学术研究和业务预报使用。该模型基于多年全球大气数据以及近5000个历史气旋案例训练而成,可以在TPU上于一分钟内生成一份15天的概率化预测。在"梅丽莎"飓风来袭期间,该模型提前5天以80%的置信度预测出其将以5级强度登陆,如今气象学家可以通过WeatherLab为每场风暴获取多达1000份概率化预测。[开源WeatherNext](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2085395452657049735) · [WeatherNext对飓风梅丽莎的预测](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2085395450656428306)
