### Sebastian Raschka, ML research engineer & author

ML research engineer and author Sebastian Raschka published a detailed breakdown of the Kimi K3 architecture, calling it essentially a scaled-up production version of Kimi Linear (48B to 2.8T parameters), making it by far the largest open-weight model released so far. He highlighted a new LatentMoE component similar to Nemotron 3 Ultra's, a broader industry trend toward inference efficiency (MoE to LatentMoE, regular attention to multi-head latent attention and Kimi Delta Attention), and "attention residuals" that connect residuals across layers using attention scores, which reportedly improve validation loss for a modest training/inference cost. Most notably, he pointed out Kimi K3 drops RoPE entirely in favor of NoPE (no positional embeddings) everywhere — the first frontier-level model he's aware of to do so — and now ships with native multimodal support. [Kimi K3 architecture deep dive](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2082098201247600765) · [architecture complexity trend](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2082101241908249014)

ML研究工程师、作者Sebastian Raschka发布了一份对Kimi K3架构的详细拆解,他认为这本质上是Kimi Linear的放大生产版本(参数从48B放大到2.8T),使其成为目前为止规模最大的开源权重模型。他重点提到了一个类似Nemotron 3 Ultra的新组件LatentMoE、行业整体转向推理效率优化的趋势(MoE变成LatentMoE,常规attention变成multi-head latent attention和Kimi Delta Attention),以及用attention分数把跨层残差连接起来的"attention residuals"设计,据报告能小幅提升验证损失,同时只增加约4%的训练成本和2%的推理成本。最值得关注的是,他指出Kimi K3完全抛弃了RoPE,全程改用NoPE(无位置编码)——据他所知这是第一个这样做的前沿级模型——并且现在原生支持多模态。[Kimi K3架构深度解析](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2082098201247600765) · [架构复杂度上升的趋势](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2082101241908249014)

Separately, he released the Forward Deployed Engineering track from AI Engineer World's Fair, curated with Basil Chatha, spanning FDE 101 through head-of-FDE talks from Decagon, Cursor, Sierra, Ramp, Cognition, Factory, Kepler, and Varick. ### Andrew Ng, Coursera co-founder & Stanford adjunct faculty

Coursera co-founder and Stanford adjunct faculty Andrew Ng announced he's starting LearnVector, a new venture aiming to turn learning from one-to-many into one-to-one with a personalized AI learning guide, backed by a $100M investment from Coursera and close collaboration with Coursera and Udemy. He was careful to distinguish this from an ungated chatbot, citing research that chatbots without guardrails can harm learning through cognitive offloading even as they boost homework completion, and positioned LearnVector as building on Coursera's trusted content library instead. [launching LearnVector](https://x.com/AndrewYNg/status/2082199333920027009)

Coursera联合创始人、斯坦福客座教授Andrew Ng宣布创立新项目LearnVector,目标是借助个性化AI学习向导,把学习方式从"一对多"变成"一对一",该项目获得了Coursera 1亿美元的投资,并将与Coursera和Udemy展开密切合作。他特意强调这和一个没有限制的聊天机器人不一样,并引用研究指出,没有护栏的聊天机器人虽然能提高作业完成度,却可能因为"认知外包"而损害真正的学习效果,LearnVector则会建立在Coursera可信赖的内容库之上。[创立LearnVector](https://x.com/AndrewYNg/status/2082199333920027009)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie pushed back on predictions of an AI-driven jobs collapse, arguing the data he's seeing across industries shows the opposite: enterprises are still hiring, just shifting toward roles where AI lets them do more — more engineers tackling problems they couldn't before, more sales staff going deeper with clients, more internal FDEs to help deploy AI. He warned that companies using AI purely to cut costs will eventually be outcompeted by those using it to serve customers better, though he allowed the trend could still shift. [on AI, hiring, and Jevons paradox](https://x.com/levie/status/2081930301752942703)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie反驳了"AI将导致就业崩溃"的预测,他表示自己在各行各业观察到的情况恰恰相反:企业仍在招人,只是招聘方向转向了那些AI能让员工做得更多的岗位——招更多工程师去解决以前搞不定的问题,招更多销售去和客户建立更深的关系,招更多内部FDE来帮助部署AI。他警告说,单纯用AI来削减成本的公司,最终会被那些用AI更好地服务客户的公司淘汰,不过他也承认这个趋势未来仍可能发生变化。[关于AI、招聘与杰文斯悖论](https://x.com/levie/status/2081930301752942703)

### Anthropic, official account

First, the company said it supports a petition — signed by its CEO and several co-founders and senior staff — calling for tools to deliberately pace the frontier of AI development, tying it back to its own recent research on recursive self-improvement. Second, working with academics at ETH Zurich, Tel Aviv University, and the University of Haifa, Anthropic released CryptanalysisBench, a new benchmark for studying LLMs' cryptanalysis abilities, alongside technical papers detailing model attacks on the HAWK and AES ciphers. ### OpenAI, official account

OpenAI echoed Anthropic's message the same day, stating it believes frontier AI acceleration may eventually reach a point where the world needs to deliberately pace the rate of development, and said it hopes to support work led by the U.S. government, other labs, and the open-source community to build the tools that make that possible. Separately, the company published research across eight case studies on how coding agents are reshaping scientific computing — taking on everything from routine maintenance to full system redesigns — while stressing that human verification, stewardship, and long-term ownership of the science still rest with researchers. [on pacing frontier AI development](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082208694142730340) · [coding agents in scientific computing](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082152075778293831)

OpenAI在同一天呼应了Anthropic的表态,称公司认为前沿AI的发展速度加快到某个阶段后,世界可能需要有意识地控制AI发展的节奏,并表示希望配合美国政府、其他实验室和开源社区,共同打造能实现这一目标的工具和机制。另外,公司还发布了一项涵盖八个案例研究的报告,探讨编程agent正如何重塑科学计算工作——从日常维护、针对性优化到完整的系统重构都能承担,但同时强调,科研问题的定义、结果的验证以及长期归属权,仍然要由研究者自己把关。[关于控制前沿AI发展节奏](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082208694142730340) · [编程agent在科学计算中的应用](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2082152075778293831)
