### Sebastian Raschka, Evangelist

ML and AI research engineer Sebastian Raschka, author of Build a Large Language Model From Scratch, posted the clearest technical walkthrough of the day on how Claude's watermarking is supposed to work, based on his read of the released materials. The core idea: when generating tokens, there are often multiple high-scoring candidates at a given position, and normally top-k or top-p sampling means the highest scorer usually wins but others can surface too. With watermarking, a secret key combined with the previous token decides which of the roughly equally high-scoring tokens gets picked. Repeat that across many positions and you get a pattern that is statistically very unlikely to occur by chance. His open question: the stated reason for applying it to everyone is EU regulation, but since this is an inference-time technique that needs no retraining and no separate model, why not scope it to EU users only? [how the watermarking actually works](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2088631263737364818)

ML与AI研究工程师、《Build a Large Language Model From Scratch》的作者Sebastian Raschka,贡献了这天最清楚的一篇技术拆解:基于公开材料,他讲了讲Claude的水印大致是怎么运作的。核心思路是,模型在生成token时,某些位置上往往会有好几个得分都很高的候选词;平常用top-k或top-p采样,得分最高的那个通常会被选中,但其他候选也有机会冒出来。加了水印之后,一个密钥再结合前一个token,来决定在这些得分几乎相当的候选里挑哪一个。把这套动作在大量位置上重复下去,就形成了一个从组合数学角度看极不可能自然出现的模式。他也留了个疑问:官方说法是因为欧盟监管所以必须对所有人开启,但这本身是一个推理阶段的技术,既不需要重新训练也不需要单独的模型,那为什么不只对欧盟用户启用呢?[水印到底是怎么运作的](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2088631263737364818)

### Amjad Masad, Replit CEO

Replit CEO Amjad Masad pushed back hard on the claim that AI structurally centralizes power because it is currently compute hungry, arguing that this ignores 125 years of super exponential growth in compute price-performance. Between algorithmic improvements and continued hardware efficiency gains, he sees no reason to assume AGI-level capabilities will always require a data center to run. His sharpest point is that scaling laws are not laws of physics, they are empirical relationships observed for particular architectures, objectives and datasets, and changing any one of those gives you a different curve. If the brain is any guide, true AGI will likely be very efficient, which means current scaling laws might be a bug rather than a feature, a measure of how inefficient the ML algorithms we have found so far really are. [scaling laws are not laws of physics](https://x.com/amasad/status/2088867492907327573)

Replit首席执行官Amjad Masad强硬反驳了"AI因为吃算力所以必然导致权力集中"这个说法,他认为这个论断忽略了过去125年里算力性价比的超指数级增长。考虑到算法改进加上硬件效率的持续提升,他看不出有什么理由认定AGI级别的能力永远都得靠数据中心才能跑起来。他最犀利的一点是:scaling law不是物理定律,它只是在特定架构、特定目标函数、特定数据集上观察到的经验关系,任何一个变量一变,曲线就是另一条。如果大脑可以作为参照,真正的AGI很可能是非常高效的。也就是说,现在的scaling law或许是个bug而不是feature,它衡量的其实是我们目前找到的这些ML算法有多低效。[scaling law不是物理定律](https://x.com/amasad/status/2088867492907327573)

### Nikunj Kothari, Partner at FPV Ventures

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari told a story that reframes bureaucratic pain as a moat. He sat with a stealth portfolio founder working in a deeply unsexy space, listening to them lament that critical contracts and partnerships, the ones that would bring credibility, revenue and network effects, were stuck at the one-yard line in a massively fragmented market. Champions, terms, everything was lined up, a pure win win, and yet it was blocked by summer scheduling and one random clause, potentially costing them their ambitious timelines. He started laughing, and when asked why, told the founder: do you know how long it would take anyone else to do the same thing you are doing? Even with the leverage and relationships they already have. His conclusion is that this is painful short term and a very strong moat long term. Unnecessary and 996 performative pain is not the moat, but building entrenched software and relationships that take a lot of pain absolutely is. [why the bureaucracy is the moat](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2088716743615352963)

FPV Ventures合伙人Nikunj Kothari讲了个故事,把"被官僚流程折磨"重新解读成了护城河。他和一位做隐身项目的被投创始人坐在一起,对方正在抱怨:那些关键的合同和合作,也就是能带来信誉、收入和网络效应的那些,在一个极度分散的市场里全都卡在了最后一码线上。支持者、条款、所有东西都谈妥了,是纯粹的双赢,结果就因为暑假档期加上一条莫名其妙的条款卡住,可能会拖垮他们本来很激进的时间表。他听着听着笑了出来,对方问他笑什么,他说:你知道换个人要花多久才能做成你现在正在做的事吗?而且这还是在你已经有这些资源和关系的前提下。他的结论是,这种痛苦短期难受,长期却是极强的护城河。没必要的、996式的表演型痛苦不是护城河,但那些需要熬很久才能扎下去的软件和关系,就是。[为什么繁琐流程本身就是护城河](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2088716743615352963)
