### Amanda Askell, Anthropic philosopher

Anthropic philosopher Amanda Askell pushed back on a popular framing of AI alignment, arguing there's no clean line separating "aligned" from "harmless": models, like humans, can act in aligned ways while still causing harm if they've been given false information about their situation. Her point: alignment and harmlessness are different axes, not one spectrum. [alignment vs. harmlessness](https://x.com/AmandaAskell/status/2084369056765989224)

Anthropic哲学家Amanda Askell对一种流行的AI对齐(alignment)解读提出了不同看法,她认为"对齐"和"无害"之间并不存在一条清晰的分界线:模型和人一样,即便在信息被误导的情况下依然表现出"对齐"的行为,也可能造成伤害。她的观点是:对齐和无害是两个不同的维度,而不是同一条光谱上的两端。[对齐与无害的区别](https://x.com/AmandaAskell/status/2084369056765989224)

### Peter Yang, AI educator

AI educator Peter Yang shared six takeaways from his interview with Nous Research co-founder Karan on getting the most out of the Hermes agent: personalization comes from an agent's accumulated memory and skills rather than the underlying model; the reflexive "you're absolutely right" is reward hacking, so Karan uses a fresh, context-free agent to independently evaluate another agent's work; and Nous's Hermes Curator automatically archives stale skills so a self-improving agent doesn't accumulate cruft over time. Karan's broader thesis: intelligence should be a public good, achievable by running Hermes locally with whatever model works best. [six takeaways on Hermes Agent](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084289426012897433) · [interview clip](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084330985689428290)

AI教育者Peter Yang分享了他采访Nous Research联合创始人Karan时得到的六点心得,主题是如何用好Hermes agent:个性化其实来自agent积累的记忆和技能,而不是底层模型本身;那种"你说得对"的反射式回应其实是一种reward hacking,所以Karan会用一个全新的、没有历史上下文的agent来独立评估另一个agent的工作成果;Nous的Hermes Curator还会自动把闲置的技能归档,避免自我进化的agent越积越乱。Karan更深一层的主张是:智能应该是一种公共产品,做法就是在本地运行Hermes,搭配任何效果最好的模型。[Hermes Agent六点心得](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084289426012897433) · [采访片段](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2084330985689428290)

### OpenAI, official account

OpenAI detailed GPT-Live, a new architecture and stack for realtime voice now powering ChatGPT Voice. Audio runs through a dedicated fast path while deeper reasoning and tool use happen asynchronously, so the model can keep listening while it speaks, and the team cut voice-session startup from six network round trips down to one. [GPT-Live architecture](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2084378415818579975) · [how it was built](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2084378418989379822)

OpenAI详细介绍了GPT-Live,这是一套全新的实时语音架构和技术栈,目前已经在为ChatGPT Voice提供支持。音频会走一条专用的快速通道,而更深层的推理和工具调用则异步进行,这样模型就能做到边说边听;团队还把语音会话的启动过程从原来的六次网络往返压缩到了一次。[GPT-Live架构](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2084378415818579975) · [技术实现细节](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2084378418989379822)
