### Nikunj Kothari, FPV Ventures partner

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari walked down the entire AI stack declaring no moat at every single layer: the models (OpenAI, Anthropic, XAI), the IDEs (Cursor, Windsurf), the harnesses (Cognition, Factory, LangChain), the app builders (Replit, Lovable, Bolt), the wrappers (Harvey, Abridge, OpenEvidence), the inference providers (Together, Fireworks, Groq), the voice layer (Sierra, Decagon, ElevenLabs), the data labeling companies (Scale, Surge, Mercor), the AI infrastructure (Baseten, Modal, Railway), the neoclouds (CoreWeave, Lambda, Crusoe) and the generative media companies (Runway, Higgsfield, Suno). His punchline: apparently nobody in AI has a moat, except the venture firm. [apparently nobody in AI has a moat](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2089486802356961364)

FPV Ventures合伙人Nikunj Kothari把整个AI技术栈从上到下捋了一遍,每一层都宣告"没有护城河":模型层(OpenAI、Anthropic、XAI)、IDE层(Cursor、Windsurf)、harness层(Cognition、Factory、LangChain)、应用搭建层(Replit、Lovable、Bolt)、套壳层(Harvey、Abridge、OpenEvidence)、推理服务商(Together、Fireworks、Groq)、语音层(Sierra、Decagon、ElevenLabs)、数据标注公司(Scale、Surge、Mercor)、AI基础设施(Baseten、Modal、Railway)、neocloud(CoreWeave、Lambda、Crusoe),以及生成式媒体公司(Runway、Higgsfield、Suno)。他的包袱在最后:看来AI行业里谁都没有护城河,除了风投机构自己。[看来AI行业里谁都没有护城河](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2089486802356961364)

His hottest take is that brand marketing is going to be the major differentiator and one of the most prized assets a company can have going forward. He is careful to say he does not mean launch videos or money poured into aesthetics to pump a valuation, because you still need retention before attention. He means actually thinking through what a company stands for and reading the vibe of the market, staying relentless on the long term goal while working out how to present the company in the short term, and doing that every single week. His argument is that if agents become the primary user for most products, then getting the attention of an increasingly attention deficit human audience will require standing out consistently. People in these roles have been handed tier 2 titles and passed over for CMO jobs many times over, and he expects that to finally invert in the next ten years, with these hires becoming cofounders or at least getting a real seat at the table. The design centric CEO usually fills this gap, but those are rare, so he says you need to learn to hire for it, and if you have one, hold them close. [brand marketing as the differentiator](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2089374392295842086)

他自认为最不合群的观点是:品牌营销将成为未来最主要的差异化因素,也是一家公司最值钱的资产之一。他特意澄清,自己说的不是发布会视频,也不是为了把估值抬上去而砸钱做"美学",因为留存永远要排在注意力前面。他指的是真正想清楚这家公司到底代表什么,读懂市场当下的"vibe",在长期目标上寸步不让的同时,琢磨短期里该怎么把公司呈现出去,而且每周都得做这件事。他的论证是:如果agent将成为大多数产品的主要用户,那么要抓住注意力越来越稀缺的人类受众,你就必须持续地让自己显得与众不同。做这类事情的人过去常年被安排在二线岗位,一次又一次与CMO失之交臂;他预计未来十年这个格局会彻底反转,这类人会被招进来当联合创始人,至少也能真正坐上牌桌。通常是设计出身的CEO在补这块短板,但这种CEO本来就稀有,所以他说你得学会怎么招到这样的人,而如果你手上已经有一个,一定要把人留住。[品牌营销会成为那个差异化因素](https://x.com/nikunj/status/2089374392295842086)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie says that when you hear data is the new oil, a large data sale is ultimately what that looks like in practice. AI has such a thirst for data that we are entering an era where data is valuable in almost any form, and in a world of AI, information genuinely belongs on the balance sheet as an asset. He expects the way companies manage and mine their own organizational intelligence to become one of the deciding factors for competitiveness and value creation. [information as a balance sheet asset](https://x.com/levie/status/2089499887905997272)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie说,人们常讲"数据是新石油",而一笔大规模的数据交易,就是这句话落到实处的样子。AI对数据的渴求之强,已经让我们进入了一个数据几乎以任何形态存在都有价值的时代;在AI的世界里,信息理应作为一项资产被写进资产负债表。他认为,一家公司如何管理和挖掘自己组织内部的智能,将成为决定其竞争力和价值创造能力的关键因素之一。[信息应该进资产负债表](https://x.com/levie/status/2089499887905997272)

Separately, he laid out what he thinks is a clean way to size up AI agents: you now have a technology that will tirelessly do all the things you could never fully do before, not because you did not want to or it was not valuable, but because it was simply not remotely practical. You could never find every security vulnerability in your codebase, because you could not simulate every risk vector. You could not read every contract or document, process the data and capture it for later querying. You could not comb through your entire customer base looking for every relevant upsell signal. The exercise, then, is to ask what you can now throw intelligence at that will not give up, and what the returns would be. He frames that as both a deployment guide and a startup map: go after markets where more compute on a problem qualitatively changes what customers can do in that domain. [where the agent opportunity actually is](https://x.com/levie/status/2089209131391729763)

另外他还给出了一套他觉得很干净的思路,用来判断AI agent的价值:你现在手上有一项技术,它会不知疲倦地去做那些你以前从来没能真正做完的事情,不是因为你不想做,也不是因为没价值,而是因为压根就不现实。你不可能找出代码库里的每一个安全漏洞,因为你没法把所有风险路径都模拟一遍。你不可能把每一份合同、每一份文档都读完,把数据处理好、存下来以备日后查询。你也不可能把整个客户群从头梳一遍,把每一个值得做增购的信号都挑出来。所以真正该问的是:现在有什么事情,是你可以扔给一个永不放弃的智能去做的,做完之后回报又有多大。他把这套思路同时当作落地指南和创业地图:去找那些"在问题上多堆算力就能质变客户能力"的市场。[agent的机会到底在哪](https://x.com/levie/status/2089209131391729763)

AI教育者Peter Yang发了一份完整的Grok Bot教程,他把它称为个人AI agent未来形态的一瞥。他手把手搭了五个还挺有用的bot:一个负责创建和管理其他bot的顾问、一个专门找爆款视频的YouTube研究员、一个负责挖出病毒式传播和搞笑推文的X侦察兵、一个帮你清理收件箱、砍掉付费订阅开销的数字版"近藤麻理惠",以及一个能帮你省下旅行开销的私人管家。他还试了一个游戏bot,看Grok Bot能不能装上并玩起Doom、红色警戒和Commander Keen这些经典老游戏,顺带聊了聊这东西普及路上最大的障碍。[值得一搭的五个bot](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2089401696946634801)

### Madhu Guru, Meta Sr. Director of AI

Meta Senior Director of AI Madhu Guru laid out how to actually get good at evals, and it is worth reading in full if you are building anything agentic. Take a workflow you know really well and figure out how to make its quality measurable. Study the actual traces: the sequence of prompts typical users have, what a good response looks like at each step and for the end to end outcome. Then study where your product fails and create traces that capture those failures, including messy tool call responses and missing context. Once you have a good eval, work out how to run it repeatedly and automatically, and after that, how to keep it mirroring live traffic as user patterns evolve. He offered to turn it into a daily series. [how to get good at evals](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2089480958571331623)

Meta的AI高级总监Madhu Guru讲了怎么真正把evals做好,如果你在做任何跟agent相关的东西,这段值得完整读一遍。方法是:挑一个你特别熟的工作流,想办法把它的质量变成可度量的。去研究真实的trace,也就是典型用户实际发出的prompt序列,以及每一步和整体端到端的结果里,什么样才算好的回应。然后研究你的产品在哪里出问题,把这些失败场景做成trace,包括乱七八糟的工具调用返回、上下文缺失这类情况。有了一套好的eval之后,再去想怎么让它能反复、自动地跑起来;再往后,想怎么保证随着用户行为的演变,这套eval始终能反映线上的真实流量。他还问要不要把这个做成每天更新的系列。[怎么把evals做好](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2089480958571331623)
