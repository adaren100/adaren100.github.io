### OpenAI, AI Lab

### Anthropic, AI Safety and Research Company

Anthropic ran the experiment that sits at the center of every scalable oversight debate: can a model align a model stronger than itself? As a first test, Sonnet 5 post-trained an early checkpoint of Opus 4.8, a more capable model, and the result reached safety scores approaching those of production Opus 4.8, which had gone through the full alignment training pipeline. Across 10 alignment failures, Claude reliably improved safety scores without degrading capabilities, and its best methods generalized to benchmarks it had not optimized against, to the Petri behavioral audit, and to models up to 4.7x larger. The honest caveat is the part worth keeping: Claude can reliably fix measurable misalignment, but subtle or rare failures may have no benchmark at all, so everything hinges on measuring the right things. Anthropic is releasing the automated alignment research setup for others to build on. [can a model align its stronger successors](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093386533638389907) · [results across 10 alignment failures](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093386531247718425) · [releasing the setup, and what it cannot measure](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093386535618113627)

Anthropic做了一个直击"可扩展监督"这场辩论核心的实验:一个模型能不能对齐比它更强的模型?作为第一次尝试,他们让Sonnet 5去post-train能力更强的Opus 4.8的一个早期checkpoint,结果这个版本的安全评分,已经接近走完了完整对齐训练流程的正式版Opus 4.8。在10类对齐失效场景上,Claude都稳定提升了安全评分,同时没有牺牲能力;而且它找到的最优方法还能泛化到它没有针对性优化过的基准、泛化到Petri行为审计,甚至泛化到参数量大4.7倍的模型上。真正值得记住的是那句实话:Claude能可靠地修复"可被度量"的对齐问题,但那些细微或罕见的失效,可能压根就没有对应的基准,所以一切都取决于你有没有在度量正确的东西。Anthropic把这套自动化对齐研究的框架开放了出来,供其他人在上面继续做。[模型能对齐比自己更强的后继者吗](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093386533638389907) · [10类对齐失效上的结果](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093386531247718425) · [开放框架,以及它测不到什么](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2093386535618113627)

### Guillermo Rauch, Vercel CEO

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch laid out the most structurally interesting take of the day: the web is splitting into two extremes, and the middle is going to disappear. On one end, ultra fancy human experiences built for delight, exploration, brand, and entertainment, enabled by threejs and typegpu and the WebGPU stack. On the other, agent centric content, data, and APIs, which he describes as pure lossless signal for machines, with the rise of markdown and MCP as the tip of that iceberg. Everything in between gets subsumed by agents producing just in time UI, and his closing line is the one to sit with: think of the agent as the new browser. He backed the MCP half of that thesis with numbers, pointing at npm downloads for mcp-handler as evidence the growth is not just Vercel's own MCP. He also made the ownership argument for eve, noting that plenty of easy ways to create agents exist, but few give you a Git repo where you actually own the entire intelligence stack, runtime, model choice, skills, tools, connectivity, and sandbox included. [the web splitting into two extremes](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2093482695838007318) · [MCP growth beyond Vercel](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2093463771071336497) · [owning the whole intelligence stack](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2093387887668814214)

Vercel首席执行官Guillermo Rauch给出了今天在结构上最值得琢磨的判断:Web正在向两个极端分裂,而中间地带会消失。一端是极尽华丽的人类体验,服务于愉悦、探索、品牌和娱乐,由threejs、typegpu和整套WebGPU能力托起;另一端是以agent为中心的内容、数据和API,他形容为"给机器的纯粹无损信号",而markdown和MCP的兴起只是这座冰山的一角。夹在中间的那一层,会被"agent即时生成UI"吃掉:当你需要的只是功利性的内容或数据,它会在你需要的那一刻被现场生成。他最后那句话值得停一下:把agent当作新的浏览器来理解。关于MCP这半个论点,他用数据做了支撑,拿mcp-handler这个npm包的下载量说明,增长并不只发生在Vercel自家的MCP上。他还为eve讲了"所有权"这个卖点:市面上创建agent的"简单方法"很多,但有几个能给你一个Git仓库,让你真正拥有整条智能栈,包括运行时、模型选择、skills、工具、连接能力和sandbox。[Web正在裂成两个极端](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2093482695838007318) · [MCP的增长不止于Vercel](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2093463771071336497) · [拥有完整的智能栈](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2093387887668814214)

### Aaron Levie, Box CEO

Box CEO Aaron Levie read this week's tech earnings calls as a reminder that software and AI need each other, and his argument is the clearest rebuttal going to the idea that agents will hollow out SaaS. Software provides the guardrails: how data is managed, how business logic in workflows is maintained, how access is governed, how data is protected, and you want those systems to behave identically every time without fail. Agents work inside those systems on the same tasks people used to do, but at a scale far greater than people ever did, which is exactly why deterministic controls matter more now rather than less. His conclusion is that many of the best places to deploy agents are directly within the software platforms themselves, from Salesforce to Box to Harvey to ServiceNow, because those platforms can tune agents to their own workflows and domains with vertical specific evals and context, and connect to other agents headlessly. The net result he predicts is software adoption and AI adoption rising together, growing the IT TAM dramatically over time. [software and agents grow together](https://x.com/levie/status/2093192697331011846)

Box首席执行官Aaron Levie把本周的科技公司财报电话会,读成了一个提醒:软件和AI互为必需。他这套论证,是目前对"agent会掏空SaaS"这一说法最清楚的反驳。软件提供的是护栏:数据怎么管、工作流里的业务逻辑怎么维护、访问权限怎么治理、数据怎么保护,而你要求这些系统每一次的行为都完全一致,不容出错。agent则在这些系统内部干活,做的还是过去由人来做的那些任务,但规模远超人力所能及,这恰恰意味着确定性的控制比以前更重要,而不是更不重要。他的结论是:部署agent最好的位置,很多时候就在软件平台内部,从Salesforce到Box、Harvey、ServiceNow都是如此,因为这些平台能用垂直领域独有的评测集和上下文,把agent调到贴合自己的工作流和场景,还能以无界面的方式与其他agent对接。他预测的净结果是,软件采用率和AI采用率会一起往上走,长期把整个IT市场的盘子做大。[软件和agent是一起长大的](https://x.com/levie/status/2093192697331011846)

### Peter Yang, AI Educator

AI educator Peter Yang offered the sharpest product critique of the day, arguing that Claude Cowork and ChatGPT Work are partial solutions and that Grok Bot is closer to the right end state for a capable AI agent product aimed at non technical people. His reasoning is a usability argument, not a capability one: people can easily grok that Grok Bot is a computer that runs in the cloud. The test he proposes is brutal and fair, which is to ask a normal person to explain the difference between ChatGPT Work and Codex, where one can run cloud tasks and the other cannot, or to explain how Claude Cowork actually works. His bet is that almost nobody can, and 97 replies suggest he hit something. [why Grok Bot is the right end state](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093379695144530313)

AI教育者Peter Yang给出了今天最锋利的产品批评:他认为Claude Cowork和ChatGPT Work都只是半成品,而Grok Bot更接近"面向非技术用户的强大AI agent产品"应有的终局形态。他的理由是可用性,不是能力:普通人能很轻松地理解Grok Bot就是一台跑在云上的电脑。他提出的检验方式又狠又公道:随便找个普通人,让他解释ChatGPT Work和Codex的区别在哪(一个能跑云端任务,另一个不能),或者解释Claude Cowork到底是怎么运作的。他赌几乎没人答得上来,而97条回复说明他确实戳到了什么。[为什么说Grok Bot才是终局形态](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2093379695144530313)
