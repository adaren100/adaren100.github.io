### Anthropic, AI Safety and Research Company

The biggest post of the day by a wide margin: Anthropic published what it calls its most detailed threat intelligence report to date. It covers how people tried to misuse Claude for cyberattacks, influence operations, surveillance, biology, and building weapons, and how Anthropic found and stopped them. The company says it disrupted every operation in the report, used the lessons to strengthen its safeguards, and where appropriate shared findings with authorities and other AI companies. It is explicit that these cases are not typical. They were picked as some of the most sophisticated misuse seen so far, because they show where AI misuse is headed, where safeguards work, and where they need to improve. The stated goal is to help others spot the same activity on their own platforms. Over 12,800 likes and nearly 2,400 retweets. [threat intelligence report](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2098097512544444447)

今天热度最高的一条,而且遥遥领先:Anthropic发布了他们称为"迄今最详细"的威胁情报报告。报告讲的是有人如何试图滥用Claude来做网络攻击、舆论操控、监控、生物相关研究以及制造武器,以及Anthropic是怎么发现并阻止这些行为的。公司表示,报告里提到的每一起行动都已被瓦解,相关经验也被用来加固安全防护,必要时还把发现同步给了执法部门和其他AI公司。他们也说得很清楚,这些案例并不典型,挑出来的是目前见过的最复杂的滥用手法,因为它们最能说明AI滥用的走向:哪些防护有效,哪些还需要改进。发布的目的,是让其他平台也能识别出类似的活动。这条帖子拿到了12800多个赞和将近2400次转发。[threat intelligence report](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2098097512544444447)

### Madhu Guru, Sr Director of AI at Meta

Meta AI Sr Director Madhu Guru, formerly leading Gemini, Veo, and Nano Banana at Google, posted part 10 of his series on building great evals: measure the steps, not just the result. Two agent trajectories can reach the same answer, but one searches the right sources, retrieves the right document, and makes 4 clean tool calls, while the other makes 17 calls, repeats the same search 3 times, and recovers from 2 errors. His checklist: define the whole workflow, define the tasks in each step, decide how to measure each step (separate evals or a slice of a bigger one), and reflect your median and hard tasks in the evals. When reading results, study the steps first and the final answer second. [How to build great evals, part 10](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098064969464217720)

Meta AI高级总监Madhu Guru(之前在Google负责Gemini、Veo和Nano Banana)发了"如何构建好的eval"系列的第10篇:要衡量过程,而不只是结果。两条agent轨迹可能得出同一个答案,但一条搜对了来源、找对了文档、干净利落地调了4次工具;另一条调了17次,同一个东西搜了3遍,中间还从2个错误里恢复过来。他给的清单是:把整个工作流定义清楚,定义每一步的任务,想清楚每一步怎么衡量(单独做eval还是作为大eval的一个切片),并且让eval覆盖中等难度和高难度的任务。看eval结果时,先看过程,再看最终结果。[How to build great evals, part 10](https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098064969464217720)
