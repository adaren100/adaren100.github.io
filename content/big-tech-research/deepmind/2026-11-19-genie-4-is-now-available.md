**[Genie 4 is now available](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-is-now-available/)** — _Google DeepMind · Nov 19_

**Main takeaway:** A product rollout: Genie 4, DeepMind's most realistic and interactive world model yet, is now available to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers inside a rebuilt Project Genie, where you can edit a generated world mid-session by typing a prompt and hear real-time native audio that adapts as the scene unfolds. Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist.

**Main methods:**
- **Mid-session prompting instead of regeneration.** You type a new prompt while navigating the world you already made — shift the weather, drop in a character, redirect the action — rather than generating a fresh world from scratch.
- **Native audio in the loop.** Genie 4 is the first in the line to pair its visuals with real-time native audio that tracks the scene, from ambient soundscapes through to character dialogue.
- **Genie 3 stays in the model picker for length.** Genie 4 is the high-fidelity option; Genie 3 remains selectable when you want longer runs or panorama-seeded spaces, and got its own update today — up to 3 minutes per generation (from 60 seconds), world chaining to link multiple worlds into a longer journey, 360-degree panorama image grounding, and real-time in-world events.
- **A separate agent does the building.** The new Project Genie agent constructs complex environments from plain conversation, so composing a world is a dialogue rather than prompt engineering.
- **Community gallery for discovery and remixing.** Creations can be published for others to explore and remix, which is also how DeepMind gets a stream of real user worlds out of the prototype.
- **Driven by tester feedback.** DeepMind says early testers of the original Project Genie prototype asked for deeper control over what happens inside the worlds and easier ways to build and share them — the three things this release targets.

**[Genie 4 开放，世界可以边逛边改](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-is-now-available/)** — _Google DeepMind · 11月19日_

**Main takeaway:** 一次产品开放。Genie 4 是 DeepMind 到目前为止最真实、交互性最强的 world model，现在符合条件的 Google AI Ultra 用户能在改版后的 Project Genie 里用上：世界生成出来之后你还能边逛边打字改它，而且配了会跟着场景走的实时原生音频。Google AI Pro 的用户可以先排 waitlist。

**Main methods:**
- **改世界不用重新生成。** 你在自己已经生成的世界里走着，随手敲一句新 prompt 就行，换天气、丢进一个角色、把剧情拐个方向，不需要从零再生成一个。
- **音频这次是实时跟着画面的。** Genie 4 是这条线上第一个把画面和实时原生音频配起来的，从环境音一直到角色对话，都会随着场景变化。
- **想跑长一点还是留着 Genie 3。** Genie 4 走的是画质和物理最高那档，Genie 3 继续留在 model picker 里，专门应对更长的时长或者用全景图起手的空间；今天它也顺手更新了：单次生成从 60 秒拉到 3 分钟，多个世界可以串起来连成一段更长的旅程，支持 360 度全景图 grounding，还能实时触发世界内事件。
- **搭世界的活交给另一个 agent。** 新的 Project Genie agent 能靠纯对话把复杂环境搭出来，所以做世界更像聊天，不太需要琢磨怎么写 prompt。
- **加了社区 gallery。** 做好的世界可以发出去让别人逛、让别人 remix；对 DeepMind 来说，这也等于从这个原型里持续拿到真实用户造的世界。
- **这波更新是测试用户提的。** DeepMind 说第一版 Project Genie 的早期测试者反馈集中在两点，想对世界里发生的事有更深的控制，以及想更容易地搭和分享，这次发布基本就是冲着这些来的。
