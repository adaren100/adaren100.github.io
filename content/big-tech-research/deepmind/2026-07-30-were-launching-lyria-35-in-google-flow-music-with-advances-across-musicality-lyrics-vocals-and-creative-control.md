**[We're launching Lyria 3.5 in Google Flow Music, with advances across musicality, lyrics, vocals, and creative control](https://deepmind.google/blog/were-launching-lyria-35-in-google-flow-music-with-advances-across-musicality-lyrics-vocals-and-creative-control/)** — _Google DeepMind · Jul 29_

**Main takeaway:** Google DeepMind is rolling out Lyria 3.5, its newest music generation model, into Google Flow Music today, with gains in musicality, lyric quality, and vocal realism.

**Main methods:**
- **Improved musicality.** The model produces richer, more complex melodic structures that sound more natural than prior versions.
- **Enhanced lyrics.** Lyric generation has better prompt adherence and structural awareness, so generated lyrics track the requested theme and song structure more closely.
- **Improved vocals.** Vocals carry more expression and emotional nuance, with better pronunciation, aiming for more realistic-sounding singing.
- **Creative control.** Users get easier control over tempo and output duration when crafting tracks.

**[我们在 Google Flow Music 里上线了 Lyria 3.5，旋律、歌词、人声和创作控制都有提升](https://deepmind.google/blog/were-launching-lyria-35-in-google-flow-music-with-advances-across-musicality-lyrics-vocals-and-creative-control/)** — _Google DeepMind · 7月29日_

**Main takeaway:** Google DeepMind 把最新的音乐生成模型 Lyria 3.5 直接放进了 Google Flow Music，这次在旋律、歌词质量和人声表现上都往前走了一步。

**Main methods:**
- **旋律更丰富了。** 能写出更复杂、更自然的旋律结构，听感上更接近真实作曲。
- **歌词质量更好。** 生成的歌词跟 prompt 贴合度更高，对歌曲结构（主歌、副歌等）的把握也更准。
- **人声更有感情。** 唱出来的声音表现力和情绪层次更丰富，发音也更准，整体更接近真人演唱。
- **创作控制更顺手。** 用户可以更方便地调节曲子的速度（tempo）和时长。
