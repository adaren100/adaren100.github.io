**[Accelerating the frontiers of scientific discovery: Google's $40M commitment to the Genesis Mission](https://deepmind.google/blog/accelerating-the-frontiers-of-scientific-discovery-googles-40m-commitment-to-the-genesis-mission/)** — _Google DeepMind · Jul 22_

**Main takeaway:** Google DeepMind is committing $40 million in AI tokens and cloud credits to the U.S. Department of Energy's Genesis Mission, giving National Laboratory researchers in-kind access to its frontier AI-for-science portfolio plus a year of Gemini for Government for tens of thousands of DOE staff.

**Main methods:**
- **Five-model science portfolio.** AlphaEvolve (Gemini-powered coding/discovery agent for algorithm design), AlphaFold 3 (protein and biomolecule structure prediction), AlphaGenome (non-coding DNA variation effects), WeatherNext (AI weather forecasting), and AlphaEarth Foundations (planetary mapping) go to Genesis Mission awardees in-kind.
- **Gemini for Government at scale.** One year of seats and tokens for tens of thousands of users spanning operations, research, and management across all 17 DOE National Laboratories.
- **Early deployment example.** PNNL scientist Dr. Henry Kvinge is using AlphaEvolve to search massive combinatorics and math systems, automatically surfacing hidden connections that would normally take researchers years to find by hand.
- **Builds on prior commitments.** Follows Google's December pledge to the Genesis Mission and an earlier early-access program that already gave all 17 DOE labs AI-for-science tools.
- **Framed as national strategy.** Positioned as supporting the White House's goal to double the pace of American scientific discovery within a decade.

**[Google 再砸 4000 万美元，给 Genesis Mission 加速科学发现](https://deepmind.google/blog/accelerating-the-frontiers-of-scientific-discovery-googles-40m-commitment-to-the-genesis-mission/)** — _Google DeepMind · 7月22日_

**Main takeaway:** Google DeepMind 向美国能源部的 Genesis Mission 追加投入 4000 万美元的 AI token 和云资源，把 AlphaEvolve、AlphaFold 3、AlphaGenome、WeatherNext、AlphaEarth 这套前沿科学 AI 组合开放给国家实验室的研究者，还给几万名 DOE 员工配了一年的 Gemini for Government。

**Main methods:**
- **五件套科学模型。** AlphaEvolve（Gemini 驱动的算法设计 agent）、AlphaFold 3（蛋白质和生物分子结构预测）、AlphaGenome（非编码 DNA 变异效应）、WeatherNext（AI 天气预报）、AlphaEarth Foundations（地球测绘），全部以实物形式提供给 Genesis Mission 的资助对象。
- **Gemini for Government 大规模铺开。** 覆盖全部 17 个 DOE 国家实验室的运营、研究和管理团队，几万名用户拿到一年的席位和 token。
- **已经有实际用例。** PNNL 的科学家 Henry Kvinge 用 AlphaEvolve 搜索庞大的组合数学系统，自动挖出人手很难发现的隐藏关联，原本要花好几年才能找到的东西。
- **不是突然冒出来的。** 是去年 12 月 Google 对 Genesis Mission 承诺的延续，此前已经给全部 17 个 DOE 实验室提供过 AI-for-science 工具的早期访问。
- **定位是国家战略。** 官方说法是配合白宫目标，把美国科学发现的速度在十年内翻一倍。
