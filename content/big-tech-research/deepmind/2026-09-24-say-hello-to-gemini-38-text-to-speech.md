**[Gemini 3.8 text-to-speech says hello](https://deepmind.google/blog/say-hello-to-gemini-38-text-to-speech/)** — _Google DeepMind · Sep 24_

**Main takeaway:** Google DeepMind released two new TTS models, Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS, which it calls its most expressive audio generation models yet — they turn voice generation from a fixed set of 30 presets into a prompt-driven voice studio with 2,000+ production-ready voices.

**Main methods:**
- **Generative voice design.** Flash TTS builds bespoke voices from scratch through natural-language prompts that set role, accent, and voice characteristics across 100+ languages and dialects — a Melbourne DJ, a tinny monotone robot, a Japanese dragon.
- **Line-by-line direction.** You can direct a performance line by line with granular control over acting cues, pacing, dialect shifts, and backchanneling, plus realistic conversational sounds.
- **Two tiers.** Flash TTS targets deep creative direction and character design; Flash-Lite TTS is optimized for high-volume, cost-efficient work like dubbing, bulk audio content, and expressive voice agents.
- **Expansive voice library.** 2,000+ production-ready voices with broad language coverage, including regional varieties such as Mexican Spanish and Quebec French.
- **Distribution.** Available across Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini Enterprise, Gemini Notebook, and Google Vids; target use cases are audiobooks, podcasts, and real-time voice agents at scale.
- **Safety.** Built-in watermarking ships with the models. They join a fast-growing Gemini Audio family that already includes 3.5 Live Translate, 3.5 Transcribe, 3.8 Live, and 3.8 Live Extended Thinking.

**[Gemini 3.8 TTS 上线，声音可以自己"捏"了](https://deepmind.google/blog/say-hello-to-gemini-38-text-to-speech/)** — _Google DeepMind · 9月24日_

**Main takeaway:** Google DeepMind 放出两个新的 TTS 模型，Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS 和 Gemini 3.8 Flash-Lite TTS，他们说这是目前表现力最强的音频生成模型。以前只有 30 个固定预设音色，现在直接变成一个拿 prompt 就能捏声音的录音棚，库里还有 2,000 多个能直接上生产的声音。

**Main methods:**
- **用自然语言捏声音。** Flash TTS 可以从零生成音色，用 prompt 描述角色、口音、嗓音特征就行，覆盖 100 多种语言和方言，官方演示里有墨尔本的 DJ、金属音的单调机器人，还有一条日本龙。
- **逐行调表演。** 支持一行一行地给演出指令，节奏、情绪、口音切换、backchanneling 这些都能细调，连聊天时那种自然的语气音都能做出来。
- **两档分工明确。** Flash TTS 偏创作和角色设计，Flash-Lite TTS 主打量大又便宜的场景，比如批量配音、内容生产和有表现力的 voice agent。
- **现成音色库够用。** 2,000 多个能直接用的声音，语言覆盖也广，包括墨西哥西班牙语、魁北克法语这类地区变体。
- **哪里能用上。** Google AI Studio、Gemini API、Gemini Enterprise、Gemini Notebook 和 Google Vids 都接了，主要瞄准有声书、播客和大规模实时语音 agent。
- **安全和家族定位。** 自带 watermarking。这两个模型接在 Gemini Audio 家族后面，前面已经有 3.5 Live Translate、3.5 Transcribe、3.8 Live 和 3.8 Live Extended Thinking。
