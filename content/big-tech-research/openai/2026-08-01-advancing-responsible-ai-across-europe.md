**[Advancing responsible AI across Europe](https://openai.com/index/advancing-responsible-ai-across-europe)** — _OpenAI · Aug 1_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI lays out how its safety and governance practices — the Preparedness Framework, Frontier Governance Framework, system cards, and Red Teaming Network — line up with the EU AI Act's General-Purpose AI Code of Practice as the Act's next phase takes effect.

**Main methods:**
- **Endorsed two EU Codes of Practice.** Signed onto the GPAI Code of Practice and the Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content, both developed through multi-stakeholder processes.
- **Preparedness Framework.** In place since 2023 and updated in 2025, it sets out how OpenAI identifies, evaluates, and manages serious risks from advanced AI systems.
- **Frontier Governance Framework.** Builds on the Preparedness Framework to explicitly map OpenAI's safety and security practices onto emerging legal requirements, including the EU AI Act's GPAI Code.
- **External validation channels.** Publishes system cards with major releases, opens models to outside testers through its Red Teaming Network, and maintains a public Model Spec describing intended model behavior.
- **Cross-industry collaboration.** Works through the Frontier Model Forum and with government bodies like US CAISI and UK AISI on third-party evaluation practices.
- **Framed as ongoing.** Positions this as continuing work as EU AI Act obligations phase in, not a completed compliance milestone.

**[OpenAI 在欧洲的负责任 AI 实践](https://openai.com/index/advancing-responsible-ai-across-europe)** — _OpenAI · 8月1日_

**Main takeaway:** 这篇讲的是 OpenAI 的安全治理体系——Preparedness Framework、Frontier Governance Framework、system card、Red Teaming Network——怎么对上欧盟 AI Act 里的 General-Purpose AI Code of Practice，正好赶上 AI Act 进入下一阶段落地。

**Main methods:**
- **签了两份欧盟行为准则。** 加入了 GPAI Code of Practice 和 AI 生成内容透明度的 Code of Practice，这两份都是多方共同讨论出来的。
- **Preparedness Framework。** 2023 年就有了，2025 年更新过，专门定义怎么识别、评估、管控先进 AI 系统带来的严重风险。
- **Frontier Governance Framework。** 在 Preparedness Framework 基础上再往前一步，明确把安全安保实践和欧盟 AI Act 这类新出台的法规要求对应起来。
- **对外验证的几条渠道。** 每次大版本发布都出 system card，通过 Red Teaming Network 让外部测试者参与进来，还维护了一份公开的 Model Spec 说明模型该有的行为边界。
- **跨行业协作。** 通过 Frontier Model Forum，也和美国 CAISI、英国 AISI 这些政府机构一起推进第三方评估的做法。
- **强调是持续的工作。** OpenAI 把这个定位成 AI Act 逐步落地过程中的持续投入，而不是"合规已完成"的收尾动作。
