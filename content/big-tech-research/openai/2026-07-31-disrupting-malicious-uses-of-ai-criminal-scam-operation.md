**[Disrupting a Criminal Scam Operation](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-criminal-scam-operation)** — _OpenAI · Jul 31_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI disrupted a Cambodia-based scam network, likely operating around Poipet, that used ChatGPT to run investment, romance, gambling, and law-enforcement-impersonation scams — and found signs some of the operators were themselves trafficking victims.

**Main methods:**
- **Lead from an industry peer.** Investigation started from a tip from WhatsApp, followed by sharing threat signals with other industry partners and relevant authorities.
- **Multi-scam-type network.** The same accounts blended tactics — dating personas to build trust, then pivots into fake crypto/gold investment pitches, fake gambling bonuses, or fake law-enforcement fine demands.
- **Model uses identified.** ChatGPT was used to generate and translate scam messages, create fake personas and promotional content, forge documents (passports, legal notices, stock confirmations), and handle internal admin work.
- **Trafficking signal.** Some accounts also produced content suggesting workers were held under debt bondage or coercion, consistent with public reporting on Southeast Asian scam compounds — OpenAI notes the people running scams can themselves be exploitation victims.
- **Response.** Banned the coordinated network of accounts tied to the operation.

**[打掉一个犯罪诈骗团伙](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-malicious-uses-of-ai-criminal-scam-operation)** — _OpenAI · 7月31日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 端掉了一个总部大概率在柬埔寨、活动范围很可能在波贝一带的诈骗网络，他们用 ChatGPT 支撑投资诈骗、杀猪盘、赌博诈骗和冒充执法人员这几种骗局，调查中还发现部分操作者本身可能也是人口贩运的受害者。

**Main methods:**
- **线索来自同行。** 这次调查是从 WhatsApp 那边给的一条线索开始的，之后 OpenAI 又把发现的威胁信号分享给了其他行业伙伴和相关执法部门。
- **一个团伙玩好几种骗局。** 同一批账号先用交友人设培养信任感，接着切换成虚假的加密货币或黄金投资机会，或者冒充赌博平台发假奖金，又或者冒充执法机构要求"缴罚款"。
- **ChatGPT 被用来干什么。** 生成和翻译诈骗话术、编造虚假人设和推广内容、伪造护照/法律通知/股票确认单等文件，还用来处理内部的日常行政工作。
- **人口贩运的迹象。** 部分账号生成的内容显示相关人员可能被债务奴役或胁迫控制，这跟公开报道里东南亚诈骗园区的情况对得上——OpenAI 特别指出，这些实施诈骗的人自己也可能是被剥削的受害者。
- **处理结果。** OpenAI 封禁了这个协同作案的账号网络。
