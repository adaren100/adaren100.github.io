**[Advancing the price-performance frontier with GPT-5.6](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6)** — _OpenAI · Jul 30_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI cut GPT‑5.6 Luna pricing 80% and Terra 20%, and introduced a Fast mode (up to 2.5× Sol's standard speed at 2× the price) that replaces Priority Processing, positioning Luna as a cheap, capable workhorse for agentic workloads.

**Main methods:**
- **New pricing.** Luna is 80% cheaper, Terra 20% cheaper; the cuts also flow through to how usage is counted against Codex and ChatGPT Work subscriptions.
- **Fast mode replaces Priority Processing.** For Sol, delivers up to 2.5× standard speed at 2× the price with no change in intelligence; it's backward compatible with requests previously tagged "priority."
- **Customer-reported results.** Notion says Terra matches GPT‑5.5 quality at half the cost and in 60% less time; Blitzy reports Luna handles 2.2× more context with 8.5× fewer output tokens at 87% lower cost than GPT‑5.4 mini, and lifted its prompt-cache reuse from 24% to 90%.
- **Positioned for agentic workloads.** Ramp says Luna is now the default model for its background coding-agent automations; Replit's president calls it "the closest we've come to intelligence too cheap to meter."

**[GPT-5.6 把性价比前沿又往前推了一步](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-price-performance-frontier-with-gpt-5-6)** — _OpenAI · 7月30日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 把 GPT‑5.6 Luna 降价 80%、Terra 降价 20%，还推出了取代 Priority Processing 的 Fast mode，主打让 Luna 成为跑 agent 任务又便宜又能打的主力模型。

**Main methods:**
- **新定价。** Luna 便宜了 80%，Terra 便宜了 20%；这个降价也会同步反映在 Codex 和 ChatGPT Work 订阅的用量计费里。
- **Fast mode 取代 Priority Processing。** 针对 Sol，速度能到 Standard 模式的 2.5 倍，价格是 2 倍，智能水平不变；之前打了 priority 标签的请求会自动切到 Fast mode，向后兼容。
- **客户实测反馈。** Notion 说 Terra 效果跟 GPT‑5.5 差不多，但成本只要一半，耗时少 60%；Blitzy 说 Luna 处理的上下文多了 2.2 倍，output token 却少了 8.5 倍，比 GPT‑5.4 mini 便宜 87%，prompt cache 命中率也从 24% 提到了 90%。
- **主打 agentic 场景。** Ramp 说 Luna 现在是他们后台代码 agent 自动化任务的默认模型；Replit 的总裁直接说这是"我们见过最接近'智能便宜到可以忽略计价'的模型"。
