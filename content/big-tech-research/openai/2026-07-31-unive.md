**[Univé builds an AI-ready workforce](https://openai.com/index/unive)** — _OpenAI · Jul 31_

**Main takeaway:** Case study on Univé, a large Dutch cooperative insurer, rolling out ChatGPT Enterprise across its workforce by pairing leadership-driven change management with governance-by-design — resulting in employee-built custom GPTs at scale and pet insurance claims now prepared for decision in minutes instead of hours.

**Main methods:**
- **Leadership-first rollout.** Ran dedicated AI leadership sessions for the entire management team focused on rethinking how work changes, rather than product demos.
- **Governance designed in from day one.** Built enterprise authentication, connector permission inheritance tied to existing access rights, privacy assessments, and continuous monitoring into the deployment before scaling adoption.
- **Employee-led experimentation.** Gave employees dedicated time and permission to rethink their own work and build custom GPTs, without requiring a formal business case for every idea.
- **Concrete result.** Pet insurance claims that used to take hours to prepare for a decision now take minutes.

**[Univé 打造 AI-ready 的员工队伍](https://openai.com/index/unive)** — _OpenAI · 7月31日_

**Main takeaway:** 这是荷兰大型合作制保险公司 Univé 的案例：靠"领导层带头 + 治理先行"的方式在全公司推开 ChatGPT Enterprise，结果是员工自己动手做了一大批 custom GPT，宠物保险理赔从原来要几个小时缩短到几分钟就能出决定。

**Main methods:**
- **领导层先动起来。** 没把这事当 IT 项目推，而是专门给整个管理层办了 AI 领导力研讨会，重点不是演示产品，而是逼着管理者重新想清楚工作方式会怎么变。
- **治理从一开始就设计进去。** 企业级身份认证、connector 权限继承（跟着员工原有权限走）、隐私评估、持续监控，这些都是部署一开始就搭好的，不是后补的。
- **员工自己带动起来。** 给员工专门的时间和授权，不用为每个小点子写正式立项报告，就能自己动手改造工作流程、做 custom GPT。
- **实打实的结果。** 宠物保险理赔以前要人工审几个小时，现在几分钟就能整理好交给人做决定。
