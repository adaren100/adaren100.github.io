**[Building abundant intelligence](https://openai.com/index/building-abundant-intelligence)** — _OpenAI · Aug 1_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI frames its "abundant intelligence" strategy — cutting GPT‑5.6 Luna price 80% and Terra 20% — as part of a full-stack cycle where cheaper, better models expand what work is worth automating, which in turn funds further compute and research investment.

**Main methods:**
- **New pricing.** GPT‑5.6 Luna now costs $0.20/M input and $1.20/M output tokens (down 80%); Terra costs $2/$12 per M tokens (down 20%); GPT‑5.6 Sol's Fast mode gives up to 2.5× the speed of Standard processing at 2× the price, with no change in intelligence.
- **"Cost of a successful outcome" framing.** Argues the right comparison isn't raw token price but the total cost to reach a correct outcome, including retries and human oversight — a stronger, pricier model can end up cheaper than a weak one that needs redoing.
- **Self-improving infrastructure.** GPT‑5.6 Sol helped OpenAI's own engineers optimize the software that serves its models, cutting end-to-end serving costs 20%, and improved speculative decoding for 15%+ faster token generation.
- **Full-stack thesis.** Argues efficiency gains require owning the whole stack — models, serving infrastructure, hardware strategy — rather than just building more data centers.

**[打造"充裕智能"](https://openai.com/index/building-abundant-intelligence)** — _OpenAI · 8月1日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 把这次 GPT‑5.6 Luna 降价 80%、Terra 降价 20% 的操作，包装成了一个全栈闭环的战略故事：模型更便宜更强，就有更多工作值得交给 AI 做，进而带来更多收入去反哺下一代模型和基础设施的研发。

**Main methods:**
- **新定价。** GPT‑5.6 Luna 现在每百万 input token 0.2 美元、output 1.2 美元（降了 80%）；Terra 是 2 美元和 12 美元（降了 20%）；GPT‑5.6 Sol 的 Fast mode 速度能到 Standard 的 2.5 倍，价格是 2 倍，智能水平不变。
- **强调"成功结果的成本"而不是单价。** 认为该比的不是 token 单价，而是拿到正确结果的总成本，包括重试和人工介入——一个更强但更贵的模型，只要能一次做对，反而可能比便宜模型返工几次更划算。
- **模型自己帮着降本增效。** GPT‑5.6 Sol 参与优化了 OpenAI 自家的模型 serving 软件，把端到端服务成本压低了 20%，还改进了 speculative decoding，把 token 生成效率提了 15% 以上。
- **全栈打法。** OpenAI 的说法是效率提升靠的是把模型、serving 基础设施、硬件策略整套一起抓，而不只是多建几个数据中心。
