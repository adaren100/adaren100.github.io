**[NTT DATA Group cuts incident analysis to 30 minutes with Codex](https://openai.com/index/ntt-data)** — _OpenAI · Jul 22_

**Main takeaway:** NTT DATA Group expanded Codex to roughly 9,000 employees after it cut a critical-system incident analysis that used to take five engineers three days down to 30 minutes, building on a companywide ChatGPT Enterprise rollout.

**Main methods:**
- **ChatGPT Enterprise as foundation.** Since a May 2025 global partnership with OpenAI, NTT DATA built an internal "Center of Excellence" to drive adoption — license distribution, technical validation, events, usage monitoring — reporting 96%+ satisfaction and 95%+ productivity gains internally.
- **Breakout Codex use case.** A complex incident analysis for a critical system, previously requiring five experienced engineers and three days, was completed in 30 minutes with Codex.
- **Agentic, not just assistive.** The post frames Codex as able to independently investigate, execute, test, and revise based on an instruction, distinguishing it from a conventional coding assistant.
- **Expanded beyond engineering.** Codex access grew to about 9,000 employees across both technical and nontechnical roles, moving from conversational use to delegating clearly defined tasks.
- **Secure rollout emphasis.** The case study highlights building a secure environment as part of scaling Codex access company-wide.

**[NTT DATA Group 用 Codex 把事故分析压缩到 30 分钟](https://openai.com/index/ntt-data)** — _OpenAI · 7月22日_

**Main takeaway:** NTT DATA Group 把 Codex 扩展到大约 9000 名员工，起因是他们用 Codex 把一次关键系统的事故分析——原本要 5 个工程师干 3 天——压缩到了 30 分钟，这是在全公司铺开 ChatGPT Enterprise 之后的延伸。

**Main methods:**
- **ChatGPT Enterprise 打底。** 自 2025 年 5 月和 OpenAI 建立全球战略合作以来，NTT DATA 内部搭了个"Center of Excellence"来推动落地——发牌照、技术验证、办活动、监控使用情况，内部调研显示满意度超过 96%，95% 以上的人反馈生产力有提升。
- **Codex 的破圈用例。** 一次关键系统的事故分析，原本要 5 个资深工程师干 3 天，用 Codex 30 分钟就搞定了。
- **不只是辅助，是能自己干活。** 文章特意强调 Codex 能根据指令独立调查、执行、测试、修正，跟传统的编程助手不是一回事。
- **从技术团队扩展到全员。** Codex 访问权限扩大到约 9000 名员工，覆盖技术和非技术岗位，用法也从对话式咨询变成直接委派明确任务。
- **强调安全落地。** 案例里特别提到，扩大 Codex 使用范围的同时，也在搭建一套安全的使用环境。
