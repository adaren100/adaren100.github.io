**[Hebbia sees improvements in financial research with GPT-6 Astra](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/hebbia)** — _OpenAI · Nov 20_

**Main takeaway:** A customer story: Hebbia tested a prerelease GPT‑6 Astra inside its Matrix product for investment-banking research. In Hebbia's own tests the model led the best alternative tested by 13.6 percentage points on citing the required source types, and 99.5% of the excerpts it cited were relevant to the question.

**Main methods:**
- **The workflow being tested.** Two parts of banker research: finding the sources a question requires across a data room of thousands of files, and building a presentation that follows the banker's brief.
- **Citations as the product.** Matrix links answers back to the underlying documents in cited cells; Hebbia's Kavita frames traceability as essential because users are making million-dollar decisions on the output.
- **Source coverage plus precision.** 13.6-point lead over the best alternative on required source types, with 99.5% of cited excerpts judged relevant — broad retrieval without losing relevance.
- **Deck generation scored too.** GPT‑6 Astra met 97.9% of applicable presentation requirements, 14.4 points ahead of the best alternative, and generates native PowerPoint slides in the firm's template.
- **It didn't trade polish for instruction-following.** Hebbia says other models traded off presentation quality against adherence to the brief; GPT‑6 Astra ranked first on both.
- **Eval setup and caveat.** A prerelease build tested in August–September 2026, same financial research cases and grading criteria for every model, with domain experts writing or auditing criteria against source documents — but these are the vendor's own tests, not a public benchmark.

**[Hebbia 实测 GPT-6 Astra 做金融研究](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/hebbia)** — _OpenAI · 11月20日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇客户案例。Hebbia 把 prerelease 的 GPT‑6 Astra 放进自家产品 Matrix 里做投行研究，他们自己测出来的结果是：在"该引的几类材料有没有引到"这项上比测过的最好的其他模型高 13.6 个百分点，而且引用的片段里有 99.5% 跟问题是相关的。

**Main methods:**
- **测的是什么活。** 投行研究里的两块：一是在几千份文件的 data room 里把一个问题需要的材料捞出来，二是按 banker 给的 brief 把研究做成一份能交出去的演示材料。
- **citation 本身就是产品。** Matrix 会把答案落回到具体文件，在 cited cell 里能点回去。Hebbia 的 Kavita 说 traceability 特别关键，因为用户是拿这个输出去做上百万美元级别的决定。
- **又广又准。** 必引材料类型领先 13.6 个百分点，同时 99.5% 的引用片段被判定为相关，说明捞得多并没有把相关性拖下来。
- **做 deck 也单独打了分。** GPT‑6 Astra 满足了 97.9% 的演示要求，比测过的最好的其他模型高 14.4 个百分点，而且直接出按公司模板走的原生 PowerPoint。
- **没有在"好看"和"听话"之间二选一。** Hebbia 说别的模型往往是保了排版就不守 brief，守了 brief 就不好看，GPT‑6 Astra 两项都是第一。
- **测试设置和一个 caveat。** 用的是 2026 年 8 到 9 月的 prerelease 版本，每个模型跑同一批金融研究案例、同一套打分标准，评分标准由领域专家对着原始文件写或者审过。不过要说清楚，这是厂商自己的测试，不是公开 benchmark。
