**[Partnering with CodeAI to prepare the first AI generation](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-codeai)** — _OpenAI · Aug 18_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI and CodeAI announced a "signature partnership" on AI literacy for students and teachers, timed to the ChatGPT for Teens launch. The framing number: most students already use AI, but only 16% of high school leaders say all of their students are learning the technical knowledge to understand it in class.

**Main methods:**
- **The gap the partnership targets.** Usage is near-universal while understanding is not — 16% of high school leaders report all students getting technical instruction on AI, and in a CodeAI survey 75% of high school students said understanding AI will matter more to their futures than it does now.
- **Division of labor.** ChatGPT for Teens is the product side; the CodeAI partnership covers the education *around* the technology — helping teens understand how AI works, direct it, question it, and create with it, with teachers shaping how it enters the classroom.
- **A joint advisory council.** OpenAI and CodeAI will stand up a council of child development, youth public policy, and learning science experts to give ongoing guidance on emerging risks and responsible practices, feeding back into how ChatGPT for Teens is built.
- **One-year program scope.** Over the next year the two will run practical programs helping students understand AI, use it carefully, and explore how it supports learning and creativity.
- **Builds on existing education work.** Extends ChatGPT for Teachers (district- and educator-facing) and OpenAI's ongoing AI-skills work with the American Federation of Teachers.
- **How they pitch it.** Allison Mishkin (OpenAI's Head of Child Development) frames the goal as helping students "ask better questions" rather than just use AI more; CodeAI CEO Karim Meghji frames it as teaching students to catch AI's mistakes and know when to stop trusting it.

**[OpenAI 拉上 CodeAI，给第一代 AI 原住民补课](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-codeai)** — _OpenAI · 8月18日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 和 CodeAI 宣布了一个所谓 signature partnership，做面向学生和老师的 AI 素养教育，时间点卡在 ChatGPT for Teens 发布这天。文章拿来立论的数字是：学生基本都在用 AI 了，但只有 16% 的高中管理者说自己学校全体学生在课堂上学过 AI 是怎么回事。

**Main methods:**
- **要补的到底是哪块缺口。** 用是都在用，懂的人不多。16% 的高中管理者说全体学生接受过 AI 技术层面的教学；CodeAI 自己做的调查里，75% 的高中生认为搞懂 AI 对他们未来会比现在更重要。
- **两边分工很清楚。** 产品那一半是 ChatGPT for Teens，CodeAI 这一半管技术外围的教育：让青少年明白 AI 怎么运作，会指挥它、会质疑它、也能拿它做点东西，同时让老师在 AI 进课堂这件事上有话语权。
- **搞了个联合顾问委员会。** 拉来做儿童发展、青少年公共政策、learning science 的专家，持续给新出现的风险和负责任使用提建议，这些意见会直接反过来影响 ChatGPT for Teens 后面怎么做。
- **一年的项目范围。** 接下来一年两家一起落地一批实操型项目，帮学生理解 AI、谨慎使用，也顺便看看它到底能怎么帮学习和创作。
- **接着之前的教育线在做。** 这是在 ChatGPT for Teachers（面向老师和学区）之上再往前走一步，也接着 OpenAI 和 American Federation of Teachers 一直在做的 AI 技能培训。
- **两边的说法。** OpenAI 儿童发展负责人 Allison Mishkin 强调目标不是让更多学生用上 AI，而是让他们会问更好的问题；CodeAI CEO Karim Meghji 的说法更直接：学生得能抓出 AI 的错，知道什么时候别再信它。
