**[How RingCentral builds AI-native work from engineering to ops](https://openai.com/index/ringcentral)** — _OpenAI · Aug 12_

**Main takeaway:** A customer case study: RingCentral, a business communications company with more than $2.6 billion in annual revenue, gave every employee ChatGPT Work and Codex, and reports that thousands of staff — including non-technical employees and executives — shipped working end-to-end projects in an internal AI-Native Challenge.

**Main methods:**
- **The AI-Native Challenge.** Sponsored by RingCentral's Office of the CEO, every participant got ChatGPT Work and Codex and was asked to build a complete end-to-end project with no mandated workflow or other constraints; nearly every participant ended up with a working repository.
- **Full lifecycle, not just codegen.** The challenge deliberately covered planning, implementation, testing, documentation, CI/CD, and iteration rather than being a one-off coding exercise.
- **Same approach applied to shipped products.** RingCentral uses the Codex-enabled workflow to accelerate its own AI portfolio — AI Receptionist (AIR), AI Virtual Assistant (AVA), and AI Conversation Expert (ACE) — to shorten the distance between an idea and a customer feature.
- **PMO as an operating system.** The Program Management Office built ChatGPT Work workflows for status tracking, reporting, release governance, and knowledge transfer, replacing scattered notes and chat history.
- **Automated status reporting.** Those workflows generate notifications from issues tracked across Jira, Google Sheets, CRM systems, and other sources, so a meeting starts with blockers, owners, and actions already defined instead of asking what changed.
- **What the post doesn't establish.** This is a marketing case study: the claims — that AI amplifies rather than replaces engineers with humans in the loop, and that the PMO handles more projects with greater accuracy — come from RingCentral leadership, with no independent measurement or baseline.

**[RingCentral 把 ChatGPT Work 和 Codex 铺到了全员](https://openai.com/index/ringcentral)** — _OpenAI · 8月12日_

**Main takeaway:** 这是一篇客户案例：年收入超过 26 亿美元的通信公司 RingCentral 给全员发了 ChatGPT Work 和 Codex，在一场内部的 AI-Native Challenge 里，几千号人（包括不写代码的同事和高管）都交出了能跑起来的完整项目。

**Main methods:**
- **AI-Native Challenge 是怎么办的。** 由 Office of the CEO 牵头，每个参与者都拿到 ChatGPT Work 和 Codex，题目就是从头到尾做一个完整项目，不规定流程也不加限制。最后几乎每个人都留下了一个能跑的 repo。
- **走的是完整开发周期。** 这事不只是写代码，规划、实现、测试、文档、CI/CD、迭代全包在里面，让员工把整个 AI-native 开发流程实打实走一遍。
- **同一套做法也用在自家产品上。** RingCentral 用这套 Codex 流程加速自己的 AI 产品线：AI Receptionist（AIR）、AI Virtual Assistant（AVA）和 AI Conversation Expert（ACE），目的是把"有个想法"到"功能上线"之间的距离压短。
- **PMO 直接拿它当"操作系统"用。** 项目管理办公室用 ChatGPT Work 搭了一整套状态跟踪、汇报、发版 governance 和知识交接的流程，把原来散在笔记和聊天记录里的东西收拢起来。
- **自动状态汇报。** 这些 workflow 会从 Jira、Google Sheets、CRM 这些系统里的 issue 自动生成通知，开会前 blocker、负责人、下一步动作就已经列好了，不用到会上才问这周有什么变化。
- **有一点要说清楚。** 这篇本质是 marketing case study，里面"AI 是放大工程师而不是取代""PMO 项目接得更多、准确率更高"这类说法都来自 RingCentral 自己，既没有独立测量，也没有 baseline 对比。
