**[From assistance to execution: How enterprises put AI to work](https://openai.com/index/how-enterprises-put-ai-to-work)** — _OpenAI · Aug 12_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI published two studies on enterprise AI adoption, and the headline number is the widening "frontier gap": firms in the top 10% of AI usage each month now generate 8.3× as many output tokens per active user as typical firms, up from 2.6× in January.

**Main methods:**
- **Two companion reports.** Enterprise Signals gives a practical view of agentic AI across OpenAI's enterprise customer base; the working paper How Organizations Use AI: Evidence from ChatGPT examines how adoption grows across companies, roles, and levels of seniority.
- **Output tokens as the proxy.** The gap is measured in output tokens per active user, which OpenAI treats as a proxy for depth of use rather than headcount adoption, and it went from 2.6× in January to 8.3× by June.
- **Enterprise use is going agentic.** As of June, Codex generated 64% of combined Codex and ChatGPT output tokens among enterprise customers, which OpenAI reads as a shift from asking questions to delegating substantive work.
- **Advanced capabilities separate the leaders.** Each week, 21% of active users at frontier firms use Plugins versus 9% at typical firms; at OpenAI itself the figure is 95% of employees, which the post cites as the headroom available.
- **Agents spread fastest outside engineering.** Since February, weekly active enterprise Codex users grew 108× in legal, 41× in sales, 41× in recruiting, and 26× in marketing, against 5× in engineering.
- **Seniority gradient, and the recommended agenda.** Usage is highest among early-career workers and falls with seniority, which OpenAI frames as a comparative advantage for junior employees; its advice to leaders is to connect agents to company context and tools, set clear permissions, review, and governance, and turn effective individual workflows into shared ones.

**[企业用 AI 从"问"转向"做"：frontier firm 已经拉开 8.3 倍](https://openai.com/index/how-enterprises-put-ai-to-work)** — _OpenAI · 8月12日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 一口气发了两份讲企业 AI 采用情况的报告，最抢眼的是那个越拉越大的 frontier gap：每月用量排前 10% 的公司，人均输出 token 已经是普通公司的 8.3 倍，1 月的时候这个差距还只有 2.6 倍。

**Main methods:**
- **两份报告分工。** Enterprise Signals 偏实操，讲 agentic AI 在 OpenAI 企业客户里落到什么程度；配套的 working paper《How Organizations Use AI: Evidence from ChatGPT》则看采用率怎么在公司、岗位和职级之间扩散。
- **为什么用 output token 来衡量。** 他们拿人均输出 token 当"用得有多深"的 proxy，而不是看开了多少账号。这个差距半年里从 2.6 倍拉到了 8.3 倍。
- **企业的用法在往 agent 走。** 6 月的数据里，企业客户 Codex 加 ChatGPT 的输出 token 有 64% 是 Codex 产出的。OpenAI 认为这说明大家真的开始把活派出去，而不只是问问题。
- **拉开差距的是那些进阶功能。** frontier firm 里每周用 Plugins 的活跃用户占 21%，普通公司只有 9%；OpenAI 自己内部是 95% 的员工每周都在用，他们拿这个数当"还有多少空间"的参照。
- **涨得最猛的反而不是工程口。** 从 2 月算起，企业 Codex 周活在 legal 涨了 108 倍，sales 和 recruiting 各 41 倍，marketing 26 倍，工程这边只有 5 倍。
- **越资深用得越少，以及他们给的建议。** 使用率在 early-career 员工里最高，往上反而递减，OpenAI 觉得这算年轻员工的一种比较优势。给管理层的建议是：把 agent 接上公司自己的 context 和工具，权限、review、governance 立清楚，再把个人摸索出来的好流程沉淀成团队共用的。
