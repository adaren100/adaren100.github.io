**[Asana cleared 5 years of engineering work in 2 weeks with Codex](https://openai.com/index/asana)** — _OpenAI · Aug 18_

**Main takeaway:** A customer case study: Asana used OpenAI Codex to fully remove Enzyme, a dead frontend testing framework, in about two calendar weeks for roughly $12K in model and infrastructure cost — against a previous plan estimated at five years and about $6M in staffing.

**Main methods:**
- **The migration itself.** Enzyme had fallen out of active maintenance and was blocking Asana from modernizing its frontend stack, which is what made it worth throwing agents at.
- **Four parallel agents, one prompt.** From a five-sentence prompt, up to four coding agents ran in parallel, each working in a separate copy of the codebase.
- **Humans stayed on review, not authoring.** An engineer checked progress twice a day and reviewed every proposed change; the actual engineering effort came to about 1.5 weeks spread across two calendar weeks.
- **Simpler prompting beat elaborate scaffolding.** The post explicitly notes that simple instructions worked better than a more elaborate setup.
- **What it changed downstream.** The result shifted Asana's view of which long-running projects are practical at all — they now plan to point agents at other migrations, rewrites, and performance problems previously assumed to take years.

**[Asana 用 Codex 两周干完原计划五年的活](https://openai.com/index/asana)** — _OpenAI · 8月18日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇客户案例：Asana 用 OpenAI Codex 把 Enzyme 这个已经没人维护的前端测试框架整个拆干净，前后两个自然周，model 加基础设施花了大概 1.2 万美元。而按他们之前的人力方案，这活儿至少要五年、大约 600 万美元。

**Main methods:**
- **要迁的是什么。** Enzyme 早就没人维护了，卡着 Asana 的前端技术栈升不上去，所以才值得拿 agent 硬啃一遍。
- **一个 prompt，四个 agent 并行。** 起手就是五句话的 prompt，最多同时跑四个 coding agent，每个在自己那份 codebase 副本里干活。
- **人只管 review，不动手写。** 工程师一天看两次进度，每一个改动都过一遍。真正投进去的工程量大概 1.5 周，摊在两个自然周里。
- **prompt 写简单点反而更好用。** 文章专门点了一句：简单指令的效果比那套更复杂的配置更好。
- **真正的变化在后面。** 这次之后 Asana 对"哪些长周期项目其实是可做的"这件事的判断变了，接下来打算把 agent 放到别的 migration、重写和性能问题上，那些以前默认要做好几年的活。
