**[How Sumitomo Life's leaders helped teams adopt ChatGPT Enterprise](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/sumitomo-life)** — _OpenAI · Nov 18_

**Main takeaway:** An enterprise adoption case study: Japanese life insurer Sumitomo Life rolled ChatGPT Enterprise out to thousands of employees after an executive-led pilot. Overall estimated ROI is 203%, based on employee-reported time savings, with the headline figure drawn from employees using ChatGPT at least 101 times a month.

**Main methods:**
- **Adoption ladder, 2023 → 2026.** Started with generative AI in 2023 as plain chat Q&A; piloted ChatGPT Business with roughly 100–150 people including executives and planning staff in 2025; moved to ChatGPT Enterprise in June 2026.
- **Top-down modeling as the adoption lever.** Executives and managers were among the first users, and the president used it during the Business pilot and pushed it in management meetings and on the internal portal — which Kakeru Tateyama of the planning department credits for broader uptake.
- **Two concrete workloads.** Investment teams have ChatGPT draft the statistical code for return forecasting and risk analysis; planning teams research other Japanese insurers and overseas examples to strengthen proposals to senior management.
- **Projects to hold context.** Planning teams use ChatGPT projects to keep files, related chats, and custom instructions together alongside firsthand information from people close to the work.
- **Security review gated the rollout.** Sumitomo Life worked with OpenAI to assess ChatGPT Enterprise against its financial-institution standards, covering workspace access control, feature permissions, and OpenAI's default exclusion of business data from model training.
- **Caveat on the ROI number.** The 203% figure is self-reported time savings from employees, not an independently measured financial result.

**[住友生命的高管带头，把 ChatGPT Enterprise 推下去了](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/sumitomo-life)** — _OpenAI · 11月18日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇企业落地案例。日本寿险公司住友生命先让高管层试点，再把 ChatGPT Enterprise 铺到几千名员工。按员工自己报的省下来的时间算，整体 estimated ROI 是 203%，这个数字来自那些每月至少用 101 次的员工。

**Main methods:**
- **从 2023 一路爬到 2026。** 2023 年开始用生成式 AI，那时候基本就是在 chat 里问问题；2025 年拉上大约 100 到 150 人试点 ChatGPT Business，里面有高管也有规划部门的人；2026 年 6 月切到 ChatGPT Enterprise。
- **推广的抓手是上面先用起来。** 高管和中层是最早一批用户，社长在 Business 试点期间自己就在用，还在管理会议和公司内网上反复提。规划部门的 Kakeru Tateyama 说，普及开来主要就是靠这个示范效应。
- **两个具体场景。** 投资团队让 ChatGPT 帮他们写收益预测和风险分析用的统计代码，自己要手写的部分少了很多；规划团队用它跟踪其他日本险企的动向、找海外案例，再拿这些研究去支撑给高层的提案。
- **用 projects 把上下文攒在一起。** 规划团队把文件、相关对话、custom instruction 都放进 ChatGPT 的 project 里，再配上从一线的人那儿拿到的一手信息。
- **安全审查是放量的前置条件。** 住友生命跟 OpenAI 一起把 ChatGPT Enterprise 对着金融机构的安全标准过了一遍，包括 workspace 权限怎么管、功能权限怎么配，以及 OpenAI 默认不拿企业数据训模型这条。
- **ROI 这个数要打个折扣看。** 203% 是员工自己报的省时时间换算出来的，不是独立核算过的财务结果。
