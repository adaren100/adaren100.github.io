**[Introducing ChatGPT for Teens: Built for learning, backed by protections](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens)** — _OpenAI · Aug 18_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens, a learning-centered variant with stronger built-in protections, healthy-use features, and extra parental controls. Placement is automatic: anyone the system estimates to be under 18, or who states an age of 13–17, gets routed into it.

**Main methods:**
- **Automatic routing, not opt-in.** Age-Prediction estimates or a self-stated age of 13–17 puts a user into the teen experience by default, rather than leaving it to parents or teens to switch on.
- **Study Mode as the default learning surface.** Built with teachers, scientists, and pedagogy experts, it uses guiding questions, scaffolding, metacognitive prompts, and knowledge checks to walk students through a problem instead of handing over the solution.
- **Responsible homework reminders.** A new feature that recognizes when a teen appears to be trying to shortcut an assignment and redirects them into Study Mode for step-by-step work.
- **Quizzes, Learning Visualizations, and Study Hours.** Quizzes and visualizations give more ways to practice and see hard concepts; Study Hours lets teens or parents set windows where Study Mode is on by default, turning it into a habit rather than a choice.
- **The research claim.** OpenAI ties the design to work on active retrieval and self-explanation outperforming passive review, and says its own early evaluation of tools like Study Mode showed "promising gains in student performance" — no numbers given.
- **Where it sits in the stack.** ChatGPT for Teens covers learning outside the classroom; ChatGPT for Teachers remains the institution-managed path for schools. It builds on Parental Controls, the Under-18 Model Spec, the Teen Safety Blueprint, and Age-Prediction, under four stated commitments: teen safety first, encourage real-world support, treat teens like teens, be transparent about system behavior.

**[ChatGPT for Teens 上线：围着学习设计，安全兜底](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens)** — _OpenAI · 8月18日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 发布了 ChatGPT for Teens，一个把学习放在中心的版本，内置更强的安全保护、促进健康使用的功能，还给家长加了额外控制项。进入方式是自动的：系统判断你未满 18 岁，或者你自己填了 13 到 17 岁，就直接被放进这个版本。

**Main methods:**
- **默认分流，不是让你自己开。** Age-Prediction 的判断结果，或者用户自报 13 到 17 岁，都会被自动放进 teen 版本，不指望家长或孩子主动去打开开关。
- **Study Mode 成了学习这块的主入口。** 这东西是拉着老师、科学家和教学法专家一起做的，用引导式提问、scaffolding、metacognitive prompt 和知识点检查带着学生一步步走，而不是直接把答案递过去。
- **新的"作业提醒"。** 系统能看出来孩子是想抄近道糊弄作业，然后把他推回 Study Mode，老老实实一步步做。
- **Quizzes、Learning Visualizations 和 Study Hours。** 前两个多给几种练习和把难概念画出来的方式；Study Hours 让孩子或家长划出时间段，在这段时间里 Study Mode 默认开着，把好习惯变成日程而不是每次靠自觉。
- **他们援引的研究依据。** OpenAI 把这套设计挂到 active retrieval 和 self-explanation 比被动复习更有效的研究上，还说自己对 Study Mode 这类工具做的早期评估看到了学生表现的提升，不过没给具体数字。
- **在整个产品线里的位置。** ChatGPT for Teens 管课堂之外的学习，学校这边仍然走 ChatGPT for Teachers 那条机构统一管理的路。它是接着 Parental Controls、Under-18 Model Spec、Teen Safety Blueprint 和 Age-Prediction 一路做下来的，官方给的四条原则是：青少年安全优先、鼓励回到现实世界求助、把青少年当青少年看、系统该怎么表现要说清楚。
