**[How Zapier transformed core marketing processes with ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/zapier)** — _OpenAI · Aug 10_

**Main takeaway:** A marketing case study: Zapier's enterprise marketing team built an automated lead-funnel QA/QC system on ChatGPT Work and says the fixes it surfaced handed sales seven figures of pipeline every month.

**Main methods:**
- **The problem was drop-off, not lead volume.** Zapier had strong inbound volume but a similarly high drop-off rate, and diagnosing where leads fell out required a QA/QC pass too labor-intensive to run by hand — 35 to 45 minutes for one person to understand a single dropped lead.
- **Automated QA/QC at scale.** The system now runs over thousands of leads a month, identifies the lead problems, patches the issues, and quantifies the impact of the fixes.
- **An executive dashboard as the standing output.** Rather than a one-off analysis, the system produces ongoing reporting on recurring pipeline issues and weekly trends.
- **Second use: campaign ideation.** The demand generation team routes campaign work through ChatGPT Work to get optimization ideas, compressing the gap between ideation and execution.
- **Reporting automation frees analysis time.** Head of Enterprise Marketing Angela Ferrante says dialed-in reporting lets the team spend more time doing work than analyzing the results of the work.
- **What's next: always-on loops.** Ferrante's view is that the bigger unlock is standing, always-on loops rather than one-off prompts.

**[Zapier 用 ChatGPT Work 把营销流程重做了一遍](https://openai.com/index/zapier)** — _OpenAI · 8月10日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇营销案例。Zapier 的 enterprise marketing 团队在 ChatGPT Work 上搭了套自动化的 lead funnel QA/QC 系统，他们说光靠这套系统修出来的问题，每个月能给销售多交七位数的 pipeline。

**Main methods:**
- **卡点不在线索量，在漏斗漏水。** Zapier 的 inbound 线索质量不差，但掉队率同样高，想知道线索到底卡在哪一环，得做一轮极其费人的 QA/QC，一个人光看懂一条掉队线索就要 35 到 45 分钟，纯手工根本跑不动。
- **QA/QC 直接上规模。** 现在这套系统每个月能过几千条线索，把问题一条条挑出来、补上，还顺手把这波修复带来的收益量化出来。
- **产出是一块常驻的高管看板。** 不是做完一次分析就完事，而是持续出报告，盯 pipeline 里反复出现的问题和每周的趋势变化。
- **第二个用法是想 campaign。** demand generation 团队把 campaign 的活丢给 ChatGPT Work 过一遍，拿优化思路，从想法到落地的时间被压得很短。
- **报表自动化省下了分析时间。** Head of Enterprise Marketing Angela Ferrante 说，报表这块理顺之后，团队花在干活上的时间明显多过花在复盘结果上的时间。
- **下一步是 always-on 的循环。** Ferrante 觉得真正能改造营销团队的，不是一次次敲 prompt，而是把这些流程做成一直挂着自己跑的 loop。
