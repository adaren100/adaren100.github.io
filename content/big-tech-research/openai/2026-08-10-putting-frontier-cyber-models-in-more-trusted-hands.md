**[Putting frontier cyber models in more trusted hands](https://openai.com/index/putting-frontier-cyber-models-in-more-trusted-hands)** — _OpenAI · Aug 10_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI is expanding its Daybreak Cyber Partner Program so approved security firms can deliver its frontier cyber models through the products and services their customers already run — a distribution play rather than a new capability.

**Main methods:**
- **Partner roster spans services and product vendors.** Services and consulting partners include Accenture, IBM, Capgemini, Cognizant, EY, KPMG, PwC, NCC Group, and SpecterOps; technology partners include Palo Alto Networks, CrowdStrike, Cisco, Sophos, Akamai, Fortinet, and Cloudflare.
- **The pitch is remediation, not discovery.** OpenAI's framing is that a vulnerability report protects nobody — value comes from judging whether a weakness is actually exploitable, identifying the systems at risk, building a fix, and getting it into production.
- **Engagement types on offer.** Depending on the engagement, partners can cover vulnerability discovery and validation, red teaming, penetration testing, incident response, and remediation across complex enterprise systems.
- **Why partners rather than direct access.** Partners already know their customers' systems and how their security teams operate, so putting frontier models inside existing services reaches defenders who currently have no frontier access at all.
- **Access is paired with governance.** The post presents broader access alongside "the safeguards to match", positioning partner-side governance as the control layer, though the extracted body cuts off before the specifics.
- **Threat framing.** Attackers are finding vulnerabilities, building exploits, and moving through complex systems at machine speed, while security teams face a growing volume of weaknesses across everything they protect.

**[把前沿安全模型交到更多可信的手上](https://openai.com/index/putting-frontier-cyber-models-in-more-trusted-hands)** — _OpenAI · 8月10日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 把 Daybreak Cyber Partner 项目扩大了，让通过审核的安全厂商能把它的前沿安全模型直接塞进客户已经在用的产品和服务里。这篇讲的是渠道和分发，不是新能力。

**Main methods:**
- **合作方既有咨询也有产品。** 服务和咨询这边有 Accenture、IBM、Capgemini、Cognizant、EY、KPMG、PwC、NCC Group、SpecterOps；技术厂商这边有 Palo Alto Networks、CrowdStrike、Cisco、Sophos、Akamai、Fortinet、Cloudflare。
- **卖点在"修"，不在"找"。** OpenAI 的说法是，一份漏洞报告本身保护不了任何人，真正有价值的是判断这个 vulnerability 到底能不能被 exploit、哪些系统受影响、patch 怎么写、以及怎么推到生产环境。
- **合作能覆盖哪些活。** 看具体项目而定，可以包括漏洞挖掘和验证、red teaming、pen-testing、incident response，以及在复杂企业系统里做修复。
- **为什么走合作方而不是直接放开。** 合作方本来就熟客户的系统，也知道对方安全团队怎么运转，把模型嵌进现有服务里，就能覆盖到那些原本完全够不着前沿模型的防守方。
- **权限放开的同时配了治理。** 文章把"更大的访问权限"和"配套的 safeguard"放在一起讲，等于把合作方那边的治理当成控制层，不过抓到的正文在细节之前就断了。
- **背景叙事。** 攻击方找漏洞、写 exploit、在复杂系统里横向移动的速度已经快到机器级别，而防守方要盯的软件和基础设施里，待处理的弱点还在不断变多。
