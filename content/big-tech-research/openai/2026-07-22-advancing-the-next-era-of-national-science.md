**[Advancing the next era of national science](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-next-era-of-national-science)** — _OpenAI · Jul 22_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI details its Genesis Mission commitments to the U.S. Department of Energy — including $4M in Codex access for ~2,000 national-lab researchers and $3M in API support for large-scale scientific campaigns — building on prior work like deploying reasoning models on the Venado supercomputer at Los Alamos.

**Main methods:**
- **Codex for researchers.** $4 million in Codex access for roughly 2,000 Genesis researchers at National Laboratories and universities, aimed at coding and reasoning work.
- **Funded scientific campaigns.** $3 million in API support for two large-scale campaigns testing frontier models against major scientific challenges, plus up to $10 million in API usage for every $2.5 million researchers spend.
- **Domain-specific and early access.** Selected national-lab biology researchers get access to GPT-Rosalind's specialized bioscience capabilities; trusted lab leaders and cybersecurity researchers get early access to models and advanced cyber capabilities.
- **Supercomputer deployment.** Advanced reasoning models were deployed on Venado, the Los Alamos supercomputer, as a shared resource across National Nuclear Security Administration labs.
- **Safety evaluation work.** OpenAI and Los Alamos built evaluations studying how multimodal AI systems can be used safely by scientists in real lab settings.
- **Scale of engagement so far.** An AI Jam Session across nine National Laboratories had more than 1,000 scientists test frontier models on domain-specific problems and give structured feedback.

**[推进国家科学的下一个阶段](https://openai.com/index/advancing-the-next-era-of-national-science)** — _OpenAI · 7月22日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 公布了在 Genesis Mission 里对美国能源部的具体承诺——给约 2000 名国家实验室研究者提供 400 万美元的 Codex 访问权限，再拿出 300 万美元 API 额度支持大型科研项目，这是他们在 Los Alamos 的 Venado 超算上部署推理模型之后的进一步延伸。

**Main methods:**
- **Codex 给研究者用。** 400 万美元的 Codex 额度，覆盖国家实验室和高校的约 2000 名 Genesis 研究者，主要用在编程和推理工作上。
- **资助具体科研项目。** 300 万美元 API 支持两个大型科研项目，专门拿前沿模型去啃重大科学难题；另外研究者每花 250 万美元，还能再额外拿到最多 1000 万美元的 API 用量。
- **专项和早期访问。** 部分国家实验室的生物学研究者可以用上 GPT-Rosalind 的专项生物科学能力；受信任的实验室负责人和网络安全研究者能早期用上新模型和高级网络安全能力。
- **超算部署。** 已经在 Los Alamos 的 Venado 超算上部署了高阶推理模型，作为核安全局旗下各实验室共享的资源。
- **安全评估配套。** OpenAI 和 Los Alamos 一起做了评估体系，研究多模态 AI 系统在真实实验室环境里怎么安全地被科学家使用。
- **目前的参与规模。** 此前跨九个国家实验室办的 AI Jam Session，已经有 1000 多名科学家用前沿模型测试各自领域的问题并给出结构化反馈。
