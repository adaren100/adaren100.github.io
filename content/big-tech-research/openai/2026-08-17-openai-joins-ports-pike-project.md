**[OpenAI joins PORTS-Pike project](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project)** — _OpenAI · Aug 17_

**Main takeaway:** An infrastructure announcement: OpenAI has agreed to secure roughly 8 gigawatts-IT at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio, working with SB Energy, NVIDIA, and the U.S. Department of Energy, with a projected 35,000 construction jobs through 2032 and 2,500 long-term operating jobs.

**Main methods:**
- **Scale and partners.** ~8 GW-IT at the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio, developed with SB Energy, NVIDIA, and the Department of Energy — the site of the former Portsmouth gaseous diffusion plant.
- **Ratepayers aren't picking up the grid bill.** SB Energy pays the full cost of grid upgrades and new transmission lines needed to serve the data center; the post states those costs will not be shifted to Ohio or other regional ratepayers, and that the work should strengthen regional grid reliability.
- **Closed-loop, air-cooled water design.** Cooling recirculates water instead of using continuously-consuming cooling towers, so ongoing use is expected to be comparable to an office building supporting a similar headcount, and far below the old gaseous diffusion plant. OpenAI says it will publicly report expected water use once site design is final.
- **Community money.** A $40 million OpenAI community grant fund shaped by local residents' priorities (schools, public safety, health care, utilities, workforce training, housing, veterans' services, small businesses), on top of SB Energy's existing $40 million commitment.
- **Separate education spend.** $84 million in Codex credits through ChatGPT, giving every Ohio college student access.
- **Local hiring commitment.** OpenAI and SB Energy say they will prioritize local workers, contractors, suppliers, trades, and service providers across the six-year buildout.

**[OpenAI 加入俄亥俄 PORTS-Pike 项目](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project)** — _OpenAI · 8月17日_

**Main takeaway:** 一条基建公告：OpenAI 已经签下俄亥俄州 Pike County 的 PORTS-Pike 科技园区约 8 GW-IT 的容量，合作方是 SB Energy、NVIDIA 和美国能源部。官方预计六年建设期到 2032 年能带来 35,000 个建筑岗位，外加 2,500 个长期运营岗位。

**Main methods:**
- **体量和合作方。** 俄亥俄 Pike County 的 PORTS-Pike 科技园区，约 8 GW-IT，和 SB Energy、NVIDIA、能源部一起做。这块地就是当年 Portsmouth 气体扩散铀浓缩厂的旧址。
- **电网改造的钱不摊给居民。** 为数据中心配套的电网升级和新输电线路，全部由 SB Energy 承担，OpenAI 明确说这笔成本不会转嫁给俄亥俄或周边地区的用电户，而且这轮投入还能顺带把区域电网做得更稳。
- **闭环风冷，水基本不消耗。** 冷却系统走 closed-loop 风冷、循环用水，不用那种一直蒸发耗水的冷却塔。灌满之后日常用水量大概和一栋同等人数的写字楼差不多，比原来那个气体扩散厂低得多。场地设计定稿后，OpenAI 说会公开预计用水量。
- **给社区的钱。** OpenAI 拿出 4,000 万美元做社区基金，具体投向由当地居民定，学校、公共安全、医疗、水电、职业培训、住房、退伍军人服务、小生意都在名单上。这是在 SB Energy 此前那 4,000 万承诺之外另加的。
- **教育这块另算。** 8,400 万美元的 Codex credits 通过 ChatGPT 发放，俄亥俄所有大学生都能用上。
- **优先用本地人。** OpenAI 和 SB Energy 表态，六年建设期里会优先找本地工人、承包商、供应商、各工种和服务商。
