**[Premium seats are coming to ChatGPT Business](https://openai.com/index/premium-seats-chatgpt-business)** — _OpenAI · Aug 10_

**Main takeaway:** ChatGPT Business is getting a Premium seat at $125 per user per month ($100 billed annually) with 5× the usage of a Standard seat and no five-hour usage limit; workspace owners who sign up by August 20 get $100 in credits per Premium seat, up to $500.

**Main methods:**
- **Pricing and mixed seat types.** Premium is $125/user/month, or $100 annually; Standard stays at $25/month, or $20 annually. Both seat types can coexist in one workspace, and admins can upgrade or reassign seats as needs change.
- **What Premium actually unlocks.** 5× more usage than Standard, removal of the five-hour usage limit, and predictable weekly usage resets.
- **Overflow handled by shared credits.** Hitting a limit doesn't stop the work — workspaces can add shared credits managed by the workspace owner, with billing, usage, and spend limits administered in one place.
- **Launch promo with a cap.** The first 10,000 eligible ChatGPT Business customers get $100 in workspace credits (2,500 credits) per Premium seat, capped at $500 for five seats; some early sign-ups may also get access before general availability.
- **Positioned as the top customer ask.** OpenAI says an expanded seat with more built-in capacity was the most requested item from Business customers, citing work like inventory organization, marketing campaigns, business performance analysis, and Codex across larger codebases.

**[ChatGPT Business 要上 Premium 座席了](https://openai.com/index/premium-seats-chatgpt-business)** — _OpenAI · 8月10日_

**Main takeaway:** ChatGPT Business 新增一档 Premium 座席，每人每月 125 美元（按年付 100 美元），用量是 Standard 的 5 倍，还取消了那个五小时的用量限制；8 月 20 日前报名的 workspace owner，每个 Premium 座席送 100 美元额度，最多 500 美元。

**Main methods:**
- **价格和混搭。** Premium 每人每月 125 美元，按年付 100 美元；Standard 保持每月 25 美元、按年 20 美元。同一个 workspace 里两种座席可以混着买，管理员随时能升级或者调配。
- **Premium 到底多给什么。** 用量是 Standard 的 5 倍，取消五小时用量上限，改成按周固定重置。
- **撞上限了还能加额度。** 用超了活不用停，workspace 可以加共享额度，由 owner 统一管，账单、用量、花费上限都在一个地方看。
- **首发优惠有名额。** 前 10,000 家符合条件的 ChatGPT Business 客户，每个 Premium 座席送 100 美元 workspace 额度（2,500 credits），最多五个座席共 500 美元；早报名的还可能在正式上线前先拿到。
- **官方说这是客户呼声最高的一条。** OpenAI 表示 Business 客户最想要的就是一档容量更大的座席，理由是他们手上的活越来越重：理库存、搭营销 campaign、做经营分析、用 Codex 在更大的 codebase 上开发。
