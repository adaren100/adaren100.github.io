**[New policy ideas for the Intelligence Age](https://openai.com/index/new-policy-ideas-for-the-intelligence-age)** — _OpenAI · Aug 17_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI is funding 14 projects at independent organizations to develop AI policy ideas around economic opportunity and societal resilience, putting up $1 million in cash plus up to $1 million in API credits total, drawn from more than 400 responses to its call for proposals.

**Main methods:**
- **Follows a stated commitment.** The grants deliver on a promise made when OpenAI published *Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age* in April 2026 — to fund others to test, challenge, and build on those ideas, including challenging OpenAI's own assumptions.
- **Two organizing questions.** How AI can broaden economic opportunity, and how societies can build resilience as capabilities advance.
- **Geographic spread.** Projects span the United States, the European Union, Brazil, Singapore, and South Korea.
- **Mixed deliverables.** Some grantees will produce research and policy models; others will build prototypes, datasets, and frameworks meant to be tested in practice, examining what could work, what it might cost, and how to implement it responsibly.
- **Named examples.** The American Enterprise Institute's AEI-Urban bipartisan commission will build low-, moderate-, and high-disruption scenarios for AI's effect on employment and skills and tie observable disruption indicators to policy playbooks. The Centre for European Policy Studies' "Sharing the AI Dividend" will examine how AI-driven productivity gains are distributed across workers and firms.
- **The reach argument underneath it.** OpenAI cites ChatGPT's 1 billion users across geographies, income levels, and demographics — and claims more adult users both under and over 30 than any other frontier lab — while arguing access alone doesn't decide whether AI serves the many, and that those choices belong to democratic institutions rather than tech companies.

**[给"智能时代"找新政策思路：OpenAI 掏钱资助 14 个独立项目](https://openai.com/index/new-policy-ideas-for-the-intelligence-age)** — _OpenAI · 8月17日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 拿钱资助 14 个独立机构的项目，主题围绕 AI 时代的经济机会和社会韧性，总共 100 万美元现金加最多 100 万美元的 API credits。这轮征集收到了 400 多份提案。

**Main methods:**
- **兑现之前的承诺。** 2026 年 4 月他们发《Industrial Policy for the Intelligence Age》时就说过，会出钱让别人来检验、质疑、延伸这套想法，包括质疑 OpenAI 自己的假设。
- **两个主问题。** 一是 AI 怎么把经济机会做宽，二是能力继续往上走的过程中社会怎么把韧性建起来。
- **地域分布。** 项目覆盖美国、欧盟、巴西、新加坡和韩国。
- **产出形式不一。** 有的团队做研究和政策模型，有的直接做原型、数据集和框架，目标是能拿去实际试，看看哪条路走得通、成本多少、怎么落地才算负责任。
- **点了名的两个例子。** American Enterprise Institute 和 Urban Institute 合办的两党委员会，会把 AI 对就业和技能的冲击拆成低、中、高三档情景，再把可观测的冲击指标和对应的政策 playbook 挂钩。Centre for European Policy Studies 的 "Sharing the AI Dividend" 则去看 AI 带来的生产率红利在工人和企业之间到底怎么分。
- **底下那套叙事。** OpenAI 搬出 ChatGPT 的 10 亿用户，说这些人跨地域、跨收入层、跨人群，还说自己 30 岁以下和 30 岁以上的成年用户数都比其他任何前沿实验室多。但他们也承认，光有 access 决定不了 AI 最终服务的是多数人还是少数人，这类选择应该交给民主制度和公共讨论，而不是科技公司自己拍板。
