**[Working with the American Psychological Association on youth mental health and AI](https://openai.com/index/openai-and-apa-partner-to-advance-responsible-ai)** — _OpenAI · Aug 6_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI is partnering with the American Psychological Association (APA) to ground responsible AI development for young people in psychological science, producing resources for parents, caregivers, clinicians, and schools.

**Main methods:**
- **APA as the evidence base.** APA is the leading scientific and professional psychology organization in the US; its evidence-grounded research will inform what's known, what's uncertain, and what responsible AI should look like for youth.
- **Prior convening set the agenda.** OpenAI and APA earlier co-hosted a session with mental health organizations, researchers, clinicians, educators, and youth representatives to map where young people currently lack support and where AI could responsibly help.
- **Family-facing resources.** Planned materials will help parents and caregivers support healthy AI use, recognize when adult intervention is needed, and reinforce AI as a tool rather than a replacement for people or care — distributed broadly through school communities.
- **Practitioner resources.** Work also targets clinicians and school psychologists, covering how to promote healthy AI usage, spot overreliance, and intervene on unhealthy use patterns.
- **Grounded in lived experience.** The effort is explicitly built on the science of human behavior plus direct input from parents, caregivers, teachers, and young people themselves, not just top-down policy.
- **Framing from OpenAI.** Dr. Sara Johansen, OpenAI's head of mental health and well-being product policy, frames the goal as AI that "strengthens—not replaces" real-world relationships and care young people rely on.

**[OpenAI 联手美国心理学会（APA），一起管好青少年心理健康和 AI 的关系](https://openai.com/index/openai-and-apa-partner-to-advance-responsible-ai)** — _OpenAI · 8月6日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 找上美国心理学会（APA）合作，想把心理学的科学证据真正用到青少年 AI 使用的责任设计上，接下来会给家长、监护人、临床医生和学校都出配套资源。

**Main methods:**
- **APA 提供的是循证底子。** APA 是美国心理学领域最权威的学术和专业组织，他们的研究会帮着厘清哪些是已知的、哪些还不确定，以及面向青少年的负责任 AI 到底该长什么样。
- **前期已经开过一次闭门会。** OpenAI 和 APA 之前联合办过一场活动，拉上心理健康机构、研究者、临床医生、教育工作者和青少年代表，一起梳理年轻人现在到底缺什么支持，AI 又能在哪些地方负责任地帮上忙。
- **面向家庭的资源。** 计划中的材料会帮家长和监护人判断孩子日常怎么健康地用 AI、什么时候该介入，也强调 AI 是工具，不能替代真人陪伴和照护，这些资源打算铺到学校社区里去。
- **面向从业者的资源。** 另一块是给临床医生和学校心理老师用的，教他们怎么推广健康使用习惯、识别过度依赖，以及在不健康的使用模式出现时怎么介入。
- **不是拍脑袋定政策。** 整个项目强调要建立在人类行为科学之上，还要真正听家长、监护人、老师和青少年自己的声音，而不是自上而下定规则。
- **OpenAI 的表态。** 负责心理健康和福祉产品政策的 Dr. Sara Johansen 说，AI 应该"强化而不是取代"青少年本来就依赖的真实人际关系和照护。
