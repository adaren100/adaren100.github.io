**[Virgin Atlantic sharpens customer journeys with ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/virgin-atlantic/chatgpt-work)** — _OpenAI · Aug 10_

**Main takeaway:** A case study: Virgin Atlantic used ChatGPT Work to run the competitive research behind its five-year digital strategy, turning what used to take weeks of manual work into a few hours, and is now building authenticated dashboards on ChatGPT Sites.

**Main methods:**
- **Structured framework first, research second.** The digital product team defined a framework, then had ChatGPT Work research competitor experiences, trace available customer journeys, and organize the findings into a dataset. They kicked it off in the morning and came back a few hours later to evaluate and refine.
- **Output fed a five-year plan.** The analysis showed where Virgin Atlantic's digital experience was already strong, which capabilities it needed to protect, and which opportunities deserved investment; those findings became recommendations in the five-year plan.
- **The journey is wider than booking.** Browsing, purchase, check-in, travel, onboard experience, and post-trip feedback each generate information that sits in different systems and reporting tools, which is what makes a connected view hard.
- **ChatGPT Sites for authenticated dashboards.** Instead of navigating several dashboard tools, authenticated workspaces, licenses, user profiles, and report suites, teams get secure dashboards that pull disparate datasets into one interface.
- **Synthesis across the product lifecycle.** ChatGPT Work is also used to pull together strategy documents, customer insights, and digital experience data, which Head of Digital Product Nathan Bolt credits with getting from idea to shipped product much faster.

**[Virgin Atlantic 用 ChatGPT Work 把客户旅程理顺了](https://openai.com/index/virgin-atlantic/chatgpt-work)** — _OpenAI · 8月10日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇客户案例。Virgin Atlantic 拿 ChatGPT Work 做了支撑五年数字化战略的竞品调研，以前要手工啃好几周的活，现在几个小时就有结果；他们还在用 ChatGPT Sites 搭带登录鉴权的数据看板。

**Main methods:**
- **先定框架，再让它去查。** 数字产品团队先把评估框架定下来，然后让 ChatGPT Work 去研究竞争对手的体验、把能拿到的客户旅程都梳一遍，最后整理成一份可以直接看的数据集。早上把活派下去，几个小时后回来评估和微调就行。
- **结论直接进了五年规划。** 这轮分析理清了自家数字体验哪些地方已经做得不错、哪些能力必须守住、哪些机会值得再投钱，这些结论后来变成了五年计划里的建议。
- **航空公司的客户旅程远不止订票。** 浏览、下单、值机、飞行、机上体验、事后反馈，每一环都在产生数据，但分散在不同系统和不同报表工具里，想拼出一张完整的图很难。
- **用 ChatGPT Sites 做带鉴权的看板。** 以前员工得在好几套看板工具、鉴权工作区、license、用户档案和报表系统之间来回跳，现在把这些散落的数据集拉进同一个界面，各业务线各看各的那块。
- **在整个产品生命周期里做信息汇总。** 战略文档、客户洞察、数字体验数据都能丢进去一起看，Head of Digital Product Nathan Bolt 说，从一个想法到真正交到客户手上的东西，速度快了不少。
