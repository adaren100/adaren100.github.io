**[How Intentful uses dots to make more time for growth](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/intentful-dots)** — _OpenAI · Nov 24_

**Main takeaway:** A customer story, not research: Intentful CEO Marina Petrova describes using her "dot" — a persistent ChatGPT agent she named Aria — to screen roughly 300 client websites, assemble a pricing model, and draft a proposal in hours instead of several days.

**Main methods:**
- **The workflow it replaced.** Intentful is a lean team selling interactive marketing (ads that answer customer questions in real time, tools that make website content readable by AI) into travel and tourism, where custom proposals win the business. Petrova was losing strategic time to research, file-hunting, and deck formatting.
- **Rule-based site screening.** She handed Aria a spreadsheet of URLs plus inclusion criteria — for example, a site had to have a readable sitemap listing no more than 3,000 pages — to decide which of hundreds of local businesses the service package could actually cover.
- **Pricing pulled from internal systems via plugins.** Through ChatGPT plugins, Aria reviewed Intentful's own sales data, including past engagements and HubSpot discussions, surfacing prices from earlier agreements plus context from next year's plans.
- **Human-set model, agent-built artifact.** The two worked out a pricing model together (page counts set the tier, past customer agreements informed the price), then Aria turned it into a one-page proposal that Petrova reviewed and edited. Aria defaults to Intentful's Google Drive templates and can take an earlier proposal as a style reference.
- **It flagged an error nobody asked it to check.** Told to reuse pricing from an older proposal, Aria noticed the original renewal date didn't match what Petrova remembered, raised the discrepancy, and left the date out of the draft.
- **Ambient inbox and channel triage.** Petrova uses the Gmail and Slack plugins in ChatGPT to catch up on conversations, surface open tasks, and see where the team needs help; the company also trains other marketers on using AI this way.

**[Intentful 的 CEO 怎么用 dot 把提案从几天压到几小时](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/intentful-dots)** — _OpenAI · 11月24日_

**Main takeaway:** 这是篇客户案例，不是研究。Intentful 的 CEO Marina Petrova 讲她怎么用自己的 dot（一个常驻的 ChatGPT agent，她给它起名 Aria）筛了大概 300 个客户网站、搭出定价模型、再把提案草稿写出来，整件事从好几天变成几个小时。

**Main methods:**
- **原来时间都耗在哪。** Intentful 是个小团队，做交互式营销（比如能实时回答顾客问题的广告、把网站内容改成 AI 读得懂的工具），主要客户在旅游行业，而拿下单子靠的是定制提案。Petrova 的问题是，做调研、翻文件、调 deck 排版把她做战略的时间吃掉了。
- **拿规则去筛网站。** 她把一张 URL 表格连同筛选标准一起交给 Aria，比如网站得有一份能读的 sitemap、而且页数不超过 3,000 页，用这个判断几百家本地商户里哪些是这套服务包真能覆盖的。
- **定价数据是靠 plugin 从自家系统里捞的。** Aria 通过 ChatGPT 的 plugin 翻了 Intentful 自己的销售数据，包括过往成单和 HubSpot 里的讨论，把之前合同的报价，还有明年计划里的一些背景都拉了出来。
- **模型是人定的，东西是 agent 出的。** 两个人一起把定价模型捋清楚（页数决定档位，过往客户合同也参考进来），然后 Aria 把它变成一页纸的提案，Petrova 自己过一遍再改。Aria 默认会用 Intentful 放在 Google Drive 的模板，你也可以直接丢一份旧提案给它当风格参照。
- **它顺手挑出了一个没人让它查的错。** Petrova 让它沿用一份旧提案的报价，Aria 发现原件上的续约日期跟她印象里的不一样，就把这个对不上的地方提出来，草稿里干脆没填那个日期。
- **消息和任务也交给它盯。** 她用 ChatGPT 里的 Gmail 和 Slack plugin 补会话、找没结的任务、看团队哪里需要搭手。Intentful 本身也在教其他 marketer 这么用 AI。
