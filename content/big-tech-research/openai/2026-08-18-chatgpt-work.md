**[How NVIDIA scales expertise with ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/nvidia/chatgpt-work)** — _OpenAI · Aug 18_

**Main takeaway:** A customer case study on NVIDIA's use of ChatGPT Work, with two headline numbers: ~16 hours saved per week across a 12-week GTC planning cycle, and a weekly workflow that distills 25–40 external AI updates into 5–8 actionable signals.

**Main methods:**
- **Turning spreadsheet prep into a scheduled workflow.** Will Daney's GTC support work — assembling account lists, tracking registrations, identifying needed actions — used to eat roughly 40% of his time in manual analysis; it now runs as an automated ChatGPT Work process twice a week, saving about 16 hours weekly over the 12-week cycle.
- **Ownership is the point.** Because he owns the workflow rather than a purchased tool, he can adapt it as the event changes without waiting for procurement and implementation.
- **Workflows travel between teams.** Colleagues running events in San Jose, Taipei, Europe, and Washington, DC took his workflows and customized them locally — that's the "scaling expertise" in the title.
- **A signal filter over external research.** Rachita Jain (AI operations, marketing) built a workflow that reads trusted external sources alongside internal context, finds the overlap, and surfaces what actually matters to NVIDIA: 25–40 updates in, 5–8 actionable signals out each week. Her framing: it turned "passive reading into active intelligence."
- **One environment for the whole build loop.** The same setup covers idea → approaches → working through a codebase → debugging → refining, without hopping between disconnected tools; OpenAI cites prototypes in days versus 2–3 weeks previously.

**[NVIDIA 怎么用 ChatGPT Work 把个人经验放大成团队流程](https://openai.com/index/nvidia/chatgpt-work)** — _OpenAI · 8月18日_

**Main takeaway:** NVIDIA 用 ChatGPT Work 的客户案例，两个抓眼球的数字：GTC 那 12 周筹备期里每周省下大约 16 小时；另一条 workflow 每周把 25 到 40 条外部 AI 动态压成 5 到 8 条可以直接行动的信号。

**Main methods:**
- **把表格活儿改成定时跑的 workflow。** Will Daney 负责 GTC 期间对 field 团队的支持，整理客户名单、跟注册情况、判断该做哪些动作，以前光手工分析就占掉他 40% 的时间。现在这套在 ChatGPT Work 上一周自动跑两次，12 周的筹备期里每周省下 16 小时左右。
- **关键在于流程是自己的。** 这套东西是他自己搭的，不是买来的工具，活动一变他随手就能改，不用等采购、上线、维护那一整圈。
- **流程能在团队之间传。** 负责 San Jose、台北、欧洲、华盛顿几场活动的同事直接把他的 workflow 拿过去，按本地情况改一改就能用，标题里说的 scale expertise 就是这个意思。
- **给外部研究做了个信号过滤器。** 市场部 AI operations 的 Rachita Jain 搭了条 workflow，把可信的外部信源和内部上下文放一起读，找出重叠的地方，挑出真正跟 NVIDIA 相关的东西：每周进去 25 到 40 条更新，出来 5 到 8 条能行动的信号。她自己的说法是，这把"被动读"变成了"主动情报"。
- **从想法到成品在同一个环境里走完。** 起个念头、试几条路子、翻 codebase、debug、再打磨，中间不用在一堆断开的工具之间来回跳。OpenAI 给的对比是原型从原来的两三周缩到几天。
