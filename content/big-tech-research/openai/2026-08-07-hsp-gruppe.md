**[How HSP GRUPPE builds AI capabilities for tax advisory](https://openai.com/index/hsp-gruppe)** — _OpenAI · Aug 7_

**Main takeaway:** A case study: German tax advisory, audit, and law network HSP GRUPPE rolled ChatGPT Enterprise into a shared workspace covering 81 organizational groups, treating it as an organizational transformation rather than a software deployment and standardizing proven use cases into shared custom Agents.

**Main methods:**
- **The framing question drove the rollout.** Instead of asking how AI could help staff write faster emails or summarize documents, leadership asked what happens when AI becomes part of a professional services firm's operating model.
- **Adoption governance over feature training.** Monthly AI forums give employees a place to trade practical use cases; ChatGPT Enterprise's security features provide the technical base, while client-related information stays governed by HSP's own data protection, confidentiality, and governance rules.
- **Successful use cases become shared Agents.** AI Client Communication supports first drafts, structure, clarity, and a consistent client-facing tone; Booking Assistant SKR03 & SKR04 helps prepare and classify specific booking questions.
- **Professional sign-off stays human.** In every workflow described, final responsibility rests with the relevant tax, legal, or accounting professional — the Agents produce drafts and classifications, not decisions.
- **Two decades of process work underneath.** HSP had standardized processes, embedded quality management, and built a continuous-improvement culture long before generative AI, which the post credits for letting the rollout scale.
- **Headline metrics without values in the body.** The post advertises share of employees reporting higher productivity, ChatGPT conversations over six months (Feb 1 to Jul 14, 2026), and estimated annual hours of extra capacity across the network, but the extracted body doesn't carry the actual figures.

**[HSP GRUPPE 怎么把 AI 能力搭进税务咨询业务](https://openai.com/index/hsp-gruppe)** — _OpenAI · 8月7日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇客户案例。德国的税务咨询、审计和法律事务所网络 HSP GRUPPE 把 ChatGPT Enterprise 铺进了一个覆盖 81 个组织单元的共享 workspace，他们把这事当成组织变革在做，而不是上一套软件，还把跑通的用法固化成了共享的自定义 Agent。

**Main methods:**
- **一开始问的问题就不一样。** 管理层没去想"AI 能不能帮员工把邮件写快点、把文档总结一下"，而是问：当 AI 变成一家专业服务机构运作模式的一部分，会发生什么。
- **重点放在推广机制而不是教功能。** 每月开 AI 论坛，让员工互相交流实际用法；技术底座靠 ChatGPT Enterprise 的安全能力，涉及客户的信息则按 HSP 自己那套数据保护、保密和治理规则来管。
- **好用的场景直接沉淀成共享 Agent。** 一个叫 AI Client Communication，负责起草初稿、理结构、把话说清楚、保持对客户的口径一致；另一个是 Booking Assistant SKR03 & SKR04，帮着准备和归类具体的记账问题。
- **最后签字的还是人。** 文章里提到的每个流程，最终责任都落在对应的税务、法律或会计专业人员身上，Agent 出的是草稿和分类，不是结论。
- **底下垫着二十年的流程建设。** 生成式 AI 出来之前，HSP 就已经把流程标准化、把质量管理嵌进去、养出了持续改进的习惯，文章认为这才是这次能铺开的原因。
- **几个数字只挂了标题没给值。** 帖子在顶部列了"多少比例的员工反馈效率提升""半年内（2026 年 2 月 1 日到 7 月 14 日）的 ChatGPT 对话数""整个网络每年多出来的估算工时"，但抓到的正文里没带具体数值。
