**[OpenAI appoints Dali Rajic as Chief Revenue Officer](https://openai.com/index/dali-rajic-chief-revenue-officer)** — _OpenAI · Aug 13_

**Main takeaway:** A leadership announcement, not research: Dali Rajic joins OpenAI as Chief Revenue Officer to lead the global revenue organization, taking over from Denise Dresser, who is leaving after a transition period. OpenAI says its products now reach more than one billion weekly active users and more than two million businesses — twice as many businesses as a year ago.

**Main methods:**
- **Who he is.** Most recently President and Chief Operating Officer of Wiz, recently acquired by Google, where he helped build the operating discipline behind one of the fastest-growing cyber companies. Before that, President and COO of Zscaler and Chief Customer and Revenue Officer at AppDynamics.
- **The mandate.** OpenAI wants a "revenue operating system" built for the next phase, citing his track record running disciplined, metrics-led revenue organizations that scale globally and sell to both large enterprises and technical buyers.
- **The framing.** OpenAI calls this an inflection point where the next generation of models changes not just how work gets done but how companies are built and run.
- **The handover.** Denise Dresser built the enterprise team, strengthened major customer relationships, and established the commercial foundation; she'll work closely with the business team through the transition before leaving to pursue other opportunities.
- **Outside help on go-to-market.** OpenAI has also formed a strategic partnership with Chad Peets and RPT Partners to build out a world-class go-to-market team.
- **What's not here.** No research, product, or model content — the user and business counts are the only quantitative claims in the post.

**[OpenAI 请来 Dali Rajic 当 CRO](https://openai.com/index/dali-rajic-chief-revenue-officer)** — _OpenAI · 8月13日_

**Main takeaway:** 这是一篇人事公告，跟研究无关：Dali Rajic 加入 OpenAI 出任 Chief Revenue Officer，接手全球营收团队，原来的 Denise Dresser 会在过渡期之后离开。文中提到 OpenAI 的产品现在每周有超过 10 亿活跃用户、200 多万家企业在用，企业数量是一年前的两倍。

**Main methods:**
- **这人什么背景。** 上一份工作是 Wiz 的 President 兼 COO（Wiz 最近被 Google 收购），帮着搭起了这家增长最快的安全公司背后的一整套运营打法。再往前是 Zscaler 的 President 兼 COO，以及 AppDynamics 的 Chief Customer and Revenue Officer。
- **要他来干什么。** OpenAI 想要一套面向下一阶段的"revenue operating system"，看中的是他做那种讲纪律、看指标、能全球铺开的营收组织的经验，大企业客户和技术型客户两头都卖得动。
- **他们自己的叙事。** OpenAI 把现在称作一个拐点：下一代模型改变的不只是工作怎么干，而是公司本身怎么搭、怎么运转。
- **交接情况。** Denise Dresser 把企业团队从零带起来，理顺了大客户关系，也把商业化的底子打好了。她会在过渡期里配合业务团队照看客户，之后离开去做别的事。
- **go-to-market 还找了外援。** OpenAI 同时跟 Chad Peets 和 RPT Partners 建立了战略合作，帮忙搭一支更强的 go-to-market 团队。
- **这篇没有的东西。** 没有任何研究、产品或模型内容，用户数和企业数是全文仅有的两个量化数字。
