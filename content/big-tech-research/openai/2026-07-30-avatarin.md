**[How avatarin built a 24/7 retail agent with GPT-Realtime](https://openai.com/index/avatarin)** — _OpenAI · Jul 30_

**Main takeaway:** avatarin, an AI customer-service company spun out of ANA Holdings, built a 24/7 multilingual voice shopping agent for Japanese retailer Yamada Denki on GPT‑Realtime; roughly 30,000 shoppers used it during a two-week public trial, with 92% of survey responses positive.

**Main methods:**
- **One multimodal model over specialized pipelines.** avatarin chose GPT‑Realtime over separate speech-recognition systems because it handles speech, text, and images together with low latency in a single model.
- **RAG-grounded responses.** A retrieval-augmented generation layer grounds answers in real product data while GPT‑Realtime keeps the voice interaction responsive.
- **Context-driven, not keyword-driven.** The agent listens for a shopper's uncertainty and changing preferences rather than waiting for trigger keywords, aiming for consultative answers like recommending a fridge for a small kitchen and a family of four.
- **Built on prior OpenAI integration.** avatarin had already used the OpenAI API for speech recognition, inquiry analysis, and employee training before taking on this project.
- **Results.** Around 30,000 shoppers engaged during the two-week public campaign on Yamada Denki's online store, and 92% of post-use survey responses were positive.

**[avatarin 怎么用 GPT-Realtime 搭出 24 小时零售客服](https://openai.com/index/avatarin)** — _OpenAI · 7月30日_

**Main takeaway:** avatarin 是从 ANA Holdings 分拆出来的 AI 客服公司，他们用 GPT‑Realtime 给日本家电零售商 Yamada Denki 搭了一个 24 小时多语言语音购物助手，两周的公开试运行里大概有 3 万人用过，92% 的问卷反馈是正面的。

**Main methods:**
- **一个多模态模型顶替多套专用系统。** avatarin 放弃了原来分开的语音识别系统，改用 GPT‑Realtime，因为它能把语音、文字、图片一起处理，延迟还很低。
- **靠 RAG 保证信息准确。** 用检索增强生成（RAG）把回答锚定在真实商品数据上，同时让 GPT‑Realtime 负责保持语音交互的流畅响应。
- **听懂语境而不是等关键词。** 这个助手会听出顾客话里的犹豫和偏好变化，而不是等触发词才回应，目标是给出像"小厨房、四口之家该选哪款冰箱"这种真正有用的建议。
- **不是从零开始。** avatarin 在做这个项目之前，已经用 OpenAI 的 API 做过语音识别、咨询分析和员工培训，算是有积累。
- **实测结果。** 在 Yamada Denki 官网两周的公开活动里，大概 3 万人用过这个助手，用后问卷里 92% 的反馈是正面的。
