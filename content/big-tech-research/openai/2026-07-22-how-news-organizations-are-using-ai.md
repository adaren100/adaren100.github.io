**[How news organizations are using AI to advance their vital missions](https://openai.com/index/how-news-organizations-are-using-ai)** — _OpenAI · Jul 22_

**Main takeaway:** A customer-case-study roundup on how newsrooms like AP, POLITICO, and Axios use OpenAI tools for reporting, verification, and business operations, alongside continued OpenAI funding for journalism nonprofits like the American Journalism Project, Lenfest Institute, and WAN-IFRA.

**Main methods:**
- **AP's verification pipeline.** Tools help scan overnight news and podcasts for reportable developments, verify images/video through upload tracing, geolocation, and chronolocation, and turn thousands of Supreme Court filings into searchable structured data.
- **AP's newsroom-to-business chain.** Also used to surface story leads in government datasets, recommend member content to editors, build audience metrics reports, and convert articles into broadcast scripts.
- **POLITICO's dual use.** Journalists use AI to analyze large volumes of public documents for original reporting, while commercial teams use it to tailor client sales experiences with richer data.
- **Axios's custom GPTs.** A "FOIA Refiner GPT" helps reporters draft sound open-records requests less likely to be denied, and a caption-optimizing GPT enforces the outlet's Smart Brevity style.
- **Ongoing nonprofit funding.** OpenAI renewed support for the American Journalism Project's portfolio spanning 38 states, plus continued backing of the Lenfest Institute and WAN-IFRA.

**[新闻机构都在用 AI 做什么](https://openai.com/index/how-news-organizations-are-using-ai)** — _OpenAI · 7月22日_

**Main takeaway:** 这是一篇客户案例合集，讲 AP、POLITICO、Axios 这些新闻机构怎么用 OpenAI 的工具做报道、做核实、做business，同时 OpenAI 也在持续资助 American Journalism Project、Lenfest Institute、WAN-IFRA 这些新闻业公益组织。

**Main methods:**
- **AP 的核实流水线。** 用工具扫夜间新闻和播客找可报道的线索，通过 upload tracing、地理定位、时间定位来核实图片和视频，还把几千份最高法院文件转成可搜索的结构化数据。
- **AP 从采编延伸到business。** 同一套工具也用来在政府数据集里挖故事线索、给编辑推荐会员内容、做受众数据报告，甚至把文章转成播报稿。
- **POLITICO 两头都用。** 记者用 AI 分析大量公开文档做深度报道，商业团队则用它给客户做更有数据支撑的销售方案。
- **Axios 的定制 GPT。** "FOIA Refiner GPT" 帮记者写出更不容易被拒的公开记录申请，另一个 GPT 专门优化配文，保证符合他们家 Smart Brevity 的风格。
- **持续资助新闻业。** OpenAI 续签了对 American Journalism Project 旗下覆盖 38 个州的媒体组合的支持，也继续资助 Lenfest Institute 和 WAN-IFRA。
