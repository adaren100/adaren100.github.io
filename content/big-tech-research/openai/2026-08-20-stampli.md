**[Stampli cuts launch hours by 68% using ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/stampli)** — _OpenAI · Aug 20_

**Main takeaway:** A customer case study: procure-to-pay company Stampli used Codex and ChatGPT Work to take its Deep Finance launch from an estimated 243 modeled active role-hours down to about 77 — roughly 166 hours saved, or 3.16× faster production — while keeping human review and final approval on everything customer-facing.

**Main methods:**
- **Codex as the shared context layer.** Stampli's marketing team wired product context, meeting notes, decisions, and messaging guidelines into one system that Codex works from, rather than briefing each asset separately.
- **Prototype to shipped product in about six weeks.** Deep Finance went from an initial prototype demo to a public GTM launch and first shipped product in roughly six weeks, with design resources and outside contractors already committed elsewhere.
- **Breadth of assets from one pipeline.** Codex turned evolving product decisions into review-ready output across a seven-part blog series, launch emails, a webinar and its deck, social and paid creative, a PR Newswire release, the Deep Finance web page, and sales enablement material.
- **Roughly 90% of the hero animation.** Codex handled exploration, iteration, and packaging for the launch animation; a contractor only finished the opening scene and final format.
- **The same infrastructure runs day to day, not just at launch.** Keeping product materials current used to mean interviewing PMs, reading Jira tickets, reviewing GitHub, and working through meeting notes; a GPT-powered system now gathers from those sources and helps keep help center articles, decks, and one-pagers up to date.
- **Human approval stayed in the loop.** The 68% reduction is measured against modeled role-hours for a defined go-to-market workflow, with full human review on customer-facing work — an internal estimate, not an independently audited number.

**[Stampli 用 ChatGPT Work 和 Codex 把一次产品发布的工时砍掉 68%](https://openai.com/index/stampli)** — _OpenAI · 8月20日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇客户案例：做 procure-to-pay 的 Stampli 拿 Codex 和 ChatGPT Work 做 Deep Finance 的发布，原本估算要 243 个"角色工时"，实际只花了大概 77 个，省下 166 小时左右，产出速度快了 3.16 倍，而且所有对外的东西还是人来 review 和最终拍板。

**Main methods:**
- **Codex 当共享的上下文层用。** 市场团队把产品背景、会议记录、各种决策和 messaging 规范都接进一个系统，Codex 直接从这里取料，不用每做一份物料就重新交代一遍。
- **从原型到正式发货六周左右。** Deep Finance 从最早的原型 demo 到公开 GTM 发布加首个上线版本，前后大概六周，而且当时设计资源和外包都被别的项目占着。
- **一条流水线出了一整套物料。** 七篇连载博客、发布邮件、webinar 和配套的 deck、社交和投放素材、PR Newswire 通稿、Deep Finance 落地页、销售 enablement 材料，全是 Codex 把不断变动的产品决策转成能直接过审的稿子。
- **发布主视觉动画九成是它做的。** 从探索、迭代到打包输出，polished animation 里 90% 左右由 Codex 完成，外包只补了开场镜头和最终格式。
- **这套东西平时也在跑，不是只为发布搭的。** 以前想让产品资料保持最新，得挨个采访 PM、翻 Jira、看 GitHub、再啃会议记录；现在一个 GPT 驱动的系统会自己去这些源头抓，帮着把帮助中心文章、演示文稿、one-pager 更新住。
- **人始终在链路里。** 68% 这个数字是拿一套定好的 go-to-market 流程按"角色工时"建模算出来的，对外内容全程有人 review。需要注意的是，这属于客户自己的估算，不是第三方审计过的数。
