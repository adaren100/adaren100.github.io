**[Apple is getting this wrong](https://openai.com/index/apple-is-getting-this-wrong)** — _OpenAI · Aug 4_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI published a rebuttal to Apple's lawsuit accusing former Apple employees Chang Liu and Tang Tan of misusing Apple trade secrets, disputing Apple's timeline of events and calling the suit baseless.

**Main methods:**
- **Disputes the contact timeline.** OpenAI says Apple's outside counsel emailed the wrong person in February after confusing two Asian last names, and that Apple now admits a claimed conversation with OpenAI's General Counsel never actually happened.
- **Chang Liu allegation.** Apple accuses Liu of accessing confidential information after leaving the company, but OpenAI says Apple's own employees contacted him post-departure asking for help locating files — attributing this to Apple's own pattern of failing to revoke system access when employees leave.
- **Tang Tan allegation.** Apple accuses Tan, a 24-year Apple veteran, of trying to obtain and use its trade secrets; OpenAI says Tan has been told he must not use any other company's confidential information and denies wrongdoing.
- **Evidence published.** OpenAI released iMessages and email correspondence between the parties to back its account of what happened and when.
- **The ask.** OpenAI calls Apple's request for a preliminary injunction unnecessary and based on false information, saying it neither has nor wants Apple's trade secrets, and accuses Apple of shifting its narrative after being confronted with the facts.

**[OpenAI：苹果这次搞错了](https://openai.com/index/apple-is-getting-this-wrong)** — _OpenAI · 8月4日_

**Main takeaway:** OpenAI 发文反驳苹果那起指控前苹果员工 Chang Liu 和 Tang Tan 挪用苹果商业机密的诉讼，逐条质疑苹果给出的事件时间线，说这起诉讼站不住脚。

**Main methods:**
- **时间线对不上。** OpenAI 说苹果的外部律师 2 月份把两个亚裔姓氏搞混了，邮件发错了人；苹果现在也承认，他们说的那次跟 OpenAI 法务总监的对话其实根本没发生过。
- **关于 Chang Liu 的指控。** 苹果说 Liu 离职后还访问过机密信息，但 OpenAI 说是苹果自己的员工在他走了以后主动联系他，请他帮忙找文件——这其实是苹果自己没做好离职权限回收的老问题。
- **关于 Tang Tan 的指控。** 苹果说这位在苹果干了 24 年的老将想拿走并使用苹果的商业机密；OpenAI 说一直都跟 Tan 讲清楚不能用任何其他公司的机密信息，否认有任何不当行为。
- **直接放证据。** OpenAI 把双方之间的 iMessage 聊天记录和邮件往来都公开了，用来佐证自己这版说法。
- **诉求上的分歧。** OpenAI 说苹果申请的临时禁令既没必要又是建立在错误信息上的，强调自己既没有也不想要苹果的商业机密，还说苹果在被拿出事实后一直在改口。
