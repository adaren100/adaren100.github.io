**[Reviewing the evidence on worker retraining programs](https://www.anthropic.com/research/reviewing-the-evidence-on-worker-retraining-programs)** — _Anthropic · Aug 12_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic's Economic Research team published an evidence review, coauthored by independent researcher David Roodman and Anthropic's Maxim Massenkoff, finding that job training programs raise employment by only 2–3 percentage points and earnings by roughly $1,000 a year per training slot offered — too small to absorb labor market disruption from AI at scale.

**Main methods:**
- **New meta-analysis of 56 randomized US studies.** The review combines 56 randomized American trials in a new meta-analysis, alongside experimental evidence from Europe.
- **The core numbers.** For each person offered a training slot, employment rises 2–3 percentage points and earnings by about $1,000 a year, against a cost of roughly $13,000.
- **Fiscal accounting.** Counting added tax revenue and reduced benefit payments, the government recovers more than half of what it spends, and programs roughly break even overall.
- **Sector programs are the exception that doesn't replicate.** Programs that partner with employers in a high-demand industry and place people directly into jobs in that field produce gains several times larger, but attempts to replicate them have often failed.
- **The conclusion for AI policy.** Retraining is the most popular proposed remedy for AI-driven labor disruption, yet on this evidence existing programs would likely fall short if displacement happens at scale.
- **Recommendation.** Invest now in demonstrating, evaluating, and scaling the most promising programs, including rapidly expanding one leading program for a specific group of workers and rigorously measuring the results; Anthropic's Economic Futures Research Fund is set up to fund exactly this kind of investigation.

**[再培训真接得住 AI 冲击吗？Anthropic 把 56 项随机实验捋了一遍](https://www.anthropic.com/research/reviewing-the-evidence-on-worker-retraining-programs)** — _Anthropic · 8月12日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 的 Economic Research 团队拉上独立研究者 David Roodman，把工人再培训项目的证据系统梳理了一遍，作者之一是 Anthropic 的 Maxim Massenkoff。结论不太乐观：每提供一个培训名额，就业率只提高 2 到 3 个百分点、年收入涨大约 1,000 美元，这个量级根本撑不住 AI 可能带来的大规模岗位冲击。

**Main methods:**
- **新做了一份 56 项美国随机实验的 meta-analysis。** 把 56 项美国的随机对照研究合起来重新算了一遍，再搭上欧洲那边的实验证据一起看。
- **几个核心数字。** 按每个拿到培训名额的人算：就业率 +2 到 3 个百分点，年收入 +1,000 美元左右，而成本大约 13,000 美元。
- **算上财政这笔账。** 把多收的税和少发的福利算进去，政府能收回一半以上的支出，整体大致打平。
- **只有 sector program 是例外，但复制不了。** 那种直接跟高需求行业的雇主合作、培训完就把人送进对口岗位的项目，效果要好上好几倍，可惜后来想照着复制的尝试大多没成。
- **对 AI 政策意味着什么。** 再培训是眼下最受欢迎的应对方案，但照这些证据看，真到大规模替代发生的时候，现有的项目多半接不住。
- **他们的建议。** 现在就投钱去做示范、评估和规模化，包括挑一个针对特定人群的优秀项目快速铺开、同时严格测量效果。Anthropic 的 Economic Futures Research Fund 就是为资助这类研究设的。
