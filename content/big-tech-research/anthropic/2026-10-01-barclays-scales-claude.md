**[Barclays scales Claude to upgrade operations and improve client experience](https://www.anthropic.com/news/barclays-scales-claude)** — _Anthropic · Oct 1_

**Main takeaway:** A customer announcement: Barclays is expanding its strategic collaboration with Anthropic to roll Claude out across the bank's global operations, and expects Claude Code adoption to reach 50% of its developer population by the end of 2026 and a majority of its software engineers in 2027.

**Main methods:**
- **Where Claude is being pointed.** Accelerating software development, modernizing legacy systems, and improving operational efficiency across a large, highly regulated organization.
- **Colleague Knowledge Assistant.** Live since 2025 and powered by Claude through a retrieval-augmented generation architecture, it helps Barclays UK staff find answers while supporting over 20 million UK retail customers. More than 16,000 colleagues have adopted it, and it has handled over one million searches.
- **Email triage in Global Markets.** Claude models classify incoming client inquiries, enrich them, and determine the optimal processing route, also checking that requests carry the information operations colleagues need to act. The platform handles roughly 120,000 emails a day.
- **The framing from Barclays' side.** Group Co-Chief Operating Officer Anne Marie Darling describes the goal as transforming how work gets done — reimagining processes inside a secure and well-governed environment so colleagues spend less time on routine activity.
- **Agentic AI in the engineering stack.** Group Co-Chief Operating Officer Craig Bright frames software engineering and cyber security as both being reshaped by more capable AI, with Barclays moving toward AI as an agentic capability embedded in how it builds, tests, secures, and operates technology.
- **Governance as the gating factor.** The post is explicit that integrating AI at this scale needs disciplined governance alongside capable technology, not just model access.

**[Barclays 把 Claude 铺到全行](https://www.anthropic.com/news/barclays-scales-claude)** — _Anthropic · 10月1日_

**Main takeaway:** 一篇客户案例。英国的 Barclays 把跟 Anthropic 的合作往上提了一档，要把 Claude 铺到全球业务里。他们的目标是 2026 年底让 Claude Code 的使用率覆盖一半开发者，2027 年覆盖大多数软件工程师。

**Main methods:**
- **主要拿来干什么。** 加快软件开发、把老系统现代化、提升运营效率，而且是在一个体量大、监管又严的组织里做这些事。
- **Colleague Knowledge Assistant。** 2025 年就上线了，底下是 Claude 配一套 retrieval-augmented generation 架构，帮英国的员工在服务 2,000 多万零售客户时快速查到答案。现在有 16,000 多名员工在用，累计处理了一百多万次检索。
- **Global Markets 的邮件分流。** Claude 把进来的客户问询做分类、补充信息、判断走哪条处理路径，顺手还检查这条请求里有没有运营同事动手所需的信息。这套平台每天过大约 12 万封邮件。
- **Barclays 自己怎么说。** Group Co-Chief Operating Officer Anne Marie Darling 的说法是，重点不是"用上 AI"，而是把活干的方式换掉：在安全、治理到位的环境里重新设计流程，让同事少花时间在例行事务上。
- **AI 以 agent 的形态进到工程链路里。** 另一位 Group Co-Chief Operating Officer Craig Bright 认为软件工程和 cyber security 都在被更强的 AI 重塑，Barclays 的方向是把 AI 当成一种 agentic 能力嵌到构建、测试、加固和运维技术的流程里。
- **治理是前提。** 文章说得很直接：在这个规模上接入 AI，光有能打的技术不够，还得有扎实的 governance。
