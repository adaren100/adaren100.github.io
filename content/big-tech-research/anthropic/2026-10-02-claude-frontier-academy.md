**[Anthropic invests $100 million to train 10,000 engineers and tackle the enterprise AI talent gap](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy)** — _Anthropic · Oct 2_

**Main takeaway:** A programs announcement: Anthropic is launching Claude Frontier Academy, backed by a $100 million commitment, to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by the end of 2027 to the same skill standard as its own engineers. First cohorts come from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk.

**Main methods:**
- **The diagnosis.** Anthropic's read is that the bottleneck in enterprise AI is talent, not model access: inside the companies they work with, a small group of deeply skilled people drives an outsized share of what AI actually delivers.
- **Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency.** The Academy's first program, built on the medical training model: engineers learn from practitioners, practice on realistic cases, and are assessed on their work before earning the credential. The stated goal is agentic systems that change how a business runs, not demos.
- **Two-stage structure.** A multi-day in-person program with Anthropic engineers and licensed instructors walks through a simulated enterprise deployment, from picking the use case through security review to handover, ending in a graded practical on a fresh scenario. Passing earns the Claude Resident Engineer badge and entry to a 12-week residency.
- **The residency is real work, not coursework.** Each engineer leads an actual Claude use case at their own organization, supported by Anthropic engineers and their cohort, and is assessed again at the end of the 12 weeks.
- **Selection criteria.** Aimed at hands-on software engineers with strong fundamentals, a track record building with LLMs, and experience helping others adopt AI. Organizations nominate their strongest people, and each arrives with a named Claude project they'll lead on return; prior agent-building experience isn't required.
- **How Anthropic frames the bet.** Steve Corfield, Global Head of Business Development and Partnerships, argues no AI company has invested in developing this talent inside its customers and partners at this depth, and wants graduates to set the standard for how AI gets built inside a business.

**[Anthropic 砸 1 亿美元，要培训 1 万名企业 AI 工程师](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy)** — _Anthropic · 10月2日_

**Main takeaway:** 一个培训项目的发布。Anthropic 拿出 1 亿美元搞 Claude Frontier Academy，打算到 2027 年底培养出 1 万名 Frontier Deployed Engineer（FDE），标准跟他们自己的工程师对齐。第一批学员来自 Accenture、Bain、Capgemini、Commonwealth Bank of Australia、Deloitte、McKinsey、Morgan Stanley 和 Novo Nordisk。

**Main methods:**
- **他们对问题的判断。** Anthropic 认为企业落地 AI 卡住的地方不是拿不到模型，而是没人。在他们服务的那些公司里，真正把 AI 跑出效果的往往就是一小撮特别能打的人。
- **Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency。** Academy 的第一个项目，直接照搬医学培养路径：跟着真正在做的人学，拿接近真实的案例练手，考核过了才发证。目标说得很明白，是要做能改变业务运转方式的 agentic 系统，不是做 demo。
- **分两段走。** 先是几天的线下集训，带课的是 Anthropic 工程师和持证讲师，整个走一遍模拟的企业部署流程：从挑用例到过 security review 再到交接，最后拿一个没见过的新场景做实操考核。过了就拿 Claude Resident Engineer 徽章，进下一阶段。
- **第二阶段是真干活。** 12 周的 residency 里，每个人回自己公司领一个真实的 Claude 用例，Anthropic 的工程师和同期学员一起帮着推，12 周结束再考一次。
- **招什么人。** 要动手型的软件工程师，基本功扎实、拿 LLM 做过东西、还带过别人用 AI。公司推荐自己最强的人来，而且每个人来的时候就得带着一个回去要领的 Claude 项目；之前没做过 agent 不要紧。
- **Anthropic 自己怎么看这笔投入。** 负责 Business Development and Partnerships 的 Steve Corfield 说，还没有哪家 AI 公司在客户和合作方内部把人才培养做到这个深度，他希望从这里毕业的人能定下"企业内部该怎么做 AI"的标准。
