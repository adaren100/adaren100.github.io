**[Ask Claude about the Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-economic-index-connector)** — _Anthropic · Jul 21_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic launches a claude.ai connector for the Anthropic Economic Index, letting anyone ask Claude natural-language questions about how AI is actually being used across occupations, regions, and tasks, grounded directly in the Index's data.

**Main methods:**
- **Connector, not a new dataset.** Surfaces the existing Anthropic Economic Index — which tracks real patterns in Claude usage across the economy — through a conversational interface rather than requiring users to dig through raw reports.
- **Example queries.** Users can ask things like "Which occupations use AI the most?", "What are the most common ways people in Colorado use Claude?", or "How has task automation changed over the past year?"
- **One-minute setup.** Available today by enabling the connector from claude.ai's connectors menu; works in any conversation with any Claude model, no separate install.
- **Drill-down workflow.** Designed for starting broad ("What does the Index say about my industry?") then narrowing to specifics, with Claude able to show the underlying data behind any answer.
- **Stated limitation.** Anthropic notes the Index reflects patterns in Claude usage specifically, not the labor market as a whole, and Claude will surface that caveat plus links to source data during use.

**[让 Claude 直接查 Anthropic Economic Index](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-economic-index-connector)** — _Anthropic · 7月21日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 上线了一个 claude.ai 的 connector，直接把 Anthropic Economic Index 接进对话，任何人都能用大白话问 Claude AI 在各个职业、地区、任务上到底是怎么被用的，答案都直接来自 Index 的数据。

**Main methods:**
- **是个 connector，不是新数据集。** 把已有的 Anthropic Economic Index（追踪 Claude 在整个经济体里的真实使用模式）接进对话界面，不用再自己去啃原始报告。
- **举例能问什么。** 比如"哪些职业最爱用 AI？""科罗拉多州的人都拿 Claude 做什么？""过去一年任务自动化的趋势变了多少？"这类问题。
- **一分钟就能配好。** 现在就能用，在 claude.ai 的 connectors 菜单里打开就行，任何 Claude 模型、任何对话里都能用，不用额外安装。
- **从粗到细的用法。** 设计上是先问个大问题（比如"Index 里我这个行业的情况是怎样的？"），再逐步细化，Claude 还能把答案背后的原始数据展示出来。
- **明确说了局限。** Anthropic 强调 Index 反映的是 Claude 使用模式，不代表整个劳动力市场，用的时候 Claude 也会主动提醒这一点并附上数据来源。
