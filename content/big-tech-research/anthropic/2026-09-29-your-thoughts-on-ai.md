**[What do you want from AI?](https://www.anthropic.com/research/your-thoughts-on-ai)** — _Anthropic · Sep 29_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is opening a new public-input study run through Anthropic Interviewer, asking people about their most meaningful experiences with AI and what they want from AI companies. Unlike the December study of 81,000 participants, this one lets each participant choose to publish their complete interview so anyone, not just Anthropic, can read it.

**Main methods:**
- **Anthropic Interviewer as the instrument.** The interview asks three things: your most meaningful AI experiences, positive and negative; anything about how work, school, healthcare, or government operates that you'd want AI to help change; and what you want from the companies building AI, Anthropic included.
- **Opt-in public transcripts.** After finishing, you decide whether to make the interview public. What gets published is the complete interview plus the country associated with the conversation, with Claude account information such as name and email excluded.
- **Builds on the December study.** That earlier round collected hopes and worries from 81,000 people, shaped the Anthropic Institute's agenda, was presented at the World Economic Forum, and still guides Anthropic's Societal Impacts and Economics research.
- **Why publish transcripts this time.** Only high-level results and a handful of quotes came out of the previous study. Publishing full interviews lets researchers and policymakers read responses in their own languages and by region, ask questions Anthropic didn't think to ask, and check whether the published findings actually match what participants said.
- **The framing.** Anthropic argues capabilities now cut both ways, with frontier AI accelerating science and medicine while a single security incident does far more damage than it did a year ago, so weighing benefits against risks shouldn't be left to AI companies alone.
- **Consent handled up front.** A FAQ accompanies the study so participants can decide, before starting, whether to go public and how much to share.

**[你希望 AI 变成什么样？Anthropic 想听你说](https://www.anthropic.com/research/your-thoughts-on-ai)** — _Anthropic · 9月29日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 又开了一轮公众访谈研究，用他们自己的 Anthropic Interviewer 来跑，问的是你和 AI 之间最有感触的经历，以及你希望做 AI 的公司做到什么。跟去年 12 月那次 81,000 人的研究不一样，这次每个参与者可以自己决定要不要把完整访谈公开，公开之后谁都能读，不只是 Anthropic。

**Main methods:**
- **访谈怎么问。** 三个问题：你和 AI 之间印象最深的经历，好的坏的都算；工作、学校、医疗、政府这些领域的运作方式里，你希望 AI 帮着改掉哪些；以及你希望做 AI 的公司（包括 Anthropic）做到什么。
- **公开与否你自己选。** 访谈做完才让你决定要不要公开。公开的内容是完整访谈加上对话所属的国家，Claude 账号信息比如姓名和邮箱都不会带出去。
- **接着 12 月那轮做。** 上一轮收了 81,000 人对 AI 的期待和担忧，直接影响了 Anthropic Institute 的研究议题，还拿到世界经济论坛上讲过，现在仍然在给他们的 Societal Impacts 和 Economics 研究定方向。
- **这次为什么要公开全文。** 上一轮只放了高层级结论和少量引语。放出完整访谈之后，各国研究者和政策制定者能用自己的语言读、按地区读，问一些 Anthropic 没想到要问的问题，也能自己核对 Anthropic 发布的结论跟参与者原话对不对得上。
- **他们的理由。** Anthropic 的说法是现在能力这件事两头都在放大，frontier AI 一边在加速科学和医学上的发现，一边一次安全事故造成的损失比一年前大得多，所以怎么权衡收益和风险不该由 AI 公司自己拍板。
- **知情同意放在前面。** 配了一份 FAQ，让人在开始之前就想清楚要不要公开、愿意分享到什么程度。
