**[Expanding our support for scientists](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-support-for-scientists)** — _Anthropic · Aug 27_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is opening 10,000 seats on a new Claude team plan for scientists — standard seats free for a year, premium seats with 5× usage limits at $15/month — and widening its AI for Science credit program beyond biology, with up to $50,000 in credits per project.

**Main methods:**
- **Who qualifies for a seat.** You must be a principal investigator or equivalent at an academic or nonprofit research institution; once verified, you add the researchers in your lab to the plan. Anthropic says it intends to go well past the initial 10,000 seats over the coming months.
- **Credits sit on top of the subscription.** When a lab burns through what the standard or premium plan allows, it can apply to AI for Science for up to $50,000 in credits per project, and any researcher is eligible to apply.
- **AI for Science broadens past biology.** The program has largely funded biological sciences so far; it now wants compute-heavy work in other fields, citing Claude's results on the Riemann zeta function and on protein design as the shape of project it's after.
- **Claude Science is the product layer.** Launched in June, it integrates the tools researchers most commonly use, produces auditable artifacts, and gives flexible access to computing resources.
- **Dual-use gating is stated explicitly.** Biology and chemistry researchers remain limited to Opus-class models, and Claude Fable models continue to block professional biology and drug development queries over dual-use risk.
- **A government-linked exception is starting up.** Anthropic is working with the US government on an access program letting life sciences professionals use Mythos-class models for research and development, and says it has enrolled its first participants.

**[Anthropic 给科研圈开 1 万个座位](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-support-for-scientists)** — _Anthropic · 8月27日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 新开一个面向科学家的 Claude team plan，放出 1 万个座位，标准席位一年免费，premium 席位用量上限翻 5 倍、每月 15 美元。同时 AI for Science 的 credit 计划不再只盯着生物，单个项目最多能拿 5 万美元额度。

**Main methods:**
- **谁能领座位。** 得是学术或非营利研究机构的 PI 或同等身份，验证通过之后可以把自己实验室的人加进来。Anthropic 说接下来几个月会把规模做到远不止 1 万。
- **credit 是叠在订阅之上的。** 实验室把标准或 premium 套餐的额度用完之后，可以再去申请 AI for Science，单项目最多 5 万美元 credit，任何研究者都能申请。
- **AI for Science 不再只做生物。** 以前资源主要给生物方向，现在明确想覆盖别的学科，尤其是吃算力的那种研究，官方拿 Claude 之前在 Riemann zeta 函数和蛋白质设计上的进展当参照。
- **Claude Science 是产品那一层。** 6月上线，把研究者本来最常用的工具整合进来，产出可审计的 artifact，算力资源也能灵活调。
- **dual-use 的口子卡得很明确。** 做生物和化学的研究者暂时只能用 Opus 级别的模型，Claude Fable 系列会继续拦掉专业生物和药物研发相关的问题，理由就是 dual-use 风险。
- **不过已经在开例外通道。** Anthropic 正跟美国政府一起搭一个准入项目，让生命科学的专业人士能用上 Mythos 级模型做研发，他们说第一批参与者已经进来了。
