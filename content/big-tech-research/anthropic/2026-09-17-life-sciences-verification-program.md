**[Introducing the Life Sciences Verification Program](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program)** — _Anthropic · Sep 16_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic opened applications for the Life Sciences Verification Program (LSVP), which gives vetted life science professionals access to Mythos, Opus, and Sonnet with classifiers deliberately relaxed for biology work that the generally available Fable models block. Dozens of organizations already came through an early-access program; the public beta starts with teams and institutions.

**Main methods:**
- **Vetting before access.** Every applicant goes through a verification process covering research credentials, security standards, and ethical research oversight before any grant is issued.
- **Two grant tiers.** "Standard Use" covers most biology R&D and can be extended to a whole team for daily workloads, renewed annually; "High-risk Use" is a per-project add-on, renewed every six months.
- **What Standard Use unblocks.** Drug discovery, research biology, clinical development, manufacturing, plus supply chain, QA, regulatory affairs, and investing/diligence — with refined classifiers more permissive than the general models. It applies to Mythos 5.1, Opus 5, and Sonnet 5 today and to future models as they ship.
- **High-risk removes the life sciences blocks entirely.** It's scoped to a single research project (the example given: characterizing how one family of viral vectors is recognized by human immune pathways), so a dual-use researcher typically holds one Standard grant plus one or more High-risk grants.
- **Mythos stays gated at the high-risk tier.** High-risk grants are available today for Opus 5 and Sonnet 5; Anthropic is working with the US government to widen Mythos high-risk access, which at launch is limited to a small set of entities with extra vetting.
- **Everything else stays on.** Non-biology safeguards such as the cyber classifiers remain in place under all LSVP grants, and access works across Claude Science, Claude.ai, Claude Code, and the API. The extracted body cuts off in the section on shared responsibility and misuse.

**[Anthropic 开放 Life Sciences Verification Program](https://www.anthropic.com/news/life-sciences-verification-program)** — _Anthropic · 9月16日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 正式开放 Life Sciences Verification Program（LSVP）申请：通过审核的生命科学从业者可以用上 Mythos、Opus 和 Sonnet，而且 classifier 专门为生物方向放松过，那些在通用 Fable 模型上会被拦下来的请求现在能过。早期内测已经接了几十家机构，这次公测先面向团队和研究机构。

**Main methods:**
- **先验身份再给权限。** 每个申请方都要过一轮审核，看研究资质、安全规范，还有伦理审查这块有没有人管。
- **两档 grant。** "Standard Use"覆盖日常的生物 R&D，可以开给整个团队，一年一续；"High-risk Use"是加购性质的，绑定到单个项目，半年一续。
- **Standard Use 放开了什么。** drug discovery、研究生物学、临床开发、生产制造，再加上供应链、QA、法规事务、投资尽调这些场景，用的是比通用模型宽松的 classifier。现在覆盖 Mythos 5.1、Opus 5 和 Sonnet 5，以后发的新模型也算。
- **High-risk 是把生命科学这块的拦截整个拿掉。** 它只对一个具体项目生效，官方举的例子是研究某一类 viral vector 怎么被人体免疫通路识别。所以做 dual-use 研究的人通常手上是一个 Standard grant 管日常，外加一个或几个 High-risk grant 管具体项目。
- **Mythos 在高风险档还是卡着。** High-risk grant 今天只对 Opus 5 和 Sonnet 5 开放，Mythos 这边 Anthropic 正在跟美国政府一起谈怎么放宽，发布时只给了极少数经过额外审核的机构。
- **其他 safeguard 一个没动。** cyber classifier 这类非生物方向的防护在 LSVP 下照常生效。权限在 Claude Science、Claude.ai、Claude Code 和 API 上都通用。抓到的正文在讲 shared responsibility 和滥用那段就断了。
