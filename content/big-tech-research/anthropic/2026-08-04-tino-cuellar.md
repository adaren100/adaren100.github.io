**[Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar to join Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tino-cuellar)** — _Anthropic · Aug 4_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic named Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar as its first Chief Global Affairs Officer, leading policy, international engagement, and government relationships worldwide; he is stepping down from his seat on Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust to take the role.

**Main methods:**
- **Legal and policy background.** Former Justice of the Supreme Court of California and past President of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, plus prior directorships of Stanford's Freeman Spogli Institute and Stanford Cyber Initiative.
- **Federal and international experience.** Served on the President's Intelligence Advisory Board and the State Department's Foreign Affairs Policy Board, and worked in the White House and federal agencies across three presidential administrations.
- **AI-specific policy work.** Co-led California's Frontier AI Working Group, sat on the National Academy of Sciences' Committee on Responsible Computing Research, and has taught AI-focused classes at Stanford Law for nearly a decade.
- **Prior tie to Anthropic.** Cuéllar has served as a Trustee of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust since January 2026 and is stepping down from that seat to join as an executive; the Trust will select a successor through its normal process.
- **Framing from leadership.** Both Cuéllar and Daniela Amodei describe the hire as coming at a "pivotal moment" for AI governance, with Cuéllar arguing that democracies must set the terms on which the technology advances.

**[Tino Cuéllar 加入 Anthropic，出任首席全球事务官](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tino-cuellar)** — _Anthropic · 8月4日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 请来 Mariano-Florentino（Tino）Cuéllar 出任公司历史上第一位首席全球事务官，主管政策、国际事务和全球政府关系；他会从 Anthropic 长期利益信托（Long-Term Benefit Trust）的受托人席位上卸任，转而加入公司当高管。

**Main methods:**
- **法律和政策背景很扎实。** 他做过加州最高法院大法官，也当过 Carnegie Endowment for International Peace 的院长，之前还是斯坦福 Freeman Spogli Institute 和 Stanford Cyber Initiative 的主任。
- **联邦和国际层面也有履历。** 他在总统情报顾问委员会和国务院对外政策委员会都任过职，还在三届总统任期内的白宫和联邦机构工作过。
- **AI 政策上也不是新手。** 他共同牵头了加州的 Frontier AI Working Group，也是美国国家科学院 Responsible Computing Research 委员会的成员，在斯坦福法学院教 AI 相关的课已经快十年了。
- **跟 Anthropic 早有渊源。** 他从 2026 年 1 月起就是 Anthropic 长期利益信托的受托人，这次是从这个席位上卸任转去当高管，信托会按正常流程选一个接任者。
- **双方的表态。** Cuéllar 和 Daniela Amodei 都把这次任命说成是 AI 治理的"关键节点"，Cuéllar 强调这项技术往哪个方向走，得由民主国家自己说了算。
