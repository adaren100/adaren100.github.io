**[Funding better evaluations of AI’s impact on wellbeing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/wellbeing-research-grants)** — _Anthropic · Aug 24_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is putting $5 million into a grant program funding independent researchers to build open-source evaluations of how AI affects user wellbeing, and is publishing its Safeguards team's criteria for what makes such an eval rigorous enough to build on. Applications are due September 21.

**Main methods:**
- **What grantees get, and what they owe.** Direct funding plus model access and technical support; grantees work fully independently and publish their work as open-source projects any developer can use.
- **Why wellbeing resists single-turn evaluation.** Most model behaviors can be judged from one answer, but wellbeing needs far more context — a user in distress may not raise thoughts of self-harm up front, and the need for a more cautious response may only become clear over a long conversation.
- **Context flips whether the same answer is safe.** Anthropic's own example: advice on balanced diets and workout routines is a reasonable reply to a weight-loss question, but potentially actively harmful if that user has shown a history of disordered eating.
- **Five stated criteria for a usable eval.** State clearly what is being measured and what counts as pass or fail; involve clinical and subject-matter experts in design and validation; test both overcompliance and overrefusal; reflect real usage with multi-turn scenarios where risk escalates and context shifts; validate graders against real subject-matter experts.
- **Who they're recruiting.** Explicitly aimed at pulling in clinicians, psychologists, and methodologists alongside ML researchers, on the framing that the industry still lacks clear standards for how models should behave when a user seeks companionship or is navigating a mental health crisis.

**[Anthropic 掏 500 万美元，找人把 wellbeing 这件事测明白](https://www.anthropic.com/news/wellbeing-research-grants)** — _Anthropic · 8月24日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 拿出 500 万美元做资助计划，找独立研究者搭开源的 eval，专门测 AI 到底怎么影响用户的 wellbeing，同时把自家 Safeguards 团队心目中"什么样的 eval 才靠谱到能往上搭东西"的标准也一并公开了。申请 9月21日截止。

**Main methods:**
- **给什么，又要求什么。** 除了直接给钱，还给模型访问权限和技术支持；拿到资助的人完全独立干活，成果要开源，任何开发者都能拿去用。
- **为什么单轮问答测不出 wellbeing。** 大多数模型行为看一条回答就能判断准不准、得不得体，wellbeing 不行，它太吃上下文。状态不好的用户不一定一上来就说自己有 self-harm 的念头，可能聊了很久才显出这里需要更谨慎地接话。
- **同样一句话，换个人就可能变成伤害。** Anthropic 自己举的例子：用户问怎么减重，回一套均衡饮食加健身安排本来挺合理，但如果这个用户之前已经露出过进食障碍的迹象，同样的回答就可能真的造成伤害。
- **他们认可的 eval 长什么样，一共五条。** 说清楚测的到底是什么、什么算 pass 什么算 fail、为什么重要；设计和验证环节要拉上临床和领域专家；overcompliance 和 overrefusal 两头都得测；场景要贴近真实用法，也就是多轮对话、风险一点点升级、上下文中途会变；grader 本身也得拿真专家的判断校准过。
- **想招的是哪批人。** 明确点名 clinician、心理学家、方法学专家，不只是搞 ML 的。理由是整个行业到现在也没形成公认标准，来规定用户找模型当情感陪伴、或者拿它渡过心理危机的时候，模型该怎么应对。
