**[Our position on open-weights models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)** — _Anthropic · Jul 26_

**Main takeaway:** Responding to debate over banning Chinese open-weights models, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei states Anthropic has never advocated for such a ban — arguing that restricting US businesses from using open models doesn't address the real risks, which come from state actors and misuse rather than legitimate companies.

**Main methods:**
- **No ban stance.** Amodei directly denies accusations that Anthropic wants Chinese open-weights models banned to protect its own business, calling protectionist bans an ineffective measure he's held this view on for years.
- **Open weights as public good.** Models without dangerous capabilities cost nothing beyond compute to run and provide real value to businesses, developers, and researchers.
- **Nightmare scenario #1 — authoritarian power.** The primary risk is authoritarian governments (chiefly the CCP) building models more powerful than the US's and using them for military superiority or domestic repression — irrelevant to whether weights are open or used by US firms.
- **Nightmare scenario #2 — misuse and alignment.** Open weights are riskier than closed models because guardrails can't be applied or usage monitored once released and they can't be withdrawn; but banning US business use doesn't stop the actual bad actors, who aren't legitimate US companies to begin with.
- **Preferred policy levers.** Anthropic instead backs restricting sales of powerful chips and chipmaking equipment to China and cracking down on smuggling and workarounds, since compute scaling limits mean China can't out-build the US without US chips.

**[关于开放权重模型，Anthropic 的立场是什么](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models)** — _Anthropic · 7月26日_

**Main takeaway:** 针对最近围绕"该不该禁用中国开放权重模型"的争论，Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei 站出来说清楚：Anthropic 从来没有主张过封杀开放权重模型——他认为真正的风险来自国家级行为体和恶意滥用，而不是那些正经做生意的美国公司，禁掉后者根本挡不住前者。

**Main methods:**
- **不支持封杀。** Amodei 直接回应了"Anthropic 想禁开放权重模型来保护自己生意"的指控，说这完全不对，protectionist 式的禁令他多年来一直不认可。
- **开放权重是公共品。** 只要模型本身不具备危险能力，开放权重除了跑起来要花点算力，几乎零成本，对企业、开发者和研究者都是实打实的价值。
- **噩梦场景一：集权政权坐大。** 他最担心的是威权政府（CCP 是最明显的一个）造出比美国更强的模型，拿去谋求军事上的绝对优势或者镇压本国民众——这跟权重开不开放、美国公司用不用没关系。
- **噩梦场景二：滥用和对齐问题。** 开放权重模型确实比闭源模型风险更高，因为一旦放出去就没法加 guardrail、没法监控使用，也收不回来了；但禁止美国公司用它，管不到真正的坏人，因为坏人本来就不是正经的美国企业。
- **他真正支持的政策。** Anthropic 主张的是别把高端芯片和制造设备卖给中国，并且严打走私和绕道操作——因为算力的 scaling law 摆在那，没有美国芯片，中国造不出比美国更强的模型。
