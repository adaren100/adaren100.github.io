**[A research agenda for the Economic Futures Research Fund](https://www.anthropic.com/news/economic-futures-research-fund-agenda)** — _Anthropic · Jul 21_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is committing $200 million to the Economic Futures Research Fund to back ambitious external research — large RCTs and creative pilots — on interventions that could prepare society for AI's economic impacts, prioritizing five research areas.

**Main methods:**
- **Five priority research areas.** Worker impact at the firm/workplace level, equipping people to navigate AI-driven transitions, modernizing income support for AI-driven displacement, building worker stakes in AI-driven growth, and generating new evidence on public investments.
- **Shift from many small grants to fewer large bets.** This evolves the year-old Economic Futures program away from managing many small grants — which proved hard to scale — toward large-scale RCTs and ambitious pilots, while still supporting partners who can scale up effective small pilots.
- **Grounded in the Economic Policy Framework.** Builds on Anthropic's June EPF, which proposed programs and policies for a range of AI-diffusion scenarios; the fund exists to generate empirical evidence on which of those interventions actually work.
- **Bias toward speed and openness.** Anthropic says it wants partners willing to share findings publicly at key milestones, arguing an early signal can be worth more than a late definitive answer given how fast AI could transform the economy.
- **Explicitly untested territory.** The post frames this as potentially unprecedented — the most promising interventions may be ones nobody has tried yet — and invites proposals beyond the listed directions.
- **Global scope.** The fund is open to research proposals globally, not restricted to any one country.

**[Economic Futures Research Fund 的研究议程](https://www.anthropic.com/news/economic-futures-research-fund-agenda)** — _Anthropic · 7月21日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 拿出 2 亿美元投入 Economic Futures Research Fund，专门资助外部的大型 RCT 和有创意的 pilot 项目，研究怎么应对 AI 带来的经济冲击，重点覆盖五个研究方向。

**Main methods:**
- **五个优先研究方向。** 企业和工作场所层面 AI 对劳动者的影响、帮人适应 AI 带来的转型、给 AI 导致的失业更新收入保障机制、让劳动者在 AI 增长中提前有股份、以及为公共投资积累新证据。
- **从"广撒小额资助"转向"少而大"。** 这是对运营了一年的 Economic Futures 项目的一次调整——之前发现同时管理很多小额资助很难规模化——现在转向大型 RCT 和有野心的 pilot，同时也愿意支持那些能把有效小 pilot 规模化的合作方。
- **承接 Economic Policy Framework。** 是 6 月发布的 EPF 的延伸，EPF 针对不同的 AI 扩散场景提出了一系列项目和政策建议，这个基金就是要为这些干预措施到底有没有用积累实证证据。
- **偏好速度和公开。** Anthropic 说他们想找愿意在关键节点公开分享研究进展的合作方，理由是在 AI 可能快速重塑经济的情况下，一个来得及行动的早期信号，可能比一个姗姗来迟的确定答案更值钱。
- **明确是在探索无人区。** 文章直言这可能是个没有历史先例的时刻——最有希望的方案说不定还没人试过——并欢迎超出列出方向之外的提案。
- **面向全球。** 这个基金不限国别，接受全球范围内的研究提案。
