**[Partnering with Accenture on embedded evaluation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation)** — _Anthropic · Sep 17_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is bringing Accenture in as an independent *embedded* evaluator — evaluators who work inside the company with access comparable to an employee's — making good on the commitment in the CEO's essay "We Must Pace the Frontier." Both Anthropic and Accenture expect to invest at least $1 billion each over the next five years to build capacity in this area.

**Main methods:**
- **Scope of the work.** Led by Faculty, Accenture's specialist AI business, the partnership covers evaluating and red-teaming models, alignment assessments, and testing model safeguards.
- **What "embedded" means.** Unlike today's external evaluators, embedded evaluators get employee-level access: they can watch models take shape during training, follow the decisions governing how models are built and deployed, and talk directly to staff.
- **What that access is for.** From that vantage point they can assess how the company operates, verify safety commitments are being kept, spot blind spots, report incidents, and give the public a better-informed account of benefits and risks.
- **Accountability framing.** Anthropic is explicit that embedded evaluators don't reduce its accountability but make it more verifiable; model safety remains Anthropic's own responsibility.
- **The funding problem is unsolved.** There are no standards yet for what access evaluators should have or how they report findings, and no settled funding system. Anthropic will fund Accenture's work directly for now, while arguing long-term funding should come from pooled or government sources (as proposed in its June Advanced AI Framework).
- **Non-exclusive, and more to come.** Anthropic is in talks with METR and other nonprofit evaluators to pilot embedded evaluation on their own funding, will announce additional evaluators in the coming weeks, and Accenture will work with other AI developers too.

**[Anthropic 把 Accenture 请进门做"驻场" evaluation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation)** — _Anthropic · 9月17日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 拉上 Accenture 做独立的 embedded evaluation，也就是让外部评估方直接进到公司内部，拿到跟员工差不多的权限，算是兑现 CEO 那篇 "We Must Pace the Frontier" 里许下的承诺。两边各自打算在未来五年至少投入 10 亿美元来把这块能力搭起来。

**Main methods:**
- **具体做什么。** 由 Accenture 旗下专做 AI 的 Faculty 牵头，内容包括给模型做 eval 和 red-teaming、做 alignment assessment，以及测试模型的各种 safeguard。
- **"embedded" 到底指什么。** 跟现在常见的外部评估方不一样，embedded evaluator 拿到的是员工级别的权限：能看着模型在训练中一点点成形，能跟到模型怎么建、怎么部署的决策链上，还能直接找员工聊。
- **拿这个权限干嘛。** 这个视角下他们能判断公司实际是怎么运转的、核实 Anthropic 有没有真的守住自己的 safety commitment、找出盲区，也能上报事故，让外界对收益和风险有个更靠谱的了解。
- **责任还是自己的。** Anthropic 特意说明，引入独立的 embedded evaluator 不是把责任转出去，而是让自己的责任更可被验证，模型安全仍然由 Anthropic 自己负责。
- **钱这块还没解。** 现在既没有标准规定评估方该拿到哪些信息、该怎么披露发现，也没有成型的出资机制。眼下 Anthropic 先直接掏钱养 Accenture 这摊活，但他们主张长期应该走 6 月 Advanced AI Framework 里提的集中出资或者政府出资。
- **不是独家，后面还有。** Anthropic 正在跟 METR 等非营利评估机构谈，想用对方自己的经费先跑通 embedded evaluation 的一部分环节，接下来几周还会公布更多评估方，Accenture 那边也会给别家 AI 公司做同样的事。
