**[Anthropic is donating another $20 million to Public First Action](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donation-public-first-action)** — _Anthropic · Jul 20_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is giving another $20 million to nonpartisan group Public First Action, bringing total support to $40 million, to fund public education and policy work pushing for AI safeguards — restricted from being used to influence any candidate election.

**Main methods:**
- **Nonpartisan framing, explicit restriction.** Both donations (first in February 2026, now this one) are earmarked solely for public education and policy work, and cannot be used to influence any federal, state, or local candidate election.
- **Cited risk case for urgency.** Points to Claude Mythos Preview discovering thousands of high-severity vulnerabilities across every major OS and browser as the reason capable models need safeguards before wider release — Anthropic released it only to trusted defenders via Project Glasswing.
- **Policy ask beyond transparency.** Argues transparency laws alone are now insufficient; wants policymakers to adopt measures from Anthropic's Advanced AI Framework, including third-party verification of safety claims, civil penalties for unsafe practices, and government authority to slow or block deployment of models posing catastrophic risk.
- **National security framing.** Ties the ask to national security stakes laid out in Anthropic's "2028: Two Scenarios for Global AI Leadership," and supports tightening chip/semiconductor export controls and curbing illicit model access and distillation attacks.
- **Balances risk and benefit language.** Frames safeguards as a precondition for realizing AI's upside — drug discovery, disease treatment, economic growth — rather than as opposed to it.

**[Anthropic 再给 Public First Action 捐 2000 万美元](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donation-public-first-action)** — _Anthropic · 7月20日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 再向无党派组织 Public First Action 捐 2000 万美元，累计捐款到 4000 万美元，钱专门用来做 AI 安全政策方面的公众教育和倡导，明确不能用来影响任何候选人的选举。

**Main methods:**
- **无党派立场，用途卡得很死。** 两笔捐款（第一笔在 2026 年 2 月，这是第二笔）都明确只能用于公众教育和政策倡导，不能拿去影响任何联邦、州或地方层面的候选人选举。
- **拿具体风险案例说事。** 提到 Claude Mythos Preview 曾在所有主流操作系统和浏览器里发现上千个高危漏洞，这也是为什么高能力模型在更大范围放开之前需要安全措施——Anthropic 当时只通过 Project Glasswing 把它给了受信任的防御方。
- **诉求不止于透明度。** 文章认为光靠透明度立法已经不够了，希望政策制定者采纳 Anthropic《Advanced AI Framework》里的措施，包括第三方核实安全声明、对不安全行为施加民事处罚、以及政府有权对存在灾难性风险的模型部署喊停或延后。
- **和国家安全挂钩。** 把这个诉求和 Anthropic《2028：全球 AI 领导权的两种情景》里讲的国家安全风险联系起来，同时支持收紧芯片和半导体制造设备的出口管制，遏制非法模型获取和蒸馏攻击。
- **风险和收益两头都说。** 文章的基调是：安全措施是释放 AI 好处（药物研发、疾病治疗、经济增长）的前提，而不是跟这些好处对立。
