**[Cognizant and Anthropic expand their partnership to bring Claude to enterprise clients](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cognizant-anthropic)** — _Anthropic · Jul 26_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic is expanding its partnership with Cognizant, which is embedding Claude across its own engineering and delivery platforms and becoming a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network, with more than 30,000 Cognizant associates already trained on Claude.

**Main methods:**
- **Claude embedded in Cognizant's own platforms.** Flowsource, Cognizant's full-stack engineering platform, now runs Claude Code inside its Spec-Driven Development module, directing Claude Code with project specs, coding standards, and architecture blueprints before evaluating the output.
- **Scale of internal adoption.** More than 30,000 Cognizant associates have completed Claude training as part of a new "Frontier Certified" workforce model.
- **Client deployment: contract intelligence.** An agentic contract-intelligence system built for a biopharma client cut contract review time by up to 40% while lifting extraction accuracy above 88%.
- **Client deployment: risk navigation.** A risk-navigation tool lets underwriters evaluate accounts in minutes instead of hours, saving roughly 8 hours per person per week.
- **Client deployment: customer experience.** A customer experience portal was built for a global manufacturer within six months of kickoff.
- **Partnership tier.** Cognizant becomes a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network.

**[Cognizant 和 Anthropic 加深合作，把 Claude 铺进企业客户](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cognizant-anthropic)** — _Anthropic · 7月26日_

**Main takeaway:** Anthropic 和 Cognizant 的合作又往深走了一步：Cognizant 正把 Claude 嵌进自家的工程和交付平台，还升级成了 Claude Partner Network 里的 Global Premier Partner，目前已经有 3 万多名 Cognizant 员工完成了 Claude 培训。

**Main methods:**
- **Claude 直接进了 Cognizant 自家平台。** 它的全栈工程平台 Flowsource 现在在 Spec-Driven Development 模块里跑 Claude Code，用项目的规格文档、编码规范和架构蓝图去指导 Claude Code 干活，再对输出做评估。
- **内部落地的规模。** 超过 3 万名 Cognizant 员工完成了 Claude 培训，这是他们新推出的 "Frontier Certified" 员工体系的一部分。
- **客户案例：合同智能。** 给一家生物制药客户做的 agentic 合同智能系统，把合同审查时间压缩了最多 40%，抽取准确率做到了 88% 以上。
- **客户案例：风险导航。** 一个风险导航工具让核保人员几分钟就能评估账户，原来要花几小时，每人每周能省下大约 8 小时。
- **客户案例：客户体验门户。** 给一家全球制造企业做的客户体验门户，从启动到上线只用了六个月。
- **合作等级。** Cognizant 升级成了 Claude Partner Network 里的 Global Premier Partner。
